Physical Picture

A crossbar maps conductance cells to row–column intersections, but unselected paths carry parasitic current unless nonlinearity or access devices isolate them. The useful state variables and conserved quantities must be identified before selecting a transport model. In a real device the intrinsic material response is coupled self-consistently to electrostatics, contacts, disorder and the external circuit.

Deep Derivation and Physical Interpretation

Ideal vector–matrix multiplication

For an ideal crossbar held at virtual ground, column current is

\[I_j=\sum_i G_{ij}V_i.\]

This is an analogue dot product performed by Ohm’s and Kirchhoff’s laws. Real word/bit lines have distributed resistance, so internal node voltages must be solved from a network; the error grows with array dimension and total conductance.

Sneak paths and selectors

During memory read, unselected cells form alternative current paths. A selector with nonlinear \(I(V)\) suppresses half-selected current. Its relevant metrics are selectivity \(I(V_{read})/I(V_{read}/2)\), current density, leakage, endurance and compatibility with bipolar/unipolar writes. 1T1R offers control at area cost; 1S1R trades transistor area for nonlinear-device variability.

Scaling test

Array size cannot be inferred from a single-cell on/off ratio. Solve worst-case patterns including line resistance, selector variation and sense threshold; then verify with tiled arrays. For analogue computing, conductance-dependent voltage error makes the matrix error data dependent, so random-matrix tests are insufficient without realistic trained weights.

Minimal Model

\[I_j=Σ_i G_ij V_i, modified by wire resistance and sneak paths\]

This expression is a compact organizing relation, not a universal fitting law. Its assumptions, dimensionality and boundary conditions must be checked before quantitative use.

Device Consequences

1T1R, 1S1R and selector-free schemes trade density, write margin and compatibility.

The correct comparison therefore follows the causal chain

\[\text{material and geometry}\rightarrow\text{states and barriers}\rightarrow\text{transport or switching}\rightarrow\text{terminal response}.\]

Limits and Diagnostic Tests

Wire resistance, IR drop, selector leakage, half-select disturb and device variation grow with array size. A credible experiment should report geometry, temperature, bias protocol, contact configuration and device-to-device statistics whenever they can change the interpretation.

深入推导与物理解释

理想向量—矩阵乘法

理想交叉阵列在虚地读出时列电流为

\[I_j=\sum_i G_{ij}V_i.\]

这是由欧姆定律和 Kirchhoff 定律直接完成的模拟点积。真实字线/位线具有分布电阻，必须求解内部节点电压；误差随阵列尺寸和总电导增大。

潜通路与选通器

存储读出时，未选单元构成替代电流路径。具有非线性 \(I(V)\) 的选通器可压制半选电流，关键指标包括选择比 \(I(V_{read})/I(V_{read}/2)\)、电流密度、泄漏、耐久及对双极/单极写入的兼容性。1T1R 以面积换控制，1S1R 则以非线性器件变异换取晶体管面积。

缩放检验

不能由单单元开关比推断阵列尺寸。应求解包含线阻、选通变异和感测阈值的最坏数据图样，再用分块阵列验证。模拟计算中，电导依赖电压误差使矩阵误差与数据相关，因此只测随机矩阵而不使用真实训练权重并不充分。

最小模型

\[I_j=Σ_i G_ij V_i, modified by wire resistance and sneak paths\]

这个式子用于组织物理关系，而不是在任何条件下都成立的拟合公式。定量使用前必须检查其近似、维度和边界条件。

器件意义

1T1R、1S1R 与无选通方案在密度、写裕量和兼容性之间权衡。

合理的分析顺序是

\[\text{材料与几何}\rightarrow\text{状态与势垒}\rightarrow\text{输运或翻转}\rightarrow\text{端口响应}.\]

限制与判据

线电阻、IR 压降、选通泄漏、半选干扰及器件变异随阵列尺寸增长。 当几何、温度、偏压流程、接触方式或器件间统计会影响结论时，可信实验必须完整报告这些条件。
