Drift, Diffusion, and Current in Semiconductors半导体中的漂移、扩散与电流

A unified account of drift and diffusion through electric fields, concentration gradients, and electrochemical potentials, including their high-field limits.用电场、浓度梯度和电化学势统一理解漂移扩散电流，并说明迁移率、扩散系数和速度饱和的边界。

Core idea: Drift and diffusion are not unrelated current sources. They are two representations of a gradient in electrochemical potential. At equilibrium they cancel exactly, leaving a spatially constant Fermi level.

Drift

At low field,

\[v_n=-\mu_n\mathcal E,\qquad v_p=\mu_p\mathcal E,\]

so conventional drift currents are

\[J_{n,\mathrm{drift}}=q\mu_n n\mathcal E,\qquad J_{p,\mathrm{drift}}=q\mu_p p\mathcal E.\]

Mobility is not a universal material constant. It depends on temperature, doping, transverse field, orientation, and scattering from phonons, ionized impurities, interfaces, and roughness. Matthiessen’s rule is only an independent-scattering approximation.

Diffusion

Concentration gradients drive particle diffusion. Including charge signs,

\[J_{n,\mathrm{diff}}=qD_n\nabla n,\qquad J_{p,\mathrm{diff}}=-qD_p\nabla p.\]

For a nondegenerate system near thermal equilibrium,

\[\frac{D_n}{\mu_n}=\frac{D_p}{\mu_p}=\frac{kT}{q}.\]

The Einstein relation shows that diffusion is not an extra empirical current; it is linked to dissipative transport by thermal statistics.

Total Current and Quasi-Fermi Levels

In one dimension,

\[J_n=q\mu_n n\mathcal E+qD_n\frac{dn}{dx},\qquad J_p=q\mu_p p\mathcal E-qD_p\frac{dp}{dx}.\]

For nondegenerate carriers this becomes

\[J_n=\mu_n n\frac{dE_{Fn}}{dx},\qquad J_p=\mu_p p\frac{dE_{Fp}}{dx}.\]

Thus quasi-Fermi-level gradients are the true current drivers. A PN junction may contain a strong built-in electric field at equilibrium, yet carry no net current because drift and diffusion cancel.

High Field and Short Length Scales

Once \(\mu\mathcal E\) approaches the saturation velocity, linear drift fails. A common interpolation is

\[v(\mathcal E)\approx\frac{\mu_0\mathcal E}{[1+(\mu_0\mathcal E/v_{sat})^\beta]^{1/\beta}}.\]

When device length approaches the mean free path, local drift–diffusion also fails. Ballistic or quasiballistic Landauer transport becomes appropriate; strong disorder may instead produce hopping. See From Ballistic Transport to Hopping in FETs.

Physical Checks

  • Electron velocity and conventional electron current point in opposite directions.
  • Zero electric field does not imply zero current; diffusion can remain.
  • Equilibrium means zero net current, not that microscopic carrier motion stops.
  • \(\sigma=q(n\mu_n+p\mu_p)\) is a local, low-field, homogeneous-response formula.

From the Boltzmann equation to drift–diffusion

Taking the first velocity moment of the relaxation-time Boltzmann equation gives low-field mobility \(\mu=q\langle\tau/m^*\rangle\). Local nondegenerate equilibrium plus electrochemical-potential gradients yields

\[\mathbf J_n=qn\mu_n\mathbf E+qD_n\nabla n,qquad D_n/\mu_n=k_BT/q.\]

The Einstein relation changes under degeneracy because \(dn/d\mu\) replaces \(n/k_BT\). At high field, the distribution is no longer a displaced equilibrium; optical-phonon emission limits velocity, often represented by

\[v(F)\approx\frac{\mu_0F}{[1+(\mu_0F/v_{sat})^\beta]^{1/\beta}}.\]

Mobility is therefore a regime-dependent response coefficient, not an immutable material number. Contact resistance, carrier heating and nonlocal transport must be separated before assigning a measured conductance change to channel scattering.

核心结论：漂移和扩散不是两个互不相关的“电流来源”，而是同一电化学势梯度的两种表达。热平衡时二者精确抵消，准费米能级在空间上为常数。

1. 漂移

弱场下平均漂移速度与电场成正比：

\[v_{n}= -\mu_n\mathcal E,\qquad v_p=\mu_p\mathcal E.\]

按常规电流方向定义，电子和空穴的漂移电流密度均沿电场方向：

\[J_{n,\mathrm{drift}}=q\mu_n n\mathcal E,\qquad J_{p,\mathrm{drift}}=q\mu_p p\mathcal E.\]

迁移率不是材料常数：它依赖温度、掺杂、垂直场和晶向，并由晶格、离化杂质、界面粗糙等散射机制共同决定。Matthiessen 规则 \(1/\mu\approx\sum_i1/\mu_i\) 只是独立散射近似。

2. 扩散

浓度梯度产生粒子扩散。计入电荷符号后：

\[J_{n,\mathrm{diff}}=qD_n\nabla n,\qquad J_{p,\mathrm{diff}}=-qD_p\nabla p.\]

非简并、近热平衡条件下，漂移与扩散由爱因斯坦关系连接：

\[\frac{D_n}{\mu_n}=\frac{D_p}{\mu_p}=\frac{kT}{q}.\]

这意味着扩散并非额外添加的经验过程，而是热运动与耗散满足涨落—耗散关系的结果。

3. 总电流与准费米势

一维漂移—扩散电流为

\[J_n=q\mu_n n\mathcal E+qD_n\frac{dn}{dx},\qquad J_p=q\mu_p p\mathcal E-qD_p\frac{dp}{dx}.\]

在非简并条件下可改写成

\[J_n=\mu_n n\frac{dE_{Fn}}{dx},\qquad J_p=\mu_p p\frac{dE_{Fp}}{dx}.\]

因此真正驱动电流的是准费米能级梯度。平衡时 \(E_{Fn}=E_{Fp}=E_F\) 且空间恒定，所以即使 PN 结内存在很强的内建电场，也没有净电流。

4. 高场、短尺度与输运区制

当 \(\mu\mathcal E\) 接近饱和速度时，线性关系失效，常用经验式

\[v(\mathcal E)\approx\frac{\mu_0\mathcal E}{[1+(\mu_0\mathcal E/v_{sat})^\beta]^{1/\beta}}.\]

当器件长度接近平均自由程时，局域漂移—扩散模型也失效，需要弹道或准弹道输运与 Landauer 图像。无序、局域态主导时还可能转入跳跃输运。区分这些模型的关键尺度是：器件长度 \(L\)、平均自由程 \(\ell\)、相干长度及非弹性散射长度。

5. 物理检查

  • 电子速度与电子电流方向相反；不要把粒子流和常规电流混淆。
  • 电场不一定是唯一驱动力；零电场下仍可有扩散电流。
  • 平衡 PN 结“没有电流”是漂移与扩散抵消，而不是载流子停止运动。
  • 电导率 \(\sigma=q(n\mu_n+p\mu_p)\) 只给局域、低场、均匀条件下的欧姆响应。

从 Boltzmann 方程到漂移—扩散

对弛豫时间近似 Boltzmann 方程取第一速度矩，可得低场迁移率 \(\mu=q\langle\tau/m^*\rangle\)。局域非简并平衡及电化学势梯度给出

\[\mathbf J_n=qn\mu_n\mathbf E+qD_n\nabla n,qquad D_n/\mu_n=k_BT/q.\]

简并体系中 Einstein 关系需要用 \(dn/d\mu\) 替代 \(n/k_BT\)。高场下分布不再是平移平衡分布，光学声子发射限制速度，常写成

\[v(F)\approx\frac{\mu_0F}{[1+(\mu_0F/v_{sat})^\beta]^{1/\beta}}.\]

因此迁移率是特定输运区间的响应系数，不是不可改变的材料常数。只有先分离接触电阻、载流子加热与非局域输运，才能把电导变化归因于沟道散射。
