Physical Picture

Atomically thin bodies improve gate control, while type-II or broken-gap vdW heterojunctions reduce tunneling distance and permit independent band alignment by dual gates. The useful state variables and conserved quantities must be identified before selecting a transport model. In a real device the intrinsic material response is coupled self-consistently to electrostatics, contacts, disorder and the external circuit.

Deep Derivation and Physical Interpretation

Lateral versus vertical geometry

In a lateral 2D TFET, tunnelling occurs across an in-plane junction and the gate must create a sharp depletion field. In a vertical/interlayer TFET, atomically thin layers separately host source and drain bands while the van der Waals gap is the tunnelling barrier. Current is then governed by spectral overlap

\[I\propto\sum_{\mathbf k}|t_{\mathbf k}|^2\int A_S(\mathbf k,E)A_D(\mathbf k,E+qV)[f_S-f_D]dE.\]

This geometry can decouple electrostatic thickness from transport length, but introduces momentum alignment, twist and layer-resolved quantum capacitance.

Electrostatic design condition

The two layer potentials obey a capacitance matrix rather than one gate divider. Interlayer charge transfer shifts both bands and may screen the gate. A useful design must maintain a small off-state overlap, then open a broad on-state energy–momentum window without excessive drain-induced tunnelling.

Interpretation hazards

Gate-dependent Schottky contacts, trap-assisted vertical leakage and graphene density-of-states effects can imitate steep turn-on. Four-terminal/contact controls, dual-gate maps, area scaling and twist dependence are needed. Report current per physical width or area consistently; mixing lateral and vertical normalizations can reverse apparent rankings.

Minimal Model

\[T_BTBT grows as barrier width and tunneling mass decrease\]

This expression is a compact organizing relation, not a universal fitting law. Its assumptions, dimensionality and boundary conditions must be checked before quantitative use.

Device Consequences

Material and device co-design can improve TFET current and suppress short-channel effects.

The correct comparison therefore follows the causal chain

\[\text{material and geometry}\rightarrow\text{states and barriers}\rightarrow\text{transport or switching}\rightarrow\text{terminal response}.\]

Limits and Diagnostic Tests

Interface contamination, twist, band tails and uncertain band offsets often make experiments worse than ideal simulations. A credible experiment should report geometry, temperature, bias protocol, contact configuration and device-to-device statistics whenever they can change the interpretation.

深入推导与物理解释

横向与垂直几何

横向二维 TFET 在面内结区隧穿，栅极需要形成尖锐耗尽电场。垂直/层间 TFET 由原子薄层分别承载源漏能带，范德华间隙充当隧穿势垒，其电流由谱重叠控制：

\[I\propto\sum_{\mathbf k}|t_{\mathbf k}|^2\int A_S(\mathbf k,E)A_D(\mathbf k,E+qV)[f_S-f_D]dE.\]

这种结构把静电厚度与输运长度部分解耦，却引入动量对准、扭角和分层量子电容。

静电设计条件

两层电势服从电容矩阵而不是单一栅分压。层间电荷转移会同时移动两侧能带并屏蔽栅场。有效设计必须在关态保持小谱重叠，在开态打开足够宽的能量—动量窗口，同时抑制漏端诱导隧穿。

解释陷阱

栅压依赖 Schottky 接触、陷阱辅助垂直泄漏与石墨烯态密度效应都可能伪装成陡峭开启。需要四端/接触对照、双栅二维图、面积标度及扭角依赖。横向器件按宽度归一、垂直器件按面积归一，若混用会彻底改变排名。

最小模型

\[T_BTBT grows as barrier width and tunneling mass decrease\]

这个式子用于组织物理关系，而不是在任何条件下都成立的拟合公式。定量使用前必须检查其近似、维度和边界条件。

器件意义

材料–器件协同设计可以提高 TFET 电流并抑制短沟道效应。

合理的分析顺序是

\[\text{材料与几何}\rightarrow\text{状态与势垒}\rightarrow\text{输运或翻转}\rightarrow\text{端口响应}.\]

限制与判据

界面污染、扭角、带尾及不确定带阶常使实验性能低于理想模拟。 当几何、温度、偏压流程、接触方式或器件间统计会影响结论时，可信实验必须完整报告这些条件。
