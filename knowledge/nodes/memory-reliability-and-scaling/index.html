Physical Picture

Retention, endurance, read/write disturb, stochastic variation and thermal stability are coupled consequences of the energy landscape and write stress. The useful state variables and conserved quantities must be identified before selecting a transport model. In a real device the intrinsic material response is coupled self-consistently to electrostatics, contacts, disorder and the external circuit.

Minimal Model

\[τ_ret≈τ0 exp(ΔE/kT)\]

This expression is a compact organizing relation, not a universal fitting law. Its assumptions, dimensionality and boundary conditions must be checked before quantitative use.

Limits and Diagnostic Tests

Accelerated tests require a validated physical acceleration law; simple time or temperature extrapolation can be misleading. A credible experiment should report geometry, temperature, bias protocol, contact configuration and device-to-device statistics whenever they can change the interpretation.
