The Bipolar Junction Transistor: Minority-Carrier Injection and Gain双极晶体管：少子注入与电流放大

The BJT as two asymmetric coupled PN junctions, combining emitter injection, thin-base transport, and collector-field collection.把BJT理解为两个非对称、相互耦合的PN结：发射结注入、薄基区输运、集电结收集共同产生电流增益。

Read in阅读语言

Core idea: BJT gain is not mysterious control by base current. The base–emitter voltage sets minority-carrier injection exponentially; a thin base lets most injected carriers reach the reverse-biased collector junction and be swept into the collector.

Figure 1 | Emitter, base, and collector in an NPN BJT. Shaddack / Predatorix, public domain; source. Real devices are strongly asymmetric in doping and geometry.

Why the Three Regions Are Asymmetric

The emitter is heavily doped for injection efficiency; the base is thin and lighter doped to suppress recombination; the collector is lighter doped and physically larger to withstand reverse bias and dissipate heat. Two separate back-to-back diodes do not make a useful BJT because they lack a shared thin base.

Forward-Active Operation

The base–emitter junction is forward biased and the base–collector junction reverse biased. Ideally,

\[I_C\approx I_Se^{V_{BE}/V_T}.\]

The common-base gain is

\[\alpha_F=\gamma\alpha_T,\]

with emitter efficiency \(\gamma\) and base transport factor \(\alpha_T\). The common-emitter gain is

\[\beta_F=\frac{\alpha_F}{1-\alpha_F}.\]

A value of \(\alpha_F\) only slightly below unity can therefore produce large but process-sensitive \(\beta\).

Four Operating Regions

BE junction BC junction Region Physical picture
reverse reverse cutoff leakage only
forward reverse forward active injection, diffusion, collection
forward forward saturation both junctions inject; large stored charge
reverse forward reverse active emitter/collector roles exchanged poorly

Ebers–Moll unifies all four regions. Gummel–Poon adds recombination, high injection, and base-width modulation.

Frequency and Nonideal Effects

Base transit time, emitter diffusion capacitance, and collector capacitance determine \(f_T\). Increasing collector bias narrows the effective base—the Early effect—and gives finite output conductance. At high current, conductivity modulation, current crowding, and the Kirk effect reduce gain.

Link to Power Devices

An IGBT uses a MOS gate to control bipolar injection. Conductivity modulation lowers drift-region resistance but creates turn-off tail current; see Power Semiconductor Devices.

核心结论：BJT 的放大不是“基极电流神秘控制集电极电流”，而是基极–发射极电压指数地设定发射结少子注入；薄基区让大多数注入载流子到达反偏集电结并被电场收集。

图 1｜NPN BJT 的发射极、基极和集电极结构。原图：Shaddack / Predatorix，公有领域；文件页。实际器件的掺杂和几何高度不对称。

1. 为什么三个区必须不对称

  • 发射极重掺杂，提高电子注入效率；
  • 基区很薄且较轻掺杂，减小基区复合；
  • 集电区较轻掺杂且面积较大，以承受反偏并散热。

若把两个普通二极管简单背靠背连接，并不会得到性能良好的 BJT，因为缺少共享的薄基区与少子连续输运。

2. 正向有源区

NPN 管的基极–发射结正偏、基极–集电结反偏。理想情况下

\[I_C\approx I_Se^{V_{BE}/V_T},\qquad V_T=\frac{kT}{q}.\]

共基电流增益

\[\alpha_F=\gamma\alpha_T,\]

由发射效率 \(\gamma\) 和基区输运因子 \(\alpha_T\) 决定；共射增益

\[\beta_F=\frac{\alpha_F}{1-\alpha_F}.\]

因此 \(\alpha_F\) 只比 1 小一点，便可产生很大的 \(\beta\)；同时也解释了为何增益对复合、基宽和工艺非常敏感。

3. 四种工作区

BE结 BC结 工作区 物理图像
反偏 反偏 截止 仅有漏电
正偏 反偏 正向有源 注入—扩散—收集，适合放大
正偏 正偏 饱和 两结同时注入，存储电荷大
反偏 正偏 反向有源 发射/集电角色互换但性能差

Ebers–Moll 模型用两个耦合二极管统一描述四区；Gummel–Poon 模型进一步加入高注入、基区宽度调制和复合。

4. 频率与非理想效应

基区渡越时间、发射结扩散电容和集电结电容决定截止频率 \(f_T\)。集电结反偏增加会使有效基宽变窄，即 Early 效应，表现为输出电流随 \(V_{CE}\) 上升。高电流下基区电导调制、发射极拥挤和 Kirk 效应使增益下降。

5. 与功率器件的联系

IGBT 用 MOS 栅控制双极载流子注入，结合 MOSFET 高输入阻抗与 BJT 漂移区电导调制的低导通压降，但会引入关断尾电流。详见功率半导体器件。
