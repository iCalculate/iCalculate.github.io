Read in阅读语言

Thesis: Semiconductor devices are not an unrelated catalog of structures. They all implement the same mapping: materials and geometry establish the band structure; terminal voltages reshape the electrostatic potential; the potential redistributes charge; carrier transport produces terminal current; and nonideal processes set the ultimate function and performance.

A Reconstructive Chain

\[\boxed{ \text{materials/geometry} \rightarrow \text{bands and barriers} \rightarrow \text{charge distribution} \rightarrow \text{transport and recombination} \rightarrow \text{terminal }I,Q,C \rightarrow \text{device function} }\]

The goal is not to memorize one formula set per device, but to answer six questions:

  1. What are the active degrees of freedom? Electrons, holes, ionized dopants, interface states, or photons?
  2. Where is the barrier? At a PN junction, metal contact, oxide, or heterojunction band offset?
  3. Which terminal controls the barrier or charge? Junction bias, gate voltage, base drive, or illumination?
  4. How do carriers move? Drift, diffusion, thermionic emission, tunneling, or ballistic injection?
  5. Which approximation is being used? Nondegenerate statistics, low injection, depletion, gradual channel, or quasistatic response?
  6. What breaks the ideal model? Recombination, series resistance, breakdown, velocity saturation, short-channel electrostatics, or self-heating?

Five Layers of the Network

Materials and Statistics

Energy Bands, Density of States, and Carrier Statistics determines which states exist and how they are occupied. Doping moves the Fermi level through charge neutrality and thereby establishes the equilibrium electron and hole reservoirs.

Dynamics and Field Equations

Drift, Diffusion, and Current in Semiconductors connects fields and concentration gradients to current. Nonequilibrium Carriers describes generation, recombination, lifetime, and quasi-Fermi levels. The Governing Equations of Semiconductor Devices closes Poisson, carrier statistics, drift–diffusion, and continuity into one self-consistent problem.

Device Building Blocks

  • The PN Junction creates a barrier through spatially patterned doping.
  • Metal–Semiconductor Contacts use work-function alignment and interface physics to control majority carriers.
  • The MOS Capacitor controls semiconductor surface charge through an insulating gate.
  • Heterojunctions use material band offsets for carrier selection, confinement, and high-mobility channels.

Three-Terminal Devices

  • The Bipolar Junction Transistor couples two PN junctions through minority-carrier injection and transport.
  • The MOSFET extends MOS electrostatics laterally so that gate-controlled inversion charge carries source–drain current.
  • Heterojunctions, JFETs, MESFETs, and HEMTs compare depletion-controlled channels with heterojunction-generated two-dimensional carrier gases.

Limits and Functions

Breakdown, Scaling, and Nonideal Effects cuts across every device. Power Devices, Optoelectronic Devices, and Nonvolatile Memories turn the same physics into energy switching, light conversion, and information retention.

Emerging Quantum and Functional Branches

The extended atlas grows from this classical trunk through five causal gateways:

  • Two-Dimensional Quantum Foundations rebuilds electrostatics, transport and optical response for atomically thin matter.
  • Emerging Steep-Slope Transistors compares TFET, NCFET and cold-source injection without treating a minimum subthreshold slope as a complete device metric.
  • Advanced Nonvolatile Memory Physics organizes flash, FeFET, RRAM, PCM and MRAM by their stored state variable and switching barrier.
  • Functional and Neuromorphic Devices connects internal device dynamics to crossbars, compute-in-memory and sensing–memory integration.
  • Two-Dimensional Quantum Devices collects electron-optical, spin–valley, excitonic, superconducting and quantum-information prototypes.

How to Read the Figures

  • The vertical axis of a band diagram is usually electron energy. Because electron potential energy is \(-q\psi\), a rising electrostatic potential lowers the electron bands.
  • The equilibrium Fermi level is spatially constant. Under nonequilibrium conditions, electrons and holes require separate \(E_{Fn}\) and \(E_{Fp}\).
  • A cross-section shows geometry and doping; a band diagram shows barriers; an I–V or C–V graph shows terminal response. They are complementary, not interchangeable.

Governing closure: from state to terminal response

Every device chapter closes the same mathematical loop. Electrostatics converts charge into potential,

\[\nabla\cdot(\epsilon\nabla\phi)=-q(p-n+N_D^+-N_A^-+\rho_t/q),\]

statistics converts potential into occupation, and transport converts occupation gradients into current. Continuity then updates charge:

\[\frac{\partial n}{\partial t}=\frac{1}{q}\nabla\cdot\mathbf J_n+G-R,\qquad \frac{\partial p}{\partial t}=-\frac{1}{q}\nabla\cdot\mathbf J_p+G-R.\]

The terminal current is a boundary flux plus displacement current. A device model is physically closed only after material relations, geometry, contacts, initial state and external circuit are specified. This is why the Atlas uses causal links: changing a material parameter propagates through states, fields, transport and finally the measured port.

总纲：半导体器件不是彼此独立的结构清单。它们都在实现同一条映射：材料与几何结构决定能带，端电压改变静电势，静电势重排载流子，载流子输运形成端口电流，非理想过程最终限制功能与性能。

1. 一条可以重建公式的主线

\[\boxed{ \text{材料/结构} \rightarrow \text{能带与势垒} \rightarrow \text{电荷分布} \rightarrow \text{输运与复合} \rightarrow \text{端口 }I,Q,C \rightarrow \text{器件功能} }\]

学习器件物理的目标不是记住每种器件的一组公式，而是能够回答六个问题：

  1. 自由度是什么？ 电子、空穴、离化杂质、界面态还是光子？
  2. 势垒在哪里？ PN 结、金半接触、氧化层还是异质结带阶？
  3. 哪个端口控制势垒或电荷？ 正向偏压、栅压、基极电流或光照？
  4. 载流子怎样运动？ 漂移、扩散、热发射、隧穿还是弹道输运？
  5. 采用了什么近似？ 非简并、小注入、耗尽近似、缓变沟道或准静态？
  6. 什么效应使理想模型失效？ 复合、串联电阻、击穿、速度饱和、短沟道或自热？

2. 网络的五个层次

A. 材料与统计基础

能带、态密度与载流子统计回答“有多少可用状态、这些状态被占据多少”。掺杂通过电中性条件移动费米能级，由此决定平衡电子和空穴浓度。

B. 动力学与场方程

半导体中的漂移、扩散与电流给出电场和浓度梯度如何产生电流；非平衡载流子：产生、复合与准费米能级给出受光、电注入后的载流子演化；半导体器件的基本方程把泊松方程、漂移—扩散关系和连续性方程闭合成一个自洽问题。

C. 器件的基本构件

  • PN结：空间电荷、能带与整流：同一材料内用掺杂制造势垒；
  • 金属-半导体接触与肖特基势垒：用功函数差和界面控制多数载流子；
  • MOS电容：积累、耗尽与反型：用绝缘栅静电控制半导体表面电荷；
  • 异质结：用材料带阶实现载流子选择、限制或高迁移率沟道。

D. 三端与多端器件

  • 双极晶体管：少子注入与电流放大把两个耦合 PN 结组成受控输运器件；
  • MOSFET：栅控电荷与沟道输运把 MOS 电容沿横向展开，令栅压控制源漏电流；
  • 异质结、JFET、MESFET与HEMT展示“耗尽层控制沟道”和“异质结生成二维电子气”两条路线。

E. 限制与应用

击穿、缩放与器件非理想效应横向连接所有器件；功率半导体器件、光电子器件：LED、光电二极管与太阳电池和非易失存储器的器件物理分别把输运转化为能量开关、光电转换和信息保存。

F. 新兴量子与功能分支

扩展后的 Atlas 从经典主干通过五个因果入口继续生长：

  • 二维量子物理基础针对原子级薄材料重建静电、输运和光学响应；
  • 新兴陡峭亚阈值晶体管统一比较 TFET、NCFET 与冷源注入，避免只用最小亚阈值摆幅评价器件；
  • 先进非易失存储器物理按照存储状态变量与翻转能垒组织 Flash、FeFET、RRAM、PCM 和 MRAM；
  • 功能与神经形态器件把器件内部动力学连接到交叉阵列、存内计算和感存一体化；
  • 二维量子器件汇集电子光学、自旋–谷、激子、超导和量子信息器件原型。

4. 读图约定

  • 能带图纵轴通常是电子能量，电势升高时电子势能 \(-q\psi\) 降低；不要把能带弯曲方向直接当成电场方向。
  • 平衡态费米能级必须处处相等；非平衡时电子与空穴分别由 \(E_{Fn}\)、\(E_{Fp}\) 描述。
  • 器件剖面图表达几何和掺杂，能带图表达势垒，I–V/C–V 图表达端口响应；三者不能互相替代。

控制方程的闭合：从内部状态到端口响应

每一类器件最终都闭合到同一数学回路。静电把电荷转换为电势：

\[\nabla\cdot(\epsilon\nabla\phi)=-q(p-n+N_D^+-N_A^-+\rho_t/q),\]

统计关系把电势转换为占据，输运关系再把占据梯度转换为电流，连续性方程更新电荷：

\[\frac{\partial n}{\partial t}=\frac{1}{q}\nabla\cdot\mathbf J_n+G-R,\qquad \frac{\partial p}{\partial t}=-\frac{1}{q}\nabla\cdot\mathbf J_p+G-R.\]

端口电流是边界粒子通量与位移电流之和。只有给出材料关系、几何、接触、初态和外电路，器件模型才真正闭合。这也是 Atlas 采用因果链接的原因：材料参数变化会依次传播到状态、场、输运，最终才到达测量端口。
