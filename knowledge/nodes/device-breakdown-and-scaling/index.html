Breakdown, Scaling, and Device Nonidealities击穿、缩放与器件非理想效应

Tunneling, avalanche, short-channel electrostatics, high-field transport, parasitics, self-heating, and reliability across PN junctions, BJTs, and MOSFETs.横向连接PN结、BJT和MOSFET中的隧穿、雪崩、短沟道、高场、寄生电阻、自热与可靠性问题。

Core idea: Nonideal effects mark the scale boundaries of ideal models. Shrinking dimensions, raising field, or increasing density progressively breaks low-field response, local equilibrium, depletion approximations, and classical statistics.

Two Microscopic Breakdown Mechanisms

Zener or band-to-band tunneling occurs in heavily doped, narrow depletion regions where strong field makes valence-to-conduction tunneling probable. Avalanche occurs when carriers gain enough energy over a wider depletion region to generate new electron–hole pairs by impact ionization.

MOSFET Short-Channel Effects

As gate length approaches the electrostatic control length, source and drain depletion fields share control with the gate. Threshold roll-off, DIBL, punch-through, subthreshold leakage, and gate-induced drain leakage increase. Thinner oxides, thinner bodies, and multigate geometry reduce the control length; FinFET and GAAFET gains are fundamentally electrostatic.

High-Field Transport and Reliability

Velocity saturation changes square-law behavior. Hot carriers damage interfaces and oxides. Thin dielectrics show direct, defect-assisted, and Fowler–Nordheim tunneling followed by time-dependent breakdown. High current density causes electromigration; heating reduces mobility and raises leakage, producing electrothermal feedback.

Parasitics and Scaling

Contacts, extensions, junction capacitances, gate resistance, interconnect capacitance, and substrate coupling become a larger fraction of the total as intrinsic channels improve. Classical constant-field scaling would shrink voltage with dimensions, but supply voltage has not scaled proportionally, creating power-density and reliability pressure. Device innovation therefore shifts toward multigate electrostatics, backside delivery, new channels, contact engineering, and 3D integration.

Field, geometry and positive feedback

Avalanche becomes self-sustaining when electron and hole ionization coefficients satisfy a multiplication integral of order unity, for example

\[\int_0^W\alpha_n(x)\exp\!\left[-\int_0^x(\alpha_n-\alpha_p)dx'\right]dx\approx1.\]

Short-channel electrostatic integrity is organized by a natural length \(\lambda\); for a thin-body double gate, \(\lambda\) scales roughly as \(\sqrt{(\epsilon_s/\epsilon_{ox})t_st_{ox}}\). Robust control needs \(L_G\) several times \(\lambda\).

Electrical and thermal failure are coupled through \(T=T_0+R_{th}IV\). Mobility reduction may stabilize current, while leakage or impact ionization can create positive feedback and runaway. Breakdown qualification must therefore specify pulse width, thermal boundary, area and failure criterion; a single terminal voltage is not an intrinsic material constant.

核心结论：非理想效应不是独立的附录，而是理想模型的尺度边界。减小长度、增加电场或提高载流子密度，会依次破坏低场、局域平衡、耗尽近似与经典统计。

1. 击穿的两个微观机制

Zener/带间隧穿

重掺杂使耗尽层很窄，强电场使价带电子量子隧穿到导带。隧穿概率对带隙、有效质量和场强呈强指数依赖，因此窄禁带、重掺杂结更易发生。

雪崩

较宽耗尽区内的载流子从电场获得能量，通过碰撞电离产生新的电子–空穴对；倍增若超过损失便出现雪崩。其条件常写为电离系数沿耗尽区积分达到自持值，而不是“电场超过一个普适常数”。

2. MOSFET短沟道效应

长沟道中势垒主要由栅控制；沟道缩短后源漏耗尽区参与控制：

  • 阈值电压随沟道长度下降；
  • 漏致势垒降低（DIBL）；
  • 穿通与亚阈值漏电增加；
  • 栅诱导漏极泄漏（GIDL）和带间隧穿增强。

核心指标不是绝对栅长，而是栅长相对于电静控制长度 \(\lambda\)。减薄栅介质、减薄沟道或采用多栅结构可降低 \(\lambda\)，因此 FinFET/GAAFET 改善的是电静完整性。

3. 高场输运与可靠性

  • 速度饱和降低平方律指数；
  • 热载流子注入氧化层，改变阈值和跨导；
  • 极薄介质发生直接隧穿、缺陷辅助隧穿与时变介质击穿；
  • 高电流密度造成电迁移；
  • 功耗通过晶格温升降低迁移率、增大漏电，形成电热反馈。

4. 寄生元件

真实器件还包含接触电阻、源漏延伸电阻、结电容、栅电阻、互连电容和衬底耦合。当内在沟道性能持续提高时，外部寄生量占总延迟和总电阻的比例反而上升。

5. 缩放的守恒关系

经典恒场缩放希望尺寸与电压同比例缩小，使电场保持不变；实际电源电压无法长期同比例降低，导致功率密度与可靠性压力。由此器件创新从平面缩放转向：多栅、环栅、背面供电、新沟道材料、接触工程与三维集成。相关路线见Introducing 2D-material based devices in the logic scaling roadmap。

电场、几何与正反馈

当电子和空穴碰撞电离系数的倍增积分达到自持条件时出现雪崩，例如

\[\int_0^W\alpha_n(x)\exp\!\left[-\int_0^x(\alpha_n-\alpha_p)dx'\right]dx\approx1.\]

短沟道静电完整性由自然长度 \(\lambda\) 组织；对薄体双栅，\(\lambda\) 近似按 \(\sqrt{(\epsilon_s/\epsilon_{ox})t_st_{ox}}\) 缩放。可靠栅控通常要求 \(L_G\) 为数倍 \(\lambda\)。

电失效与热失效通过 \(T=T_0+R_{th}IV\) 耦合。迁移率下降可能稳定电流，而泄漏或碰撞电离会产生正反馈和热失控。因此击穿表征必须注明脉宽、热边界、面积与失效判据；单一端口电压不是本征材料常数。
