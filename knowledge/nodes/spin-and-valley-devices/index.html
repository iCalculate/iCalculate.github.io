Spin and Valley Devices自旋与谷器件

Physical Picture

Spin and valley devices generate, manipulate and detect internal quantum labels through optical helicity, magnetic proximity, spin–orbit fields or Berry-curvature-driven transverse motion. The useful state variables and conserved quantities must be identified before selecting a transport model. In a real device the intrinsic material response is coupled self-consistently to electrostatics, contacts, disorder and the external circuit.

Deep Derivation and Physical Interpretation

Generate, manipulate, detect

An internal polarization vector \(\mathbf s\) follows a Bloch transport equation

\[\frac{\partial\mathbf s}{\partial t}=D_s\nabla^2\mathbf s -\frac{\mathbf s}{\tau_s}+\mathbf\Omega\times\mathbf s+\mathbf G.\]

Generation \(\mathbf G\) may use magnetic contacts or circular light; \(\mathbf\Omega\) represents spin–orbit/proximity fields; detection converts polarization to voltage, light helicity or tunnel conductance. Valley devices use an analogous population/coherence description with intervalley relaxation.

Nonlocal signal scale

Diffusive spin accumulation decays as \(\exp(-L/\lambda_s)\) with \(\lambda_s=\sqrt{D_s\tau_s}\). Hanle precession versus perpendicular field tests both sign and lifetime. Contact polarization and interface resistance must be included; conductivity mismatch can suppress injection.

Avoiding false positives

Local magnetoresistance mixes anisotropic/contact effects. Strong evidence uses nonlocal geometry, magnetization switching sequence, Hanle line shape, reciprocity and controls without the spin/valley channel. For valley Hall devices, ordinary Hall, thermoelectric and edge-current contributions must be separated.

Minimal Model

\[Information variable may be spin s, valley τ or a locked pair (s,τ)\]

This expression is a compact organizing relation, not a universal fitting law. Its assumptions, dimensionality and boundary conditions must be checked before quantitative use.

Device Consequences

Candidate devices include spin FETs, valley filters, valley valves and valley Hall transistors.

The correct comparison therefore follows the causal chain

\[\text{material and geometry}\rightarrow\text{states and barriers}\rightarrow\text{transport or switching}\rightarrow\text{terminal response}.\]

Limits and Diagnostic Tests

Injection efficiency, intervalley relaxation and unambiguous electrical readout remain central challenges. A credible experiment should report geometry, temperature, bias protocol, contact configuration and device-to-device statistics whenever they can change the interpretation.

深入推导与物理解释

产生、操纵与探测

内部极化向量 \(\mathbf s\) 服从 Bloch 输运方程

\[\frac{\partial\mathbf s}{\partial t}=D_s\nabla^2\mathbf s -\frac{\mathbf s}{\tau_s}+\mathbf\Omega\times\mathbf s+\mathbf G.\]

产生项 \(\mathbf G\) 可来自磁接触或圆偏振光，\(\mathbf\Omega\) 表示自旋轨道/邻近场，探测则把极化转为电压、光学手性或隧穿电导。谷器件可用带谷间弛豫的类似布居/相干方程描述。

非局域信号尺度

扩散自旋积累按 \(\exp(-L/\lambda_s)\) 衰减，其中 \(\lambda_s=\sqrt{D_s\tau_s}\)。垂直磁场 Hanle 进动同时检验信号符号与寿命。还必须计入接触极化和界面电阻；电导失配会压制注入。

避免假阳性

局域磁阻混合各向异性和接触效应。强证据需要非局域几何、磁化翻转顺序、Hanle 线形、互易性及去除自旋/谷通道的对照。谷霍尔器件还必须分离普通霍尔、热电与边缘电流。

最小模型

\[Information variable may be spin s, valley τ or a locked pair (s,τ)\]

这个式子用于组织物理关系，而不是在任何条件下都成立的拟合公式。定量使用前必须检查其近似、维度和边界条件。

器件意义

候选器件包括自旋 FET、谷过滤器、谷阀和谷霍尔晶体管。

合理的分析顺序是

\[\text{材料与几何}\rightarrow\text{状态与势垒}\rightarrow\text{输运或翻转}\rightarrow\text{端口响应}.\]

限制与判据

注入效率、谷间弛豫及无歧义电学读出仍是核心挑战。 当几何、温度、偏压流程、接触方式或器件间统计会影响结论时，可信实验必须完整报告这些条件。
