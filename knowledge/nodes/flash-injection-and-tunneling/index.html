Physical Picture

Flash changes stored charge through channel hot-electron injection, Fowler–Nordheim tunneling or related high-field transport, then reads the resulting threshold shift at low bias. The useful state variables and conserved quantities must be identified before selecting a transport model. In a real device the intrinsic material response is coupled self-consistently to electrostatics, contacts, disorder and the external circuit.

Deep Derivation and Physical Interpretation

Two injection mechanisms

Fowler–Nordheim tunnelling through a triangular oxide barrier follows

\[J_{FN}=A F_{ox}^2\exp(-B/F_{ox}),\]

where \(A\) and \(B\) contain oxide effective mass and barrier height. Channel hot-electron programming instead accelerates carriers laterally; a small high-energy fraction crosses the Si/oxide barrier. Its efficiency depends strongly on drain field and energy relaxation.

Self-limiting electrostatics

As electrons accumulate, floating-gate potential shifts and reduces the injection field. Program kinetics are therefore nonlinear: \(dQ/dt=AJ[F_{ox}(Q)]\). Incremental step-pulse programming uses this feedback deliberately to narrow threshold distributions.

Distinguishing leakage paths

Direct/FN tunnelling, trap-assisted tunnelling, stress-induced leakage and defect percolation have different area, temperature and stress-history signatures. Plotting \(\ln(J/F^2)\) versus \(1/F\) is useful only after the actual oxide field—not terminal voltage—has been established.

Minimal Model

\[J_FN≈A E_ox² exp(-B/E_ox)\]

This expression is a compact organizing relation, not a universal fitting law. Its assumptions, dimensionality and boundary conditions must be checked before quantitative use.

Device Consequences

Pulse amplitude, duration and program–verify feedback determine speed and threshold distribution.

The correct comparison therefore follows the causal chain

\[\text{material and geometry}\rightarrow\text{states and barriers}\rightarrow\text{transport or switching}\rightarrow\text{terminal response}.\]

Limits and Diagnostic Tests

High fields create oxide and interface defects, coupling program speed directly to endurance and retention loss. A credible experiment should report geometry, temperature, bias protocol, contact configuration and device-to-device statistics whenever they can change the interpretation.

深入推导与物理解释

两种注入机制

三角氧化层势垒中的 Fowler–Nordheim 隧穿满足

\[J_{FN}=A F_{ox}^2\exp(-B/F_{ox}),\]

其中 \(A\)、\(B\) 包含氧化层有效质量与势垒高度。沟道热电子编程则先在横向强场中加速载流子，再由少量高能电子越过 Si/氧化层势垒，其效率强烈依赖漏端电场和能量弛豫。

自限制静电反馈

电子积累后浮栅电势移动并减小注入场，因此编程动力学非线性：\(dQ/dt=AJ[F_{ox}(Q)]\)。增量阶梯脉冲编程正是利用这一反馈收窄阈值分布。

区分泄漏路径

直接/FN 隧穿、陷阱辅助隧穿、应力诱导泄漏与缺陷贯通具有不同的面积、温度和应力历史特征。只有先确定真实氧化层电场而非端口电压，\(\ln(J/F^2)\) 对 \(1/F\) 作图才有物理意义。

最小模型

\[J_FN≈A E_ox² exp(-B/E_ox)\]

这个式子用于组织物理关系，而不是在任何条件下都成立的拟合公式。定量使用前必须检查其近似、维度和边界条件。

器件意义

脉冲幅值、持续时间及编程–校验反馈决定速度和阈值分布。

合理的分析顺序是

\[\text{材料与几何}\rightarrow\text{状态与势垒}\rightarrow\text{输运或翻转}\rightarrow\text{端口响应}.\]

限制与判据

高电场会产生氧化层和界面缺陷，使编程速度直接牵连耐久与保持退化。 当几何、温度、偏压流程、接触方式或器件间统计会影响结论时，可信实验必须完整报告这些条件。
