Physical Picture

Two-dimensional superconductors combine phase fluctuations, strong spin–orbit coupling and gate-tunable carrier density; proximity coupling transfers pairing correlations into graphene or TMD channels. The useful state variables and conserved quantities must be identified before selecting a transport model. In a real device the intrinsic material response is coupled self-consistently to electrostatics, contacts, disorder and the external circuit.

Deep Derivation and Physical Interpretation

Pairing and two-dimensional phase stiffness

Mean-field pairing produces a gap \(\Delta\) and quasiparticle spectrum

\[E_k=\sqrt{\xi_k^2+|\Delta|^2}.\]

In 2D, however, loss of global superconductivity can be governed by vortex unbinding rather than disappearance of pair amplitude. The Berezinskii–Kosterlitz–Thouless criterion is

\[k_BT_{BKT}=\frac{\pi}{2}\rho_s(T_{BKT}^-),\]

with superfluid stiffness \(\rho_s\). Above \(T_{BKT}\) free vortices generate resistance even if local pairing survives.

Field response and Ising protection

Orbital pair breaking dominates perpendicular field. In some non-centrosymmetric TMDs, spin–valley locking pins opposite spins out of plane and raises the in-plane critical field beyond the weak-coupling Pauli value \(B_P\approx1.84T_c\) tesla per kelvin. This “Ising superconductivity” is reduced by intervalley scattering.

Experimental hierarchy

A resistance drop is only the first indication. Stronger evidence includes zero resistance within resolution, Meissner/diamagnetic response, a tunnelling gap with coherence peaks, critical-current scaling and BKT-like nonlinear \(V\propto I^{a(T)}\). Contact-induced superconductivity and filamentary paths must be separated from an intrinsic 2D condensate.

Minimal Model

\[I_s=I_c sinφ, Φ0=h/2e\]

This expression is a compact organizing relation, not a universal fitting law. Its assumptions, dimensionality and boundary conditions must be checked before quantitative use.

Device Consequences

The branch covers BKT physics, Ising pairing, Andreev reflection, Josephson junctions, SQUIDs and qubit components.

The correct comparison therefore follows the causal chain

\[\text{material and geometry}\rightarrow\text{states and barriers}\rightarrow\text{transport or switching}\rightarrow\text{terminal response}.\]

Limits and Diagnostic Tests

Zero resistance, Meissner response and phase coherence should be separated; interfaces often control the measured critical current. A credible experiment should report geometry, temperature, bias protocol, contact configuration and device-to-device statistics whenever they can change the interpretation.

深入推导与物理解释

配对与二维相位刚度

平均场配对产生能隙 \(\Delta\) 与准粒子谱

\[E_k=\sqrt{\xi_k^2+|\Delta|^2}.\]

但二维体系的全局超导消失可能由涡旋解绑控制，而不是配对振幅消失。Berezinskii–Kosterlitz–Thouless 判据为

\[k_BT_{BKT}=\frac{\pi}{2}\rho_s(T_{BKT}^-),\]

其中 \(\rho_s\) 是超流刚度。高于 \(T_{BKT}\) 时，自由涡旋即使在局域配对仍存在的情况下也会产生电阻。

磁场响应与 Ising 保护

垂直磁场主要通过轨道效应破坏配对。在某些无反演对称 TMD 中，自旋—谷锁定把相反自旋钉在面外，使面内临界场超过弱耦合 Pauli 值 \(B_P\approx1.84T_c\)（T/K）。这种“Ising 超导”会被谷间散射削弱。

实验证据层级

电阻下降只是第一层证据。更强证据包括分辨率内零电阻、Meissner/抗磁响应、带相干峰的隧穿能隙、临界电流标度以及 BKT 型非线性 \(V\propto I^{a(T)}\)。还需把接触诱导超导和丝状通路与本征二维凝聚区分开。

最小模型

\[I_s=I_c sinφ, Φ0=h/2e\]

这个式子用于组织物理关系，而不是在任何条件下都成立的拟合公式。定量使用前必须检查其近似、维度和边界条件。

器件意义

该分支涵盖 BKT 物理、Ising 配对、Andreev 反射、约瑟夫森结、SQUID 和量子比特元件。

合理的分析顺序是

\[\text{材料与几何}\rightarrow\text{状态与势垒}\rightarrow\text{输运或翻转}\rightarrow\text{端口响应}.\]

限制与判据

零电阻、迈斯纳响应和相位相干应分别验证，测得临界电流常由界面主导。 当几何、温度、偏压流程、接触方式或器件间统计会影响结论时，可信实验必须完整报告这些条件。
