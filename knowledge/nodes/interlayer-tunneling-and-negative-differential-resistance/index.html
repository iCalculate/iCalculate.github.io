Physical Picture

Atomically sharp vdW interfaces allow tunneling between two separately gated layers; energy and in-plane momentum alignment can create a resonant current peak followed by negative differential resistance. The useful state variables and conserved quantities must be identified before selecting a transport model. In a real device the intrinsic material response is coupled self-consistently to electrostatics, contacts, disorder and the external circuit.

Deep Derivation and Physical Interpretation

Spectral-overlap formulation

For weak tunnelling between two separately equilibrated 2D layers, Fermi’s golden rule can be written

\[I=\frac{ge}{\hbar}\sum_{\mathbf k,\mathbf q}|t_{\mathbf k\mathbf q}|^2 \int\frac{dE}{2\pi}A_1(\mathbf k,E)A_2(\mathbf q,E+eV)[f_1-f_2].\]

Clean aligned layers enforce approximately \(\mathbf k=\mathbf q\); twist displaces the valleys by \(\Delta\mathbf K\); disorder and phonons broaden momentum conservation. Current peaks when occupied spectral weight in one layer overlaps empty weight in the other in both energy and momentum.

Origin of negative differential resistance

As bias first improves alignment, \(dI/dV>0\). Past resonance the overlap decreases even while voltage rises, hence \(dI/dV<0\). The peak-to-valley ratio is controlled by lifetime broadening \(\Gamma\), temperature, twist disorder and electrostatic feedback. If charge transfer shifts the layer potentials, Poisson and tunnelling current must be solved self-consistently and bistability can appear.

Evidence hierarchy

A robust resonance should move predictably with both gates, sharpen on cooling until limited by \(\Gamma\), and depend systematically on twist angle. NDR alone is not proof of coherent momentum-conserving tunnelling: traps, self-heating and series resistance can also generate S-shaped or N-shaped curves.

Minimal Model

\[I∝Σ_k∫A_1(k,E)A_2(k,E+qV)[f_1-f_2]dE\]

This expression is a compact organizing relation, not a universal fitting law. Its assumptions, dimensionality and boundary conditions must be checked before quantitative use.

Device Consequences

The effect supports resonant tunneling diodes, oscillators and transverse TFET architectures.

The correct comparison therefore follows the causal chain

\[\text{material and geometry}\rightarrow\text{states and barriers}\rightarrow\text{transport or switching}\rightarrow\text{terminal response}.\]

Limits and Diagnostic Tests

Twist angle, disorder and phonon-assisted momentum relaxation broaden resonance and reduce peak-to-valley ratio. A credible experiment should report geometry, temperature, bias protocol, contact configuration and device-to-device statistics whenever they can change the interpretation.

深入推导与物理解释

谱函数重叠形式

对两个各自处于准平衡的二维层，弱隧穿的费米黄金律可写成

\[I=\frac{ge}{\hbar}\sum_{\mathbf k,\mathbf q}|t_{\mathbf k\mathbf q}|^2 \int\frac{dE}{2\pi}A_1(\mathbf k,E)A_2(\mathbf q,E+eV)[f_1-f_2].\]

洁净对准层近似保持 \(\mathbf k=\mathbf q\)；扭角使两个谷错开 \(\Delta\mathbf K\)；无序和声子则放宽动量守恒。当一层已占据谱权重与另一层空态在能量和动量上同时重叠时，电流达到峰值。

负微分电阻的来源

偏压起初改善对准，故 \(dI/dV>0\)；越过共振后，即使电压继续增大，谱重叠反而减小，出现 \(dI/dV<0\)。峰谷比由寿命展宽 \(\Gamma\)、温度、扭角无序和静电反馈决定。若电荷转移会移动层电势，就必须自洽求解泊松方程与隧穿电流，并可能出现双稳态。

证据层级

可靠共振应随双栅按可预测轨迹移动，降温后变尖直至受 \(\Gamma\) 限制，并系统依赖扭角。只有 NDR 并不能证明相干动量守恒隧穿；陷阱、自热和串联电阻也可能产生 S 型或 N 型曲线。

最小模型

\[I∝Σ_k∫A_1(k,E)A_2(k,E+qV)[f_1-f_2]dE\]

这个式子用于组织物理关系，而不是在任何条件下都成立的拟合公式。定量使用前必须检查其近似、维度和边界条件。

器件意义

该效应可用于共振隧穿二极管、振荡器和横向 TFET。

合理的分析顺序是

\[\text{材料与几何}\rightarrow\text{状态与势垒}\rightarrow\text{输运或翻转}\rightarrow\text{端口响应}.\]

限制与判据

扭角、无序及声子辅助动量弛豫会展宽共振并降低峰谷比。 当几何、温度、偏压流程、接触方式或器件间统计会影响结论时，可信实验必须完整报告这些条件。
