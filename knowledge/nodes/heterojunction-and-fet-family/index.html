Heterojunctions, JFETs, MESFETs, and HEMTs异质结、JFET、MESFET与HEMT

A unified view of JFETs, MESFETs, MODFETs/HEMTs, and two-dimensional carrier gases through depletion control and heterojunction band engineering.用耗尽层控制和异质结能带工程统一理解JFET、MESFET、MODFET/HEMT及二维电子气。

Field-Effect TransistorsInterface Physics2D Materials
Read in阅读语言

Core idea: Every field-effect transistor uses a terminal voltage to reshape a barrier or channel charge. The differences are whether the gate barrier is a PN junction, Schottky contact, or insulator, and whether the channel comes from bulk doping or a heterojunction interface.

JFET: A PN-Depletion Gate

A reverse-biased gate junction expands its depletion region and narrows the conductive channel. A JFET is a majority-carrier device with low ideal gate current, but it retains junction leakage and capacitance.

MESFET: A Schottky Gate

MESFETs suit III–V materials lacking a high-quality native oxide. Reverse bias on the Schottky gate controls channel thickness. Forward gate swing is limited by Schottky conduction.

Heterojunction Band Offsets

Two semiconductors generally form conduction- and valence-band discontinuities \(\Delta E_C\) and \(\Delta E_V\). These offsets create selective contacts, quantum wells, spatial separation between dopants and channels, and enhanced emitter efficiency in HBTs. Strain, polarization, lattice mismatch, and interface states modify the ideal lineup.

MODFET/HEMT and the Two-Dimensional Electron Gas

Figure 1 | A heterojunction well confines a 2DEG while a spacer separates it from ionized dopants. Sfu et al., CC BY-SA 3.0; source.

Electrons transfer from a wide-gap barrier into a narrow-gap channel, forming a triangular quantum well. Vertical motion is quantized while in-plane motion remains free. To first order,

\[Q_{2D}\approx-C_{eff}(V_G-V_T),\]

where \(C_{eff}\) may include barrier, quantum, and interface-state capacitances. In AlGaN/GaN, spontaneous and piezoelectric polarization can create a dense 2DEG without conventional modulation doping.

Band offsets as confinement and barriers

At a heterojunction, band-gap difference partitions into \(\Delta E_C\) and \(\Delta E_V\). These offsets confine one carrier while permitting another to cross, but interface dipoles and strain can shift the lineup away from simple electron-affinity rules.

In a modulation-doped HEMT, remote donors transfer electrons into a quantum well, separating carriers from ionized impurities. Sheet charge is approximately controlled by

\[qn_s\simeq C_{barrier}(V_G-V_T),\]

while subband energy and quantum capacitance refine this relation. JFETs control a conducting channel by depletion; MESFETs use a Schottky gate; HEMTs use a heterointerface 2DEG. Comparing them requires separating the common electrostatic principle from different injection, confinement and contact physics.

核心结论：场效应管的共同机制是“端电压改变势垒或沟道电荷”。区别只在于栅势垒由 PN 结、Schottky 结还是绝缘层提供，以及沟道由体掺杂还是异质结界面形成。

1. JFET：PN结耗尽层挤压沟道

JFET 的反偏栅结耗尽层随 \(|V_G|\) 增宽，逐步缩窄导电沟道。它是多数载流子器件，栅电流理想上很小，但仍存在反偏结漏电与结电容。与 MOSFET 相比，JFET 没有栅氧界面，却无法通过简单绝缘层获得同样强的栅电容控制。

2. MESFET：Schottky栅替代PN栅

MESFET 常用于没有高质量本征氧化层的 III–V 材料。金属 Schottky 栅的反偏耗尽区控制沟道厚度。正向栅压受 Schottky 导通限制，因此可用栅压范围小于理想 MOS 结构。

3. 异质结的带阶

两种半导体接触后，导带与价带通常不连续，形成 \(\Delta E_C\) 与 \(\Delta E_V\)。带阶可实现：

  • 只允许一种载流子越过的选择性接触；
  • 将电子或空穴限制在量子阱；
  • 把掺杂杂质与输运沟道空间分离，降低离化杂质散射；
  • 在 HBT 中提高发射效率。

材料晶格失配、极化电荷、界面态和应变会共同改变理想带阶图。

4. MODFET/HEMT与二维电子气

图 1｜调制掺杂异质结中，势阱容纳二维电子气，间隔层将沟道与离化杂质分开。原图：Sfu 等，CC BY-SA 3.0；文件页与许可。

宽禁带势垒层中的施主向窄禁带沟道转移电子，导带不连续形成三角势阱。电子在垂直方向量子化、平面内自由运动，形成 2DEG。片电荷与栅电压的第一近似仍是

\[Q_{2D}\approx-C_{eff}(V_G-V_T),\]

但 \(C_{eff}\) 可能由势垒几何电容、量子电容和界面态共同决定。AlGaN/GaN HEMT 中自发极化与压电极化也可在无传统调制掺杂时产生高密度 2DEG。

这组器件与二维材料 FET 可继续沿From Capacitors to FETs中的“电压 → 势 → 电荷 → 输运”统一分析。

能带偏移既是限域也是势垒

异质结带隙差分配为 \(\Delta E_C\) 与 \(\Delta E_V\)。这些偏移可限域一种载流子并允许另一种越过，但界面偶极与应变会使实际排列偏离简单电子亲和势规则。

调制掺杂 HEMT 中，远程施主把电子转移到量子阱，使载流子与离化杂质空间分离。面电荷近似满足

\[qn_s\simeq C_{barrier}(V_G-V_T),\]

更精确时还需加入子带能与量子电容。JFET 用耗尽控制导电沟道，MESFET 使用 Schottky 栅，HEMT 使用异质界面二维电子气。比较它们时，应把共同静电原理与不同注入、限域和接触物理解耦。
