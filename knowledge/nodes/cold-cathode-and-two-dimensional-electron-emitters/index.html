Physical Picture

A cold cathode emits electrons into vacuum without thermionic heating, commonly through field emission from a high-field nanoscale edge or tip. The useful state variables and conserved quantities must be identified before selecting a transport model. In a real device the intrinsic material response is coupled self-consistently to electrostatics, contacts, disorder and the external circuit.

Deep Derivation and Physical Interpretation

Field emission through the vacuum barrier

An external field \(F\) tilts the work-function barrier. Including image-charge lowering, Fowler–Nordheim current density is organized as

\[J=\frac{A_FF^2}{\Phi}t^{-2}(y) \exp\!\left[-\frac{B_F\Phi^{3/2}}{F}v(y)\right].\]

The local field is \(F=\beta V/d\), where geometric enhancement \(\beta\) concentrates field at an edge or tip. Extracted \(\beta\) is an effective parameter and can absorb uncertain emission area or work function.

Why atomically thin edges help—and fail

2D edges provide small radius and potentially sharp electronic states, lowering turn-on voltage. But emission current heats the tiny region; adsorbates change \(\Phi\); neighbouring emitters screen each other; ion bombardment and electromigration reshape the edge.

Source-quality metrics

Beyond a Fowler–Nordheim plot, report current stability, noise spectrum, lifetime at fixed current, emitting-area uniformity, vacuum level, turn-on definition and post-mortem morphology. For electron optics, brightness, energy spread and angular emittance matter more than maximum current alone.

Minimal Model

\[J_FN≈A F²/Φ exp[-B Φ^{3/2}/F]\]

This expression is a compact organizing relation, not a universal fitting law. Its assumptions, dimensionality and boundary conditions must be checked before quantitative use.

Device Consequences

Graphene edges, carbon nanostructures and other 2D materials offer sharp emitters for displays, electron sources and vacuum nanoelectronics.

The correct comparison therefore follows the causal chain

\[\text{material and geometry}\rightarrow\text{states and barriers}\rightarrow\text{transport or switching}\rightarrow\text{terminal response}.\]

Limits and Diagnostic Tests

Adsorbates, local heating, field screening, breakdown and emission-current stability dominate practical lifetime. A credible experiment should report geometry, temperature, bias protocol, contact configuration and device-to-device statistics whenever they can change the interpretation.

深入推导与物理解释

穿越真空势垒的场发射

外电场 \(F\) 倾斜功函数势垒。计入镜像电荷降低后，Fowler–Nordheim 电流密度写成

\[J=\frac{A_FF^2}{\Phi}t^{-2}(y) \exp\!\left[-\frac{B_F\Phi^{3/2}}{F}v(y)\right].\]

局域场 \(F=\beta V/d\)，几何增强因子 \(\beta\) 把电场集中到边缘或尖端。拟合得到的 \(\beta\) 是有效参数，可能吸收未知发射面积或功函数误差。

原子薄边缘为何有利、又为何失效

二维边缘曲率半径小并可能具有尖锐电子态，可降低开启电压；但发射电流会加热极小区域，吸附物改变 \(\Phi\)，相邻发射体彼此屏蔽，离子轰击与电迁移还会重塑边缘。

电子源质量指标

除 Fowler–Nordheim 作图外，应报告定电流稳定性、噪声谱、寿命、发射面积均匀性、真空度、开启定义及失效后形貌。用于电子光学时，亮度、能量展宽与角发射度比单纯最大电流更重要。

最小模型

\[J_FN≈A F²/Φ exp[-B Φ^{3/2}/F]\]

这个式子用于组织物理关系，而不是在任何条件下都成立的拟合公式。定量使用前必须检查其近似、维度和边界条件。

器件意义

石墨烯边缘、碳纳米结构及其他二维材料可形成尖锐发射体，用于显示、电子源和真空纳电子学。

合理的分析顺序是

\[\text{材料与几何}\rightarrow\text{状态与势垒}\rightarrow\text{输运或翻转}\rightarrow\text{端口响应}.\]

限制与判据

吸附物、局域发热、场屏蔽、击穿及发射电流稳定性主导实际寿命。 当几何、温度、偏压流程、接触方式或器件间统计会影响结论时，可信实验必须完整报告这些条件。
