Physical Picture

A TFET gate controls the spatial overlap of source valence-band states and channel conduction-band states, turning band-to-band tunneling on and off. The useful state variables and conserved quantities must be identified before selecting a transport model. In a real device the intrinsic material response is coupled self-consistently to electrostatics, contacts, disorder and the external circuit.

Deep Derivation and Physical Interpretation

Gate-controlled band-to-band tunnelling

An n-TFET aligns the source valence band with empty channel conduction-band states. For a locally triangular junction, a Kane/WKB form is

\[J_{BTBT}\propto F^P\exp\!\left[-\frac{B\sqrt{m_r}E_g^{3/2}}{F}\right],\]

where \(F\) is junction field, \(m_r^{-1}=m_e^{-1}+m_h^{-1}\) and \(P\) depends on dimensionality and directness. The gate changes both tunnelling width and the energy window; small-gap, light-mass materials improve current but also increase ambipolar leakage.

Why steepness is not automatically high current

Once the bands overlap, available modes, source degeneracy and transmission determine current. Quantum confinement can enlarge \(E_g\) and effective mass in the same thin body chosen for electrostatic control. Source depletion and interface charge also reduce the junction field, so a transfer curve must be solved with Poisson and tunnelling self-consistently.

Diagnostic signatures

Band-to-band current should show strong gate-field and junction-design dependence but weaker thermal activation than thermionic leakage. Temperature-independent current is not sufficient because trap-assisted tunnelling can also be weakly activated. Look for length/thickness scaling, polarity-consistent ambipolar branches, low-frequency noise and statistical tails that expose traps.

Minimal Model

\[I_BTBT∝F² exp[-B E_g^{3/2}sqrt(m_r)/F]\]

This expression is a compact organizing relation, not a universal fitting law. Its assumptions, dimensionality and boundary conditions must be checked before quantitative use.

Device Consequences

Low leakage and subthermal turn-on are attractive for low-voltage logic, but source degeneracy, electrostatics and tunneling mass determine useful on-current.

The correct comparison therefore follows the causal chain

\[\text{material and geometry}\rightarrow\text{states and barriers}\rightarrow\text{transport or switching}\rightarrow\text{terminal response}.\]

Limits and Diagnostic Tests

Low I_on, ambipolar leakage, trap-assisted tunneling and Miller capacitance remain major constraints. A credible experiment should report geometry, temperature, bias protocol, contact configuration and device-to-device statistics whenever they can change the interpretation.

深入推导与物理解释

栅控带间隧穿

n 型 TFET 使源区价带与沟道空导带态对准。对局域三角势垒，Kane/WKB 形式为

\[J_{BTBT}\propto F^P\exp\!\left[-\frac{B\sqrt{m_r}E_g^{3/2}}{F}\right],\]

其中 \(F\) 为结区电场，\(m_r^{-1}=m_e^{-1}+m_h^{-1}\)，幂次 \(P\) 取决于维度及直接/间接跃迁。栅极同时改变隧穿宽度与能量窗口；小带隙、轻有效质量提高开态电流，却也增加双极泄漏。

陡峭并不自动意味着大电流

能带重叠后，模式数、源端简并度与透射率共同决定电流。为改善静电而采用的超薄体又可能因量子限域增大 \(E_g\) 和有效质量。源区耗尽与界面电荷也会削弱结电场，因此必须自洽求解泊松方程和隧穿过程。

诊断特征

带间隧穿应强烈依赖栅场与结设计，但热激活弱于热发射泄漏。仅凭温度不敏感仍不充分，因为陷阱辅助隧穿也可弱激活。还需检查长度/厚度标度、与极性一致的双极支、低频噪声及揭示陷阱的统计长尾。

最小模型

\[I_BTBT∝F² exp[-B E_g^{3/2}sqrt(m_r)/F]\]

这个式子用于组织物理关系，而不是在任何条件下都成立的拟合公式。定量使用前必须检查其近似、维度和边界条件。

器件意义

低漏电和亚热开启适合低压逻辑，但源区简并、静电控制及隧穿质量决定可用开态电流。

合理的分析顺序是

\[\text{材料与几何}\rightarrow\text{状态与势垒}\rightarrow\text{输运或翻转}\rightarrow\text{端口响应}.\]

限制与判据

低开态电流、双极性泄漏、陷阱辅助隧穿和 Miller 电容仍是主要限制。 当几何、温度、偏压流程、接触方式或器件间统计会影响结论时，可信实验必须完整报告这些条件。
