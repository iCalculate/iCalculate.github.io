Physical Picture

When momentum-conserving electron–electron collisions are faster than momentum-relaxing scattering, carriers behave collectively as a viscous charged fluid. The useful state variables and conserved quantities must be identified before selecting a transport model. In a real device the intrinsic material response is coupled self-consistently to electrostatics, contacts, disorder and the external circuit.

Deep Derivation and Physical Interpretation

Collision hierarchy

Electron hydrodynamics requires momentum-conserving collisions to be fastest:

\[\ell_{ee}\ll W\ll\ell_{mr},\]

where \(W\) is channel width, \(\ell_{ee}\) the electron–electron collision length and \(\ell_{mr}\) the momentum-relaxing length from disorder or phonons. Local equilibrium then permits a velocity field \(\mathbf v\) and linearized Navier–Stokes equation

\[-\eta\nabla^2\mathbf v+\frac{mn}{\tau_{mr}}\mathbf v+\nabla P=ne\mathbf E.\]

For no-slip boundaries, a long strip develops a parabolic Poiseuille profile. The viscous length \(D_\nu=\sqrt{\eta\tau_{mr}/mn}\) controls current vortices and nonlocal voltage.

Gurzhi effect and observables

Because frequent electron–electron collisions can steer momentum away from rough boundaries, resistance may decrease as temperature initially rises—the Gurzhi effect—before phonon momentum relaxation takes over. Spatial imaging of current density, width scaling, vicinity resistance and the temperature window should be fitted consistently with one viscosity.

Alternative explanations

Ballistic trajectories can also yield negative vicinity resistance, and inhomogeneous current injection can mimic vortices. Hydrodynamic attribution requires the collision hierarchy, geometry dependence and temperature evolution, not one sign change. Viscosity is a collective transport coefficient and should not be inferred from mobility alone.

Minimal Model

\[η∇²v-neE-∇P≈0\]

This expression is a compact organizing relation, not a universal fitting law. Its assumptions, dimensionality and boundary conditions must be checked before quantitative use.

Device Consequences

Hydrodynamic flow enables nonlocal resistance, viscous imaging and possible low-loss electron-fluid structures.

The correct comparison therefore follows the causal chain

\[\text{material and geometry}\rightarrow\text{states and barriers}\rightarrow\text{transport or switching}\rightarrow\text{terminal response}.\]

Limits and Diagnostic Tests

Negative nonlocal resistance alone is not unique evidence; ballistic transport and geometry can mimic it. A credible experiment should report geometry, temperature, bias protocol, contact configuration and device-to-device statistics whenever they can change the interpretation.

深入推导与物理解释

碰撞层级

电子流体要求动量守恒碰撞最快：

\[\ell_{ee}\ll W\ll\ell_{mr},\]

其中 \(W\) 为沟道宽度，\(\ell_{ee}\) 为电子—电子碰撞长度，\(\ell_{mr}\) 为无序或声子导致的动量弛豫长度。局域平衡允许定义速度场 \(\mathbf v\)，线性化 Navier–Stokes 方程为

\[-\eta\nabla^2\mathbf v+\frac{mn}{\tau_{mr}}\mathbf v+\nabla P=ne\mathbf E.\]

在无滑移边界下，长条沟道形成抛物型 Poiseuille 速度分布；黏性长度 \(D_\nu=\sqrt{\eta\tau_{mr}/mn}\) 控制电流涡旋和非局域电压。

Gurzhi 效应与观测量

频繁电子—电子碰撞可使动量避开粗糙边界，因此电阻可能在升温初期反而下降，即 Gurzhi 效应；更高温时声子动量弛豫再占主导。电流空间成像、宽度标度、邻近电阻及温度窗口应能由同一黏度自洽解释。

替代解释

弹道轨迹同样可能产生负邻近电阻，非均匀注入也会伪装成涡旋。确认流体动力学需要同时满足碰撞层级、几何依赖和温度演化，不能只看一次符号反转。黏度是集体输运系数，也不能直接由迁移率推出。

最小模型

\[η∇²v-neE-∇P≈0\]

这个式子用于组织物理关系，而不是在任何条件下都成立的拟合公式。定量使用前必须检查其近似、维度和边界条件。

器件意义

电子流体可产生非局域电阻、黏性流成像，并启发低损耗电子流体结构。

合理的分析顺序是

\[\text{材料与几何}\rightarrow\text{状态与势垒}\rightarrow\text{输运或翻转}\rightarrow\text{端口响应}.\]

限制与判据

仅观察到负非局域电阻并非充分证据，弹道输运与几何效应也可能产生类似信号。 当几何、温度、偏压流程、接触方式或器件间统计会影响结论时，可信实验必须完整报告这些条件。
