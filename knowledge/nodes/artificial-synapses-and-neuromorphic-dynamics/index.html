Physical Picture

A physical synapse maps pulse history to conductance, while dynamical memristors can use relaxation, thresholds and volatility to emulate short- and long-term plasticity or neuronal dynamics. The useful state variables and conserved quantities must be identified before selecting a transport model. In a real device the intrinsic material response is coupled self-consistently to electrostatics, contacts, disorder and the external circuit.

Deep Derivation and Physical Interpretation

Pulse-history-dependent plasticity

A conductance synapse has update law

\[G_{n+1}=G_n+\Delta G_\pm(G_n,V_n,t_n)+\eta_n.\]

State-dependent \(\Delta G\) causes saturation and update asymmetry; noise \(\eta_n\) produces weight uncertainty. Pair-based STDP is often summarized as \(\Delta G\propto\pm e^{-|\Delta t|/\tau_\pm}\), but a device must generate this timing dependence from an explicit overlap circuit or internal relaxation.

Volatile dynamics as computation

Short-term plasticity can arise from a decaying state \(\dot x=-x/\tau+S(t)\), giving paired-pulse facilitation or depression. Networks exploit a distribution of \(\tau\) values to encode temporal context. Calling every relaxing current a “biological synapse” obscures whether the dynamics are reproducible and computationally useful.

Metrics tied to learning

Measure dynamic range, number of distinguishable states, potentiation/depression symmetry, nonlinearity, cycle noise, retention and update energy. Then train with the measured model. Open-loop pulse plots over a few cycles do not establish learnability; closed-loop verification may improve accuracy but its sensing overhead must be counted.

Minimal Model

\[ΔG=F(V_pulse,t_pulse,G,history)\]

This expression is a compact organizing relation, not a universal fitting law. Its assumptions, dimensionality and boundary conditions must be checked before quantitative use.

Device Consequences

FeFET, RRAM, PCM, floating-gate and sliding-ferroelectric devices provide different update mechanisms.

The correct comparison therefore follows the causal chain

\[\text{material and geometry}\rightarrow\text{states and barriers}\rightarrow\text{transport or switching}\rightarrow\text{terminal response}.\]

Limits and Diagnostic Tests

Biological resemblance is not a sufficient metric; update symmetry, linearity, noise, endurance and peripheral cost determine utility. A credible experiment should report geometry, temperature, bias protocol, contact configuration and device-to-device statistics whenever they can change the interpretation.

物理图像

物理突触把脉冲历史映射为电导，动态忆阻器还可利用弛豫、阈值与易失性模拟长短时可塑性或神经元动力学。 建模前必须先确定有效状态变量和守恒量。真实器件中的本征材料响应还会与静电势、接触、无序及外部电路自洽耦合。

深入推导与物理解释

依赖脉冲历史的可塑性

电导突触更新律为

\[G_{n+1}=G_n+\Delta G_\pm(G_n,V_n,t_n)+\eta_n.\]

状态依赖的 \(\Delta G\) 导致饱和和更新不对称，噪声 \(\eta_n\) 造成权重不确定。成对 STDP 常写成 \(\Delta G\propto\pm e^{-|\Delta t|/\tau_\pm}\)，但器件必须通过明确的脉冲重叠电路或内部弛豫产生这一时序关系。

把易失动力学用于计算

短时可塑性可来自衰减状态 \(\dot x=-x/\tau+S(t)\)，产生双脉冲易化或抑制。网络利用一组不同 \(\tau\) 编码时间上下文。把所有弛豫电流称作“生物突触”会掩盖其是否可重复、是否真的有计算价值。

与学习直接相关的指标

应测动态范围、可分辨状态数、增强/抑制对称性、非线性、循环噪声、保持与更新能量，再用实测模型训练。少量循环的开环脉冲图不能证明可学习性；闭环验证虽能提高精度，却必须计入感测开销。

最小模型

\[ΔG=F(V_pulse,t_pulse,G,history)\]

这个式子用于组织物理关系，而不是在任何条件下都成立的拟合公式。定量使用前必须检查其近似、维度和边界条件。

器件意义

FeFET、RRAM、PCM、浮栅和滑移铁电器件提供不同的权重更新机制。

合理的分析顺序是

\[\text{材料与几何}\rightarrow\text{状态与势垒}\rightarrow\text{输运或翻转}\rightarrow\text{端口响应}.\]

限制与判据

外观类似生物突触并非充分指标，更新对称性、线性、噪声、耐久及外围成本才决定实用性。 当几何、温度、偏压流程、接触方式或器件间统计会影响结论时，可信实验必须完整报告这些条件。
