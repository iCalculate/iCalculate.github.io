Physical Picture

An NC-FET places a ferroelectric response in the gate stack so that a change in gate voltage may produce a larger change in semiconductor surface potential. The useful state variables and conserved quantities must be identified before selecting a transport model. In a real device the intrinsic material response is coupled self-consistently to electrostatics, contacts, disorder and the external circuit.

Deep Derivation and Physical Interpretation

Landau curvature and voltage amplification

For a ferroelectric charge coordinate \(Q\approx P\), write

\[U_{FE}(Q)=\frac{\alpha}{2}Q^2+\frac{\beta}{4}Q^4+\frac{\gamma}{6}Q^6-V_{FE}Q, \qquad V_{FE}=\frac{dU_0}{dQ}.\]

The differential ferroelectric capacitance is \(C_{FE}^{-1}=dV_{FE}/dQ\). A negative local curvature can amplify the MOS surface potential when placed in series with \(C_{MOS}\). Quasistatic stability of the combined stack requires

\[\frac{d^2U_{tot}}{dQ^2}=C_{FE}^{-1}+C_{MOS}^{-1}>0.\]

Thus the negative branch cannot be discussed independently of capacitance matching and domain configuration.

Static stabilization versus transient overshoot

Landau–Khalatnikov dynamics \(\rho\dot P=-\partial U/partial P\) can produce transient voltage amplification when polarization lags a pulse. This is not equivalent to a single-valued quasistatic negative capacitance. Domain nucleation, wall motion and leakage can create rate-dependent hysteresis outside a uniform Landau model.

Required evidence

Compare simultaneous internal-node voltage, charge and polarization under matched sweep rates; demonstrate subthermal slope over useful current range with negligible hysteresis; measure capacitance and pulse response; test area and thickness scaling. A ferroelectric-gated FET showing a steep loop is not by itself an NCFET.

Minimal Model

\[A_V=dψ_s/dV_G=C_FE/(C_FE+C_MOS)\]

This expression is a compact organizing relation, not a universal fitting law. Its assumptions, dimensionality and boundary conditions must be checked before quantitative use.

Device Consequences

Capacitance matching seeks stable, preferably non-hysteretic internal voltage amplification for low-voltage logic.

The correct comparison therefore follows the causal chain

\[\text{material and geometry}\rightarrow\text{states and barriers}\rightarrow\text{transport or switching}\rightarrow\text{terminal response}.\]

Limits and Diagnostic Tests

Transient switching, domain dynamics, traps and measurement rate can imitate or obscure stabilized negative capacitance. A credible experiment should report geometry, temperature, bias protocol, contact configuration and device-to-device statistics whenever they can change the interpretation.

深入推导与物理解释

Landau 曲率与电压放大

以铁电电荷坐标 \(Q\approx P\) 写自由能

\[U_{FE}(Q)=\frac{\alpha}{2}Q^2+\frac{\beta}{4}Q^4+\frac{\gamma}{6}Q^6-V_{FE}Q, \qquad V_{FE}=\frac{dU_0}{dQ}.\]

微分铁电电容为 \(C_{FE}^{-1}=dV_{FE}/dQ\)。与 \(C_{MOS}\) 串联时，局域负曲率可放大 MOS 表面势；但组合堆叠的准静态稳定性要求

\[\frac{d^2U_{tot}}{dQ^2}=C_{FE}^{-1}+C_{MOS}^{-1}>0.\]

因此不能脱离电容匹配与畴结构单独讨论“负电容支”。

静态稳定化与瞬态过冲

Landau–Khalatnikov 动力学 \(\rho\dot P=-\partial U/\partial P\) 在极化滞后脉冲时可产生瞬态电压放大，但这不等价于单值准静态负电容。畴成核、畴壁运动与漏电还会产生超出均匀 Landau 模型的速率依赖滞回。

所需证据

应在相同扫描速率下同步比较内部节点电压、电荷和极化；证明有用电流区间内的亚热斜率与近零滞回；测量电容及脉冲响应，并检查面积、厚度标度。一个铁电栅 FET 出现陡峭回线，本身不能证明 NCFET 机制。

最小模型

\[A_V=dψ_s/dV_G=C_FE/(C_FE+C_MOS)\]

这个式子用于组织物理关系，而不是在任何条件下都成立的拟合公式。定量使用前必须检查其近似、维度和边界条件。

器件意义

电容匹配旨在实现稳定且尽量无滞回的内部电压放大，用于低压逻辑。

合理的分析顺序是

\[\text{材料与几何}\rightarrow\text{状态与势垒}\rightarrow\text{输运或翻转}\rightarrow\text{端口响应}.\]

限制与判据

瞬态翻转、畴动力学、陷阱及测量速率可能模拟或掩盖稳定负电容。 当几何、温度、偏压流程、接触方式或器件间统计会影响结论时，可信实验必须完整报告这些条件。
