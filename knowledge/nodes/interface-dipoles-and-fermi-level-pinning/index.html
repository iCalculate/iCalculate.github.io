Physical Picture

A metal–2D semiconductor contact is set by work functions, interface dipoles, metal-induced gap states, defects and local electrostatics rather than by the ideal Schottky–Mott rule alone. The useful state variables and conserved quantities must be identified before selecting a transport model. In a real device the intrinsic material response is coupled self-consistently to electrostatics, contacts, disorder and the external circuit.

Deep Derivation and Physical Interpretation

From Schottky–Mott to pinning

The ideal electron barrier \(\Phi_{Bn}=\Phi_M-\chi\) assumes an abrupt, charge-free interface. Interface states with charge \(Q_{it}\) and a dipole step \(\Delta\) modify the alignment. Linearizing charge neutrality gives the pinning factor

\[S\equiv\frac{d\Phi_{Bn}}{d\Phi_M} \approx\left(1+\frac{q^2D_{it}}{C_i}\right)^{-1},\]

so that \(S\to1\) is the Schottky–Mott limit and \(S\to0\) is strong pinning near a charge-neutrality level. In a van der Waals contact, suppressed chemical bonding can reduce \(D_{it}\), but metal wavefunction tails, contamination and processing defects can restore pinning.

Injection and apparent mobility

For thermionic injection, \(\rho_c\propto T^{-2}\exp(q\Phi_B/k_BT)\); for a thin barrier, thermionic-field emission and tunnelling dominate. A two-terminal channel then measures

\[R_{2p}=2R_c+\frac{L}{W\mu C_G(V_G-V_T)}.\]

Using this resistance to extract \(\mu\) without separating \(R_c\) produces a gate-dependent “mobility” that may mostly describe contact transparency.

Experimental separation

Use transfer-length structures or four probes to extract \(R_c\); vary contact length to test current crowding; combine temperature dependence with electrostatic band diagrams; compare top, edge and phase-engineered contacts. A claimed depinning strategy should change the work-function slope \(S\) across several metals, not merely improve one device after annealing.

Minimal Model

\[Φ_Bn≈S(Φ_M-χ)+(1-S)Φ_pin\]

This expression is a compact organizing relation, not a universal fitting law. Its assumptions, dimensionality and boundary conditions must be checked before quantitative use.

Device Consequences

Contact engineering determines injection, apparent mobility and whether an intrinsic channel effect can be measured.

The correct comparison therefore follows the causal chain

\[\text{material and geometry}\rightarrow\text{states and barriers}\rightarrow\text{transport or switching}\rightarrow\text{terminal response}.\]

Limits and Diagnostic Tests

Two-terminal transfer curves cannot cleanly separate channel transport from contact resistance. A credible experiment should report geometry, temperature, bias protocol, contact configuration and device-to-device statistics whenever they can change the interpretation.

深入推导与物理解释

从 Schottky–Mott 到费米能级钉扎

理想电子势垒 \(\Phi_{Bn}=\Phi_M-\chi\) 假设界面突变且无电荷。界面态电荷 \(Q_{it}\) 与偶极势阶 \(\Delta\) 会重新排列能带。对电荷中性条件线性化可得钉扎因子

\[S\equiv\frac{d\Phi_{Bn}}{d\Phi_M} \approx\left(1+\frac{q^2D_{it}}{C_i}\right)^{-1}.\]

\(S\to1\) 对应 Schottky–Mott 极限，\(S\to0\) 则表示势垒被钉扎在电荷中性能级附近。范德华接触因化学键较弱可降低 \(D_{it}\)，但金属波函数尾、污染和工艺缺陷仍会重新引入钉扎。

注入与表观迁移率

热发射满足 \(\rho_c\propto T^{-2}\exp(q\Phi_B/k_BT)\)；势垒足够薄时则转入热场发射或隧穿。二端器件测得

\[R_{2p}=2R_c+\frac{L}{W\mu C_G(V_G-V_T)}.\]

若不分离 \(R_c\) 就提取 \(\mu\)，所得栅压相关“迁移率”可能主要反映接触透明度，而非沟道散射。

实验分离方法

应使用传输线结构或四探针提取 \(R_c\)，改变接触长度检验电流拥挤，结合温度依赖与静电能带图，并比较面接触、边接触及相工程接触。真正的去钉扎方案应在多种金属上改变功函数斜率 \(S\)，而不只是退火后改善单个器件。

最小模型

\[Φ_Bn≈S(Φ_M-χ)+(1-S)Φ_pin\]

这个式子用于组织物理关系，而不是在任何条件下都成立的拟合公式。定量使用前必须检查其近似、维度和边界条件。

器件意义

接触工程决定载流子注入、表观迁移率以及能否观察到沟道本征效应。

合理的分析顺序是

\[\text{材料与几何}\rightarrow\text{状态与势垒}\rightarrow\text{输运或翻转}\rightarrow\text{端口响应}.\]

限制与判据

二端转移曲线无法可靠分离沟道输运与接触电阻。 当几何、温度、偏压流程、接触方式或器件间统计会影响结论时，可信实验必须完整报告这些条件。
