Why an atlas?

A chronological blog records when an idea became publishable. A knowledge graph records how ideas continue to meet one another.

This node is the starting point of the atlas. Published articles remain in the archive, while smaller notes can grow here without becoming blog posts. Links such as research taste connect both worlds.

The thematic gateways now include Energy and Chemistry and Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning, alongside the semiconductor, optoelectronic and condensed-matter networks.

Working convention

  • Articles are finished, chronological narratives.
  • Knowledge notes are compact and allowed to evolve.
  • Topics are generated automatically from tags and categories.
  • Wiki links express intentional relationships between ideas.
