Uncertainty Is Fourier Reciprocity

Heisenberg’s uncertainty principle is often paraphrased as: “A particle’s position and momentum cannot both be measured exactly.” That is not wrong, but it can suggest that uncertainty is merely a disturbance caused by an imperfect instrument.

The deeper statement is structural: the position-space and momentum-space wavefunctions are Fourier transforms of one another. Whenever a function is sharply localized in one variable, it must spread across its Fourier-conjugate variable. Position–momentum uncertainty and time–frequency uncertainty are therefore two appearances of the same Fourier reciprocity.

One Wave, Two Representations

Let the one-dimensional wavefunction in position space be \(\psi(x)\). Its amplitude in wave-number space is

\[\tilde{\psi}(k)=\frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi}} \int_{-\infty}^{\infty}\psi(x)e^{-ikx}\,dx.\]

The variables \(x\) and \(k\) are Fourier conjugates. Quantum mechanics then turns wave number into momentum through the de Broglie relation

\[p=\hbar k.\]

Consequently,

\[\tilde{\psi}(p)=\frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi\hbar}} \int_{-\infty}^{\infty}\psi(x)e^{-ipx/\hbar}\,dx.\]

The functions \(\psi(x)\) and \(\tilde\psi(p)\) are not two independent descriptions with independently chosen widths. They are two reciprocal representations of the same quantum state. Fixing either one already constrains the other.

When width is measured by the standard deviation, Fourier analysis gives

\[\Delta x\,\Delta k\geq \frac{1}{2}.\]

Using \(p=\hbar k\) produces

\[\boxed{\Delta x\,\Delta p\geq \frac{\hbar}{2}}.\]

In this sense, \(\hbar\) converts the mathematical \(x\)–\(k\) reciprocity into the physical \(x\)–\(p\) relation.

Why Narrower Means Broader

An infinitely extended monochromatic plane wave \(e^{ik_0x}\) has one exact wave number and therefore one exact momentum. Yet it fills all space, so its position is completely delocalized.

Conversely, a localized wave packet must be assembled from many plane waves:

\[\psi(x)=\frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi}} \int \tilde\psi(k)e^{ikx}\,dk.\]

The more tightly the packet is compressed, the wider the required range of wave numbers and momenta becomes. Uncertainty here is not missing knowledge: localization itself costs bandwidth.

A Gaussian packet reaches the limiting case. If

\[\psi(x)\propto \exp\left[-\frac{(x-x_0)^2}{4\sigma_x^2}\right]e^{ik_0x},\]

its Fourier transform is also Gaussian and

\[\Delta x\,\Delta k=\frac{1}{2}, \qquad \Delta x\,\Delta p=\frac{\hbar}{2}.\]

Gaussian states are therefore minimum-uncertainty states: the Fourier bound can be reached, but never crossed.

Time–Frequency: The Same Geometry

For a time-dependent signal \(s(t)\), the spectrum is

\[\tilde{s}(\omega)=\frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi}} \int_{-\infty}^{\infty}s(t)e^{-i\omega t}\,dt.\]

Time \(t\) and angular frequency \(\omega\) are Fourier conjugates, so

\[\boxed{\Delta t\,\Delta\omega\geq \frac{1}{2}}.\]

With ordinary frequency \(\nu=\omega/(2\pi)\), the same standard-deviation convention gives

\[\Delta t\,\Delta\nu\geq \frac{1}{4\pi}.\]

This single relation explains several familiar facts:

  • A long, nearly monochromatic wave has a narrow spectrum.
  • An ultrashort pulse necessarily occupies a broad frequency band.
  • A short analysis window locates when something happened but weakens frequency resolution.
  • A long observation resolves nearby frequencies but blurs rapid temporal changes.

Time and frequency resolution are not independent specifications that can both be improved without limit. They are projections of the same information onto reciprocal coordinates.

Why the Two Pairs Look Alike

Place the two Fourier kernels side by side:

\[e^{-ikx}=e^{-ipx/\hbar}, \qquad e^{-i\omega t}=e^{-iEt/\hbar},\]

where

\[p=\hbar k, \qquad E=\hbar\omega.\]

The correspondence is then transparent:

Original variable Fourier-conjugate variable Quantum conversion
Position \(x\) Wave number \(k\) \(p=\hbar k\)
Time \(t\) Angular frequency \(\omega\) \(E=\hbar\omega\)

A state cannot be infinitely localized in both members of a Fourier-conjugate pair. Sharpness in one representation must be paid for by spread in the other.

A Deeper Layer: Generators of Translation

Fourier structure enters physics for a reason deeper than wave-like appearance. Momentum generates spatial translations:

\[\psi(x-a)=e^{-ia\hat p/\hbar}\psi(x), \qquad \hat p=-i\hbar\frac{\partial}{\partial x}.\]

Energy—the Hamiltonian—generates time evolution:

\[|\psi(t)\rangle=e^{-it\hat H/\hbar}|\psi(0)\rangle.\]

Fourier modes are eigenmodes of translation: a plane wave gains only a phase under a spatial shift, just as a single-frequency oscillation gains only a phase under a time shift. Momentum is therefore naturally conjugate to position, while energy is naturally tied to the frequency of time evolution.

For position and momentum, the same structure appears as noncommutativity:

\[[\hat x,\hat p]=i\hbar.\]

The Robertson inequality

\[\Delta A\,\Delta B\geq \frac{1}{2}\left|\langle[\hat A,\hat B]\rangle\right|\]

immediately yields \(\Delta x\Delta p\geq\hbar/2\). Fourier reciprocity and operator noncommutativity are not competing explanations; they are two languages for the same mathematical structure.

A Crucial Distinction

Position–momentum and time–frequency are strongly analogous, but they should not be declared identical without qualification.

In ordinary nonrelativistic quantum mechanics, \(x\) and \(p\) are both observable operators. Time is normally a parameter describing evolution, not a universal operator satisfying \([\hat t,\hat H]=i\hbar\). Therefore:

  • \(\Delta x\Delta p\geq\hbar/2\) is a standard operator uncertainty relation.
  • \(\Delta t\Delta\omega\geq1/2\) is a Fourier-width relation for a waveform or signal.
  • The often-written \(\Delta E\Delta t\gtrsim\hbar/2\) requires a definition of \(\Delta t\), such as a lifetime, measurement duration, or the time required for a state to change appreciably.

Their safest common core is not that two perfectly identical operator pairs exist, but that spatial localization requires momentum bandwidth, while temporal localization requires frequency—and therefore energy—bandwidth.

Conclusion: Uncertainty Is the Price of Representation

The apparent conflict between position and momentum, or between time and frequency, is more precisely a reciprocal trade-off:

\[\text{greater concentration in one domain} \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad \text{greater spread in the conjugate domain}.\]

This is neither a temporary failure of instrumentation nor nature deliberately hiding an answer. It is a geometric constraint built into Fourier representation. Through \(p=\hbar k\) and \(E=\hbar\omega\), quantum mechanics writes this universal wave principle into physical quantities.

Seen this way, Heisenberg uncertainty is not an isolated quantum prohibition. It is the physical form of a broader rule: precise localization must be purchased with a broad spectrum.

不确定性是傅里叶互易性

海森堡不确定性原理常被表述为：“粒子的位置和动量不能同时被精确测量。”这句话没有错，却容易让人误以为不确定性只是测量仪器对粒子的扰动。

更深刻的图景是：位置波函数与动量波函数互为傅里叶变换。一个函数若在某个变量上高度集中，它在傅里叶共轭变量上就必然铺展。 因此，位置—动量不确定性与时间—频率不确定性，本质上是同一种“傅里叶互易性”。

同一个波，可以用两种坐标描述

设一维波函数在位置表象中为 \(\psi(x)\)。它在波数表象中的振幅为

\[\tilde{\psi}(k)=\frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi}} \int_{-\infty}^{\infty}\psi(x)e^{-ikx}\,dx.\]

\(x\) 与 \(k\) 是一对傅里叶共轭变量。量子力学又通过德布罗意关系

\[p=\hbar k\]

把波数变成动量，于是

\[\tilde{\psi}(p)=\frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi\hbar}} \int_{-\infty}^{\infty}\psi(x)e^{-ipx/\hbar}\,dx.\]

这并不是从“位置的不精确”推测“动量也许不精确”，而是在说：\(\psi(x)\) 与 \(\tilde\psi(p)\) 是同一个量子态的两种互易表示。你不能任意指定它们的宽度，因为指定其中一个，就已经通过傅里叶变换约束了另一个。

若以标准差衡量宽度，傅里叶分析给出

\[\Delta x\,\Delta k\geq \frac{1}{2},\]

代入 \(p=\hbar k\)，便得到

\[\boxed{\Delta x\,\Delta p\geq \frac{\hbar}{2}}.\]

所以 \(\hbar\) 的作用，是把纯数学的 \(x\)—\(k\) 互易关系换算成物理的 \(x\)—\(p\) 关系。

为什么“越窄，就越宽”

一个无限延伸的单色平面波 \(e^{ik_0x}\) 只有唯一波数 \(k_0\)，因此动量完全确定；但它遍布整个空间，位置完全不确定。

反过来，要制造一个局域的波包，就必须叠加许多不同波数的平面波：

\[\psi(x)=\frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi}} \int \tilde\psi(k)e^{ikx}\,dk.\]

波包压得越窄，参与叠加的波数范围就必须越广，动量分布也就越宽。这里的“不确定”不是知识不足，而是局域化本身需要带宽。

高斯波包把这种权衡推到极限。若

\[\psi(x)\propto \exp\left[-\frac{(x-x_0)^2}{4\sigma_x^2}\right]e^{ik_0x},\]

它的傅里叶变换仍为高斯函数，并满足

\[\Delta x\,\Delta k=\frac{1}{2}, \qquad \Delta x\,\Delta p=\frac{\hbar}{2}.\]

高斯态因此是最小不确定态：傅里叶互易性的边界可以达到，却不能越过。

时间—频率：同一几何结构

对随时间变化的信号 \(s(t)\)，频谱为

\[\tilde{s}(\omega)=\frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi}} \int_{-\infty}^{\infty}s(t)e^{-i\omega t}\,dt.\]

\(t\) 与角频率 \(\omega\) 同样互为傅里叶共轭变量，因此

\[\boxed{\Delta t\,\Delta\omega\geq \frac{1}{2}}.\]

若使用普通频率 \(\nu=\omega/(2\pi)\)，则在相同的标准差定义下

\[\Delta t\,\Delta\nu\geq \frac{1}{4\pi}.\]

这解释了许多看似不同的现象：

  • 持续很久的近单色波具有很窄的频谱；
  • 极短脉冲必须包含很宽的频率带宽；
  • 音频或光谱分析中，短时间窗能精确定位“何时发生”，却会降低“频率是多少”的分辨率；
  • 延长观测时间可以分辨更接近的频率，却会模糊快速变化发生的时刻。

因此，时间分辨率与频率分辨率并不是两个可以独立提高的指标。它们是同一份信息在两种互易坐标中的投影。

两组关系为什么如此相像

把两种傅里叶核并排写出：

\[e^{-ikx}=e^{-ipx/\hbar}, \qquad e^{-i\omega t}=e^{-iEt/\hbar},\]

其中使用了

\[p=\hbar k, \qquad E=\hbar\omega.\]

于是得到一组清晰的互易对应：

原变量 傅里叶共轭变量 量子力学换算
位置 \(x\) 波数 \(k\) \(p=\hbar k\)
时间 \(t\) 角频率 \(\omega\) \(E=\hbar\omega\)

这揭示了“不确定性”的共同来源：

一个状态无法在一对傅里叶共轭变量中同时无限局域化。一个表象中的尖锐，必然以另一个表象中的弥散为代价。

更深一层：动量与能量是平移的生成元

傅里叶关系之所以进入物理，不只是因为波函数“看起来像波”。空间平移由动量生成：

\[\psi(x-a)=e^{-ia\hat p/\hbar}\psi(x), \qquad \hat p=-i\hbar\frac{\partial}{\partial x}.\]

时间演化则由能量（哈密顿量）生成：

\[|\psi(t)\rangle=e^{-it\hat H/\hbar}|\psi(0)\rangle.\]

傅里叶基底正是平移操作的本征模式：平面波在空间平移下只获得一个相位，单频振荡在时间平移下也只获得一个相位。因此，动量自然成为位置的共轭量，能量自然与时间演化的频率相联系。

对位置与动量，这种结构也可以写成算符不对易：

\[[\hat x,\hat p]=i\hbar.\]

由 Robertson 不等式

\[\Delta A\,\Delta B\geq \frac{1}{2}\left|\langle[\hat A,\hat B]\rangle\right|\]

立刻得到 \(\Delta x\Delta p\geq\hbar/2\)。傅里叶互易与算符不对易不是两套互不相关的解释，而是同一数学结构的两种语言。

一个重要但常被忽略的区别

位置—动量与时间—频率高度同构，却不能未经说明就把它们完全等同。

在通常的非相对论量子力学中，\(x\) 和 \(p\) 都是可观测量算符；时间 \(t\) 通常是描述演化的参数，而不是一个与哈密顿量普遍满足 \([\hat t,\hat H]=i\hbar\) 的算符。因此：

  • \(\Delta x\Delta p\geq\hbar/2\) 是标准的算符不确定关系；
  • \(\Delta t\Delta\omega\geq1/2\) 是波形或信号的傅里叶宽度关系；
  • 常写的 \(\Delta E\Delta t\gtrsim\hbar/2\) 需要先说明 \(\Delta t\) 的含义，例如态发生显著变化的时间、寿命或测量持续时间，不能简单照搬位置—动量的算符推导。

它们最可靠的共同核心不是“存在两对完全一样的算符”，而是：空间局域化需要动量带宽，时间局域化需要频率（因而能量）带宽。

结论：不确定性不是缺陷，而是表示之间的代价

位置与动量、时间与频率的“冲突”，更准确地说是一种互易权衡：

\[\text{一个域中越集中} \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad \text{共轭域中越分散}.\]

这不是仪器暂时不够精密，也不是自然界故意隐藏答案，而是傅里叶表示本身的几何约束。量子力学借助 \(p=\hbar k\) 与 \(E=\hbar\omega\)，把这条普遍的波动规律写进了物理量之间。

从这个角度看，海森堡不确定性原理不是一条孤立的量子禁令，而是一条更广泛原则的物理版本：精确的局域性必须由广阔的谱来支付。
