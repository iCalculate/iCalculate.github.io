Physical Picture

Landau quantization or nontrivial band topology can create bulk gaps with conducting boundary channels protected against selected scattering processes. The useful state variables and conserved quantities must be identified before selecting a transport model. In a real device the intrinsic material response is coupled self-consistently to electrostatics, contacts, disorder and the external circuit.

Deep Derivation and Physical Interpretation

Bulk quantization and edge channels

In perpendicular magnetic field, a parabolic 2D band forms Landau levels

\[E_n=\hbar\omega_c\left(n+\frac12\right),\qquad \omega_c=\frac{eB}{m^*},\qquad N_B=\frac{eB}{h}\;\text{per area}.\]

Disorder localizes bulk states between broadened Landau-level centres. When the Fermi level lies in this mobility gap, the bulk Chern number gives \(\sigma_{xy}=\nu e^2/h\) and chiral edge modes carry current. In a topological insulator, spin–orbit-coupled band inversion can create an analogous boundary channel without Landau quantization.

Why edge conduction is measurable

At a boundary the confining potential bends Landau levels through the Fermi energy. Their group velocity has one sign on a given edge, preventing elastic backscattering unless a counter-propagating state is available. Landauer–Büttiker terminal potentials then predict quantized Hall and characteristic nonlocal resistances.

Protection has conditions

Finite-size overlap, magnetic disorder, inelastic processes and poorly equilibrated contacts create dissipation. A low longitudinal resistance alone can arise from current jetting or parallel bulk conduction. Strong evidence combines plateau accuracy, vanishing \(R_{xx}\), density–field filling-factor scaling, nonlocal edge transport and controlled breaking of the protecting symmetry.

Minimal Model

\[σ_xy=C e²/h\]

This expression is a compact organizing relation, not a universal fitting law. Its assumptions, dimensionality and boundary conditions must be checked before quantitative use.

Device Consequences

These states motivate edge interconnects, topological FET concepts and precision transport standards.

The correct comparison therefore follows the causal chain

\[\text{material and geometry}\rightarrow\text{states and barriers}\rightarrow\text{transport or switching}\rightarrow\text{terminal response}.\]

Limits and Diagnostic Tests

Protection is conditional rather than absolute; finite size, inelastic scattering, magnetic disorder and imperfect contacts cause dissipation. A credible experiment should report geometry, temperature, bias protocol, contact configuration and device-to-device statistics whenever they can change the interpretation.

深入推导与物理解释

体量子化与边缘通道

垂直磁场使抛物型二维能带形成 Landau 能级

\[E_n=\hbar\omega_c\left(n+\frac12\right),\qquad \omega_c=\frac{eB}{m^*},\qquad N_B=\frac{eB}{h}\;\text{每单位面积}.\]

无序会局域化展宽 Landau 能级中心之间的体态。当费米能级位于迁移率能隙内，体 Chern 数给出 \(\sigma_{xy}=\nu e^2/h\)，手性边缘模承担电流。拓扑绝缘体中，自旋轨道耦合导致的能带反转也可在无 Landau 量子化时产生类似边界通道。

为什么边缘导电可被测量

边界限制势使 Landau 能级弯曲并穿过费米能级。同一边缘上的群速度方向固定，若不存在反向传播态，弹性背散射就受到抑制。把这些通道代入 Landauer–Büttiker 端口电势关系，可得到量子化霍尔与特征非局域电阻。

保护具有条件

有限尺寸下两侧边缘重叠、磁无序、非弹性过程以及接触不充分平衡都会产生耗散。低纵向电阻也可能来自电流喷射或并联体导电。强证据应同时包括平台精度、趋零的 \(R_{xx}\)、密度—磁场填充因子标度、非局域边缘输运，以及可控破坏保护对称性的实验。

最小模型

\[σ_xy=C e²/h\]

这个式子用于组织物理关系，而不是在任何条件下都成立的拟合公式。定量使用前必须检查其近似、维度和边界条件。

器件意义

这些状态启发边缘互连、拓扑 FET 以及精密量子输运标准。

合理的分析顺序是

\[\text{材料与几何}\rightarrow\text{状态与势垒}\rightarrow\text{输运或翻转}\rightarrow\text{端口响应}.\]

限制与判据

拓扑保护具有条件性而非绝对无耗散，有限尺寸、非弹性散射、磁无序和接触都会引入损耗。 当几何、温度、偏压流程、接触方式或器件间统计会影响结论时，可信实验必须完整报告这些条件。
