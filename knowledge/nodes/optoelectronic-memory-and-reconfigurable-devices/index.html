Optoelectronic Memory, Photomemristors and Reconfigurable Devices光电存储、光忆阻与可重构器件

Physical Picture

Coupling photogeneration to trapped charge, ions, polarization or phase creates devices whose conductance remembers illumination and can combine sensing, memory and processing. The useful state variables and conserved quantities must be identified before selecting a transport model. In a real device the intrinsic material response is coupled self-consistently to electrostatics, contacts, disorder and the external circuit.

Minimal Model

\[dx/dt=G_photo(P,hν)-R(x)+F_write(V)\]

This expression is a compact organizing relation, not a universal fitting law. Its assumptions, dimensionality and boundary conditions must be checked before quantitative use.

Device Consequences

Applications include in-sensor computing, adaptive photodetectors, optical synapses and electrically reconfigurable logic–memory elements.

The correct comparison therefore follows the causal chain

Limits and Diagnostic Tests

Persistent photoconductivity can arise from uncontrolled traps; spectral selectivity, reset energy and retention must be characterized. A credible experiment should report geometry, temperature, bias protocol, contact configuration and device-to-device statistics whenever they can change the interpretation.

物理图像

把光生载流子与陷阱电荷、离子、极化或物相耦合，可使器件电导记住光照并融合感知、存储与处理。 建模前必须先确定有效状态变量和守恒量。真实器件中的本征材料响应还会与静电势、接触、无序及外部电路自洽耦合。

\[dx/dt=G_photo(P,hν)-R(x)+F_write(V)\]

这个式子用于组织物理关系，而不是在任何条件下都成立的拟合公式。定量使用前必须检查其近似、维度和边界条件。

应用包括传感内计算、自适应光探测器、光突触及电可重构逻辑–存储单元。
