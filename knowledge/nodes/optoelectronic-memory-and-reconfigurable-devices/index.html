Physical Picture

Coupling photogeneration to trapped charge, ions, polarization or phase creates devices whose conductance remembers illumination and can combine sensing, memory and processing. The useful state variables and conserved quantities must be identified before selecting a transport model. In a real device the intrinsic material response is coupled self-consistently to electrostatics, contacts, disorder and the external circuit.

Deep Derivation and Physical Interpretation

Coupled photocarrier and memory dynamics

Photomemory requires a photogenerated population to modify a slow internal state. A minimal pair of rate equations is

\[\dot n=G(P,h\nu)-\frac{n}{\tau_r}-C_tn(N_t-n_t), \qquad \dot n_t=C_tn(N_t-n_t)-e_tn_t.\]

Trapped charge \(n_t\) shifts a threshold or barrier after illumination ends. Coupling light to ions, polarization or phase requires additional state equations and can produce much longer memory.

Gain–bandwidth–retention trade-off

Photoconductive gain \(g\sim\tau_{life}/\tau_{transit}\) grows with lifetime, but long lifetime slows reset and narrows bandwidth. Persistent response is useful only when erase energy, dark drift and cycle reproducibility satisfy the application.

Separating sensing from uncontrolled trapping

Report spectral responsivity, external quantum efficiency with gain disclosed, temporal impulse response, dark retention and electrical/optical erase. Compare encapsulation and atmosphere, since adsorbates can dominate persistence. A reconfigurable truth table should be stable over device and cycle statistics rather than reconstructed from different bias points.

Minimal Model

\[dx/dt=G_photo(P,hν)-R(x)+F_write(V)\]

This expression is a compact organizing relation, not a universal fitting law. Its assumptions, dimensionality and boundary conditions must be checked before quantitative use.

Device Consequences

Applications include in-sensor computing, adaptive photodetectors, optical synapses and electrically reconfigurable logic–memory elements.

The correct comparison therefore follows the causal chain

\[\text{material and geometry}\rightarrow\text{states and barriers}\rightarrow\text{transport or switching}\rightarrow\text{terminal response}.\]

Limits and Diagnostic Tests

Persistent photoconductivity can arise from uncontrolled traps; spectral selectivity, reset energy and retention must be characterized. A credible experiment should report geometry, temperature, bias protocol, contact configuration and device-to-device statistics whenever they can change the interpretation.

深入推导与物理解释

光生载流子与存储状态耦合

光存储要求光生粒子改变一个慢内部状态。最小速率方程为

\[\dot n=G(P,h\nu)-\frac{n}{\tau_r}-C_tn(N_t-n_t), \qquad \dot n_t=C_tn(N_t-n_t)-e_tn_t.\]

俘获电荷 \(n_t\) 在光照结束后仍移动阈值或势垒。若光与离子、极化或物相耦合，还需增加状态方程并可获得更长记忆。

增益—带宽—保持权衡

光导增益 \(g\sim\tau_{life}/\tau_{transit}\) 随寿命增加，但长寿命会减慢复位并缩小带宽。持续响应只有在擦除能量、暗态漂移和循环重复性满足应用时才有价值。

区分感知与不可控俘获

应报告光谱响应率、明确增益后的外量子效率、时间冲激响应、暗态保持及电/光擦除，并比较封装和气氛，因为吸附物可能主导持续性。可重构真值表应在器件和循环统计上稳定，而不是从不同偏压点拼接。

最小模型

\[dx/dt=G_photo(P,hν)-R(x)+F_write(V)\]

这个式子用于组织物理关系，而不是在任何条件下都成立的拟合公式。定量使用前必须检查其近似、维度和边界条件。

器件意义

应用包括传感内计算、自适应光探测器、光突触及电可重构逻辑–存储单元。

合理的分析顺序是

\[\text{材料与几何}\rightarrow\text{状态与势垒}\rightarrow\text{输运或翻转}\rightarrow\text{端口响应}.\]

限制与判据

持续光电导可能来自不可控陷阱，需要表征光谱选择性、复位能量和保持特性。 当几何、温度、偏压流程、接触方式或器件间统计会影响结论时，可信实验必须完整报告这些条件。
