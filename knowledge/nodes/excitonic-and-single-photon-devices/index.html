Physical Picture

Localized excitons in defects, strain potentials or moiré cells can emit one photon at a time, while interlayer excitons provide electrically tunable dipoles and transport. The useful state variables and conserved quantities must be identified before selecting a transport model. In a real device the intrinsic material response is coupled self-consistently to electrostatics, contacts, disorder and the external circuit.

Deep Derivation and Physical Interpretation

Single-photon criterion

For emitted intensity \(I(t)\), the normalized correlation

\[g^{(2)}(\tau)=\frac{\langle I(t)I(t+\tau)\rangle}{\langle I(t)\rangle^2}\]

shows antibunching with \(g^{(2)}(0)<0.5\) for a predominantly single emitter. Background and detector timing must be deconvolved; antibunching alone does not guarantee indistinguishable photons.

Brightness and coherence

Radiative rate can be enhanced by a cavity Purcell factor \(F_P\propto(Q/V_m)|\mathbf d\cdot\mathbf E|^2\). Useful sources require extraction efficiency, spectral stability and coherence time approaching the transform limit \(T_2\le2T_1\). Charge noise causes spectral diffusion; phonons create sidebands.

Deterministic integration

Defects, strain traps and moiré sites localize excitons, but random location and energy hinder circuits. Report yield per site, tuning range, collection efficiency, \(g^{(2)}(0)\), Hong–Ou–Mandel visibility where relevant, and stability over time. Electrically driven emission should separate recombination in the intended quantum state from defect-assisted leakage.

Minimal Model

\[Single-photon quality is tested by g²(0)<0.5\]

This expression is a compact organizing relation, not a universal fitting law. Its assumptions, dimensionality and boundary conditions must be checked before quantitative use.

Device Consequences

The branch includes excitonic transistors, modulators, valley-selective detectors and on-chip quantum emitters.

The correct comparison therefore follows the causal chain

\[\text{material and geometry}\rightarrow\text{states and barriers}\rightarrow\text{transport or switching}\rightarrow\text{terminal response}.\]

Limits and Diagnostic Tests

Spectral diffusion, indistinguishability, collection efficiency and deterministic placement limit scaling. A credible experiment should report geometry, temperature, bias protocol, contact configuration and device-to-device statistics whenever they can change the interpretation.

深入推导与物理解释

单光子判据

对发光强度 \(I(t)\)，归一化关联

\[g^{(2)}(\tau)=\frac{\langle I(t)I(t+\tau)\rangle}{\langle I(t)\rangle^2}\]

若 \(g^{(2)}(0)<0.5\)，说明主要来自单发射体的反聚束。必须去除背景并反卷积探测器时间响应；反聚束本身不保证光子不可区分。

亮度与相干

腔体可用 Purcell 因子 \(F_P\propto(Q/V_m)|\mathbf d\cdot\mathbf E|^2\) 增强辐射率。实用光源还要求高提取效率、光谱稳定，以及接近变换极限 \(T_2\le2T_1\) 的相干时间。电荷噪声导致谱漂移，声子产生边带。

确定性集成

缺陷、应变陷阱与莫尔位点可局域激子，但随机位置和能量阻碍电路化。应报告单位点位良率、调谐范围、收集效率、\(g^{(2)}(0)\)、必要时的 Hong–Ou–Mandel 可见度及长期稳定性。电注入发光还需区分目标量子态复合与缺陷辅助泄漏。

最小模型

\[Single-photon quality is tested by g²(0)<0.5\]

这个式子用于组织物理关系，而不是在任何条件下都成立的拟合公式。定量使用前必须检查其近似、维度和边界条件。

器件意义

该分支包括激子晶体管、调制器、谷选择探测器和片上量子发射体。

合理的分析顺序是

\[\text{材料与几何}\rightarrow\text{状态与势垒}\rightarrow\text{输运或翻转}\rightarrow\text{端口响应}.\]

限制与判据

谱漂移、光子不可区分性、收集效率及确定性定位限制规模化。 当几何、温度、偏压流程、接触方式或器件间统计会影响结论时，可信实验必须完整报告这些条件。
