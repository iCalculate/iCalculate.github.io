Energy Bands, Density of States, and Carrier Statistics能带、态密度与载流子统计

From bands in a periodic potential to electron and hole concentrations through density of states, Fermi statistics, and charge neutrality.从周期势中的能带出发，用态密度、费米分布和电中性条件得到电子与空穴浓度。

Read in阅读语言

Core idea: The band structure determines which energies are allowed, the density of states counts how many states exist per energy, and the Fermi–Dirac function gives their occupation probability. Together they determine carrier concentration.

Figure 1 | Conceptual band-gap diagram. Pieter Kuiper, public domain; source and license. It is not a quantitative \(E(k)\) dispersion.

From a Crystal to Energy Bands

Atomic levels broaden into bands when many atoms form a periodic lattice. Bloch’s theorem writes the eigenstates as

\[\psi_{n\mathbf{k}}(\mathbf r)=u_{n\mathbf{k}}(\mathbf r)e^{i\mathbf{k}\cdot\mathbf r}.\]

Near a band edge, the dispersion is often approximated by

\[E_c(\mathbf k)\approx E_c+\frac{\hbar^2k^2}{2m_n^*},\qquad E_v(\mathbf k)\approx E_v-\frac{\hbar^2k^2}{2m_p^*}.\]

The effective mass compresses the influence of the periodic crystal potential into a measurable parameter. It controls density of states, mobility, tunneling probability, and quantum-confinement scales.

Density of States and Occupation

For a three-dimensional parabolic band,

\[N_C=2\left(\frac{2\pi m_n^*kT}{h^2}\right)^{3/2},\qquad N_V=2\left(\frac{2\pi m_p^*kT}{h^2}\right)^{3/2}.\]

Fermions occupy these states according to

\[f(E)=\frac{1}{1+\exp[(E-E_F)/kT]}.\]

Figure 2 | Temperature broadens the occupation within a few \(kT\) of \(E_F\), while \(f(E_F)=1/2\). Cepheiden, public domain; source.

For a nondegenerate semiconductor,

\[n=N_Ce^{-(E_C-E_F)/kT},\qquad p=N_Ve^{-(E_F-E_V)/kT},\]

and therefore

\[np=n_i^2,\qquad n_i=\sqrt{N_CN_V}e^{-E_g/(2kT)}.\]

The mass-action law applies directly only in thermal equilibrium. Under nonequilibrium conditions, electron and hole quasi-Fermi levels replace the single \(E_F\).

Doping and Charge Neutrality

Donors and acceptors introduce levels near the band edges. The general neutrality condition is

\[p+N_D^+=n+N_A^-.\]

For shallow, fully ionized dopants with \(N_D\gg n_i\), an n-type sample has \(n\approx N_D-N_A\) and \(p=n_i^2/n\). Doping does not itself “create current”; it moves the Fermi level and changes the carrier reservoir. A field or electrochemical-potential gradient is still required for current.

The Grammar of Band Diagrams

Band edges follow the electrostatic potential:

\[E_C(x),E_V(x),E_i(x)=\text{constant}-q\psi(x),\qquad \mathcal E=\frac{1}{q}\frac{dE_i}{dx}.\]

Band slope therefore encodes electric field, while a flat equilibrium Fermi level encodes zero net current. These two rules underlie the PN junction, Schottky contact, and MOS analysis.

Common Pitfalls

  • A hole is an effective degree of freedom associated with a missing valence electron, not a fundamental positive particle.
  • \(E_F\) is not generally the highest occupied electron energy; that simplified interpretation holds at \(T=0\).
  • Band gap alone does not determine conductivity; carrier density, mobility, defects, and contacts also matter.
  • Degenerate doping requires Fermi integrals and may require band-gap narrowing and many-body corrections.

From dispersion to charge density

For a 3D parabolic conduction band, counting \(k\) states inside a sphere gives

\[D_c(E)=\frac{g_vm_d^{3/2}}{\sqrt2\pi^2\hbar^3}\sqrt{E-E_c}.\]

Carrier density is an occupation integral, \(n=\int D_c(E)f(E)dE=N_CF_{1/2}(\eta_n)\). Only in the nondegenerate limit \(\eta_n=(E_F-E_C)/k_BT\ll-1\) does this reduce to \(n=N_Ce^{\eta_n}\). Charge neutrality,

\[n+N_A^-=p+N_D^+,\]

then determines \(E_F\); incomplete ionization and band-gap narrowing must be added at low temperature or heavy doping. In 2D a parabolic band has step-like DOS, while a Dirac band has \(D(E)\propto|E|\), so dimensionality changes both quantum capacitance and how rapidly charge appears at a band edge.

1. 从晶体到能带

孤立原子的能级离散；大量原子形成周期晶格后，外层轨道耦合，原子能级展宽成能带。布洛赫定理给出周期势中的波函数

\[\psi_{n\mathbf{k}}(\mathbf r)=u_{n\mathbf{k}}(\mathbf r)e^{i\mathbf{k}\cdot\mathbf r}.\]

在带边附近常用抛物线近似

\[E_c(\mathbf k)\approx E_c+\frac{\hbar^2 k^2}{2m_n^*},\qquad E_v(\mathbf k)\approx E_v-\frac{\hbar^2 k^2}{2m_p^*}.\]

有效质量 \(m^*\) 把晶格周期势的影响压缩进一个实验可测的参数。它控制态密度、迁移率、隧穿概率和量子限域尺度。

2. 态密度与占据概率

三维抛物线能带的有效态密度为

\[N_C=2\left(\frac{2\pi m_n^*kT}{h^2}\right)^{3/2},\qquad N_V=2\left(\frac{2\pi m_p^*kT}{h^2}\right)^{3/2}.\]

费米–狄拉克分布为

\[f(E)=\frac{1}{1+\exp[(E-E_F)/kT]}.\]

图 2｜温度使费米面附近约数个 \(kT\) 的占据发生展宽；始终有 \(f(E_F)=1/2\)。原图：Cepheiden，公有领域；文件页。

非简并半导体满足 \(E_C-E_F\gtrsim3kT\)、\(E_F-E_V\gtrsim3kT\) 时，可用 Maxwell–Boltzmann 近似：

\[n=N_Ce^{-(E_C-E_F)/kT},\qquad p=N_Ve^{-(E_F-E_V)/kT}.\]

于是

\[np=n_i^2,\qquad n_i=\sqrt{N_CN_V}\,e^{-E_g/(2kT)}.\]

质量作用定律只对热平衡、同一温度的非简并半导体直接成立；非平衡时要改用准费米能级。

3. 掺杂与电中性

施主和受主引入靠近带边的杂质能级。一般电中性条件是

\[p+N_D^+=n+N_A^-.\]

在室温、浅能级近似完全电离且 \(N_D\gg n_i\) 时，n 型材料有 \(n\approx N_D-N_A\)、\(p=n_i^2/n\)。这不是“掺杂直接提供电流”，而是掺杂移动 \(E_F\)、改变平衡载流子库；电流仍需电场或电化学势梯度驱动。

4. 能带图的物理语法

带边随静电势移动：

\[E_C(x),E_V(x),E_i(x)=\text{常数}-q\psi(x),\qquad \mathcal E(x)=-\frac{d\psi}{dx}=\frac{1}{q}\frac{dE_i}{dx}.\]

因此能带斜率编码电场；平衡费米能级水平则编码“无净电流”。PN结：空间电荷、能带与整流、金属-半导体接触与肖特基势垒及 MOS 结构的分析都从这两条规则开始。

5. 常见误区

  • 空穴不是一个真实正粒子，而是近满价带中缺失电子的有效自由度。
  • \(E_F\) 不是“电子实际占据的最高能量”；只有 \(T=0\) 时才有这种简化解释。
  • 禁带宽度不等于唯一的导电性指标；载流子浓度、迁移率、缺陷和接触同样关键。
  • 简并掺杂下不能继续使用指数形式，必须计算费米积分并考虑带隙变窄等效应。

从色散关系到电荷密度

对三维抛物型导带，在 \(k\) 空间球内计数得到

\[D_c(E)=\frac{g_vm_d^{3/2}}{\sqrt2\pi^2\hbar^3}\sqrt{E-E_c}.\]

载流子密度是占据积分 \(n=\int D_c(E)f(E)dE=N_CF_{1/2}(\eta_n)\)。只有非简并极限 \(\eta_n=(E_F-E_C)/k_BT\ll-1\) 才化为 \(n=N_Ce^{\eta_n}\)。再由电中性条件

\[n+N_A^-=p+N_D^+\]

确定 \(E_F\)；低温或重掺杂时还需加入不完全电离与带隙收窄。二维抛物带具有阶梯态密度，Dirac 带则满足 \(D(E)\propto|E|\)，因此维度会改变量子电容以及带边出现电荷的速度。
