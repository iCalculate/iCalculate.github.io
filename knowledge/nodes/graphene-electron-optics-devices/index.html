Physical Picture

Ballistic Dirac carriers refract at gate-defined p–n interfaces and can be collimated or focused when phase coherence and mean free path exceed device dimensions. The useful state variables and conserved quantities must be identified before selecting a transport model. In a real device the intrinsic material response is coupled self-consistently to electrostatics, contacts, disorder and the external circuit.

Deep Derivation and Physical Interpretation

From junction optics to a device transfer function

An electron-optics element maps an injected angular distribution \(F_{in}(\theta,E)\) to a collector current

\[I_C\propto\int dE\,d\theta\,F_{in}(\theta,E)T_{pn}(\theta,E)A_C(\theta,E).\]

\(T_{pn}\) is the chiral junction transmission and \(A_C\) the collector acceptance. Gates independently tune refractive index and focal length; magnetic field bends rays with \(r_c=\hbar k_F/eB\).

Coherence and aberration

Veselago focusing needs mean free path longer than source–collector distance. Interferometers additionally require \(L_\phi\) longer than path difference. Density gradients, contact width and trigonal warping create aberration; edge roughness mixes valleys.

Device-level validation

Map collector response versus two gate voltages and magnetic field, compare with ray tracing or quantum transport using measured geometry, and verify reciprocity. A focusing peak should track the predicted caustic rather than merely coincide with a resistance minimum. Practical scaling must include collimator loss and cryogenic/encapsulation requirements.

Minimal Model

\[k_n sinθ_n=-k_p sinθ_p is the negative-refraction analogue of Snell's law\]

This expression is a compact organizing relation, not a universal fitting law. Its assumptions, dimensionality and boundary conditions must be checked before quantitative use.

Device Consequences

Concepts include Veselago lenses, collimators, interferometers and valley-sensitive beam splitters.

The correct comparison therefore follows the causal chain

\[\text{material and geometry}\rightarrow\text{states and barriers}\rightarrow\text{transport or switching}\rightarrow\text{terminal response}.\]

Limits and Diagnostic Tests

They require clean encapsulated channels, sharp electrostatic profiles and often low temperature. A credible experiment should report geometry, temperature, bias protocol, contact configuration and device-to-device statistics whenever they can change the interpretation.

深入推导与物理解释

从结区光学到器件传递函数

电子光学元件把注入角分布 \(F_{in}(\theta,E)\) 映射为收集电流

\[I_C\propto\int dE\,d\theta\,F_{in}(\theta,E)T_{pn}(\theta,E)A_C(\theta,E).\]

\(T_{pn}\) 是手性结透射率，\(A_C\) 是收集器接受函数。栅极独立调节折射率与焦距，磁场按 \(r_c=\hbar k_F/eB\) 弯曲轨迹。

相干与像差

Veselago 聚焦要求平均自由程超过源—收集距离；干涉仪还要求 \(L_\phi\) 超过路径差。密度梯度、接触宽度和三角翘曲产生像差，边缘粗糙引起谷混合。

器件级验证

应绘制收集响应随双栅与磁场的图，与使用实测几何的射线或量子输运计算比较，并检查互易性。聚焦峰应跟随预测焦散移动，而不只是偶然与电阻极小重合。实用缩放还必须计入准直损失、低温和封装要求。

最小模型

\[k_n sinθ_n=-k_p sinθ_p is the negative-refraction analogue of Snell's law\]

这个式子用于组织物理关系，而不是在任何条件下都成立的拟合公式。定量使用前必须检查其近似、维度和边界条件。

器件意义

相关概念包括 Veselago 透镜、准直器、干涉仪及谷敏感分束器。

合理的分析顺序是

\[\text{材料与几何}\rightarrow\text{状态与势垒}\rightarrow\text{输运或翻转}\rightarrow\text{端口响应}.\]

限制与判据

它们需要洁净封装沟道、陡峭静电势型，并通常依赖低温。 当几何、温度、偏压流程、接触方式或器件间统计会影响结论时，可信实验必须完整报告这些条件。
