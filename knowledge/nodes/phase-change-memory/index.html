Physical Picture

PCM stores information in the resistance contrast between amorphous and crystalline phases produced by controlled Joule-heating pulses. The useful state variables and conserved quantities must be identified before selecting a transport model. In a real device the intrinsic material response is coupled self-consistently to electrostatics, contacts, disorder and the external circuit.

Deep Derivation and Physical Interpretation

Thermal phase trajectory

PCM stores the amorphous/crystalline fraction of a chalcogenide. A short high-power RESET pulse melts a region and rapid quenching freezes an amorphous plug; a longer lower-temperature SET pulse enables crystallization. Local temperature obeys

\[\rho C_p\frac{\partial T}{\partial t}=\nabla\cdot(k\nabla T)+\mathbf J\cdot\mathbf E.\]

Electrical and thermal problems are coupled because conductivity changes by orders of magnitude during transformation.

Nucleation and growth

Crystalline fraction \(X\) is often organized by Johnson–Mehl–Avrami kinetics \(X=1-\exp[-(Kt)^n]\), but nanoscale cells have nonuniform temperature and stochastic nucleation. Threshold switching in amorphous material is an electronic high-field transition that precedes Joule heating; it should not be confused with crystallization.

Drift and endurance

Amorphous resistance drifts approximately \(R(t)=R_0(t/t_0)^\nu\) as structural relaxation changes trap landscape. Melt–quench stress, elemental segregation, voids and electrode reactions limit endurance. Multilevel storage must include drift-aware read references and distributions, not only freshly programmed resistance levels.

Minimal Model

\[C dT/dt=P_Joule-G_th(T-T_0); crystallization follows nucleation and growth\]

This expression is a compact organizing relation, not a universal fitting law. Its assumptions, dimensionality and boundary conditions must be checked before quantitative use.

Device Consequences

Fast melt–quench reset and slower crystallization set enable digital storage, multilevel states and analogue accumulation.

The correct comparison therefore follows the causal chain

\[\text{material and geometry}\rightarrow\text{states and barriers}\rightarrow\text{transport or switching}\rightarrow\text{terminal response}.\]

Limits and Diagnostic Tests

Resistance drift, thermal crosstalk, elemental segregation and write energy limit scaling and analogue precision. A credible experiment should report geometry, temperature, bias protocol, contact configuration and device-to-device statistics whenever they can change the interpretation.

深入推导与物理解释

热驱动物相轨迹

PCM 存储硫系材料的非晶/晶态比例。短而高功率的 RESET 脉冲熔化局部并快速淬火形成非晶塞；较长、较低温的 SET 脉冲促进晶化。局域温度满足

\[\rho C_p\frac{\partial T}{\partial t}=\nabla\cdot(k\nabla T)+\mathbf J\cdot\mathbf E.\]

转变过程中电导变化多个数量级，因此电学与热学必须耦合求解。

成核与生长

晶化分数 \(X\) 常用 Johnson–Mehl–Avrami 动力学 \(X=1-\exp[-(Kt)^n]\) 组织，但纳米单元温度不均且成核随机。非晶材料的阈值开关是高场电子转变，先于 Joule 加热，不能与晶化混为一谈。

漂移与耐久

结构弛豫改变陷阱景观，使非晶电阻近似按 \(R(t)=R_0(t/t_0)^\nu\) 漂移。熔融—淬火应力、元素偏析、空洞及电极反应限制耐久。多级存储必须采用漂移感知读参考并报告分布，而不只是新写入的电阻电平。

最小模型

\[C dT/dt=P_Joule-G_th(T-T_0); crystallization follows nucleation and growth\]

这个式子用于组织物理关系，而不是在任何条件下都成立的拟合公式。定量使用前必须检查其近似、维度和边界条件。

器件意义

快速熔融–淬火复位与较慢结晶置位支持数字、多级及模拟累积。

合理的分析顺序是

\[\text{材料与几何}\rightarrow\text{状态与势垒}\rightarrow\text{输运或翻转}\rightarrow\text{端口响应}.\]

限制与判据

电阻漂移、热串扰、元素偏析及写入能量限制缩放和模拟精度。 当几何、温度、偏压流程、接触方式或器件间统计会影响结论时，可信实验必须完整报告这些条件。
