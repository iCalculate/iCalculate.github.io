Physical Picture

In graphene-like systems two sublattices produce a low-energy pseudospin Hamiltonian with approximately linear dispersion and chiral wavefunctions. The useful state variables and conserved quantities must be identified before selecting a transport model. In a real device the intrinsic material response is coupled self-consistently to electrostatics, contacts, disorder and the external circuit.

Deep Derivation and Physical Interpretation

Low-energy Hamiltonian and pseudospin

Nearest-neighbour hopping on a honeycomb lattice gives two bands that touch at inequivalent \(K\) and \(K'\) points. Expanding the tight-binding Hamiltonian around valley \(\tau=\pm1\) gives

\[H_\tau=\hbar v_F(\tau\sigma_x k_x+\sigma_y k_y)+\Delta\sigma_z,\]
\[E_\pm(k)=\pm\sqrt{(\hbar v_Fk)^2+\Delta^2}.\]

The Pauli matrices act on sublattice pseudospin, not real spin. For \(\Delta=0\), pseudospin aligns with momentum in the conduction band and winds once around the Dirac point, producing Berry phase \(\pi\) and suppressing exact backscattering from smooth scalar disorder.

Density of states and electrostatics

Counting states with spin and valley degeneracy \(g=4\) gives

\[D(E)=\frac{g|E|}{2\pi(\hbar v_F)^2},\qquad n=\frac{gE_F^2}{4\pi(\hbar v_F)^2}\operatorname{sgn}(E_F).\]

Thus the density of states vanishes linearly at neutrality and the quantum capacitance becomes small. This is the same spectral property that can act as an energy filter in a cold-source contact, but charge puddles with scale \(\delta E\) round the ideal Dirac point.

Physical picture and limits

The carrier is not literally massless in vacuum; “massless Dirac” means that its two-band envelope equation is linear in momentum and its inertial response is controlled by \(v_F\). A gap \(2|\Delta|\) breaks sublattice symmetry and rotates pseudospin out of plane. Digital switching then trades the desirable chirality for a gap, while contacts, edges and intervalley disorder determine whether the continuum Dirac model remains valid.

Minimal Model

\[H=ħv_F(σ_x k_x+σ_y k_y), E=±ħv_F\]

This expression is a compact organizing relation, not a universal fitting law. Its assumptions, dimensionality and boundary conditions must be checked before quantitative use.

Device Consequences

Dirac dispersion enables ambipolar gating, electron optics, Klein tunneling and sharply energy-dependent source density of states.

The correct comparison therefore follows the causal chain

\[\text{material and geometry}\rightarrow\text{states and barriers}\rightarrow\text{transport or switching}\rightarrow\text{terminal response}.\]

Limits and Diagnostic Tests

Zero band gap complicates digital switching; contacts, puddles and symmetry-breaking gaps control real devices. A credible experiment should report geometry, temperature, bias protocol, contact configuration and device-to-device statistics whenever they can change the interpretation.

深入推导与物理解释

低能哈密顿量与赝自旋

蜂窝晶格的最近邻跃迁产生在 \(K\) 与 \(K'\) 两个不等价点相接的能带。在谷指标 \(\tau=\pm1\) 附近展开紧束缚哈密顿量：

\[H_\tau=\hbar v_F(\tau\sigma_x k_x+\sigma_y k_y)+\Delta\sigma_z,\]
\[E_\pm(k)=\pm\sqrt{(\hbar v_Fk)^2+\Delta^2}.\]

这里 Pauli 矩阵作用在亚晶格赝自旋而不是真实自旋。当 \(\Delta=0\) 时，导带赝自旋与动量锁定，并围绕 Dirac 点旋转一周，产生 \(\pi\) Berry 相位；平滑标量无序因此难以造成严格的 \(180^\circ\) 背散射。

态密度与静电学

计入自旋和谷简并 \(g=4\) 后

\[D(E)=\frac{g|E|}{2\pi(\hbar v_F)^2},\qquad n=\frac{gE_F^2}{4\pi(\hbar v_F)^2}\operatorname{sgn}(E_F).\]

态密度在电中性点线性趋零，因此量子电容很小。这一谱学性质也能在冷源接触中充当能量过滤器，但实际电荷 puddle 的能标 \(\delta E\) 会钝化理想 Dirac 点。

物理图像与适用边界

“无质量 Dirac”并不是说电子在真空中没有静质量，而是指两能带包络方程对动量线性，惯性响应由 \(v_F\) 控制。质量项 \(\Delta\) 破坏亚晶格对称性，使赝自旋转出平面并打开 \(2|\Delta|\) 能隙。数字开关因而要在手性与带隙之间权衡，接触、边缘和谷间无序则决定连续 Dirac 模型是否仍成立。

最小模型

\[H=ħv_F(σ_x k_x+σ_y k_y), E=±ħv_F\]

这个式子用于组织物理关系，而不是在任何条件下都成立的拟合公式。定量使用前必须检查其近似、维度和边界条件。

器件意义

狄拉克色散支持双极性栅控、电子光学、克莱因隧穿以及强能量依赖的源态密度。

合理的分析顺序是

\[\text{材料与几何}\rightarrow\text{状态与势垒}\rightarrow\text{输运或翻转}\rightarrow\text{端口响应}.\]

限制与判据

零带隙不利于数字开关，实际性能受接触、电荷 puddle 和对称性破缺带隙控制。 当几何、温度、偏压流程、接触方式或器件间统计会影响结论时，可信实验必须完整报告这些条件。
