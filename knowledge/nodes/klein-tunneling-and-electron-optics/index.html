Physical Picture

Chiral Dirac carriers can transmit perfectly at normal incidence through a smooth electrostatic barrier because pseudospin matching suppresses backscattering. The useful state variables and conserved quantities must be identified before selecting a transport model. In a real device the intrinsic material response is coupled self-consistently to electrostatics, contacts, disorder and the external circuit.

Deep Derivation and Physical Interpretation

Angular transmission from pseudospin conservation

Across a graphene \(p\)–\(n\) junction, transverse momentum is conserved, \(k_y=k\sin\theta\). For a smooth linear potential, each \(k_y\) channel maps onto a Landau–Zener crossing between electron and hole branches, giving

\[T(\theta)\approx\exp[-\pi k_Fd\sin^2\theta].\]

Normal incidence has \(T(0)=1\) because an electrostatic scalar potential cannot flip pseudospin. Increasing junction width \(d\) suppresses oblique trajectories and turns the junction into a collimator. For an abrupt junction, spinor matching rather than the WKB/Landau–Zener form must be used.

Negative refraction

Conservation of \(k_y\) gives an electronic Snell law

\[k_n\sin\theta_n=-k_p\sin\theta_p,\]

where the minus sign reflects opposite group velocity and momentum in the hole band. Equal densities yield refractive index \(-1\) and an ideal Veselago focus. The observed signal is an angular integral of \(T(\theta)\) multiplied by injector and collector acceptance, not a direct image of one ray.

Discriminating electron optics

Focusing peaks should move with magnetic field and carrier density according to cyclotron radius \(r_c=\hbar k_F/(eB)\), reverse across carrier polarity, and disappear as \(L/\lambda_m\) or \(L/L_\phi\) grows. Similar nonlocal peaks from current spreading, edge conduction or contact geometry must be excluded with reciprocal configurations and electrostatic simulations.

Minimal Model

\[T(θ)≈exp[-πk_F d sin²θ]\]

This expression is a compact organizing relation, not a universal fitting law. Its assumptions, dimensionality and boundary conditions must be checked before quantitative use.

Device Consequences

Graphene p–n junctions can collimate, refract and focus electron beams, forming solid-state analogues of optical elements.

The correct comparison therefore follows the causal chain

\[\text{material and geometry}\rightarrow\text{states and barriers}\rightarrow\text{transport or switching}\rightarrow\text{terminal response}.\]

Limits and Diagnostic Tests

Disorder, edge roughness, finite temperature and intervalley scattering wash out angular selectivity. A credible experiment should report geometry, temperature, bias protocol, contact configuration and device-to-device statistics whenever they can change the interpretation.

深入推导与物理解释

从赝自旋守恒得到角分辨透射

石墨烯 \(p\)–\(n\) 结保持横向动量 \(k_y=k\sin\theta\)。对平滑线性势，每个 \(k_y\) 通道等价于电子支与空穴支之间的 Landau–Zener 穿越，因此

\[T(\theta)\approx\exp[-\pi k_Fd\sin^2\theta].\]

正入射满足 \(T(0)=1\)，因为静电标量势不能翻转赝自旋。增大结宽 \(d\) 会压制斜入射轨迹，使结成为准直器。若结非常陡峭，则必须直接匹配自旋量波函数，不能继续使用 WKB/Landau–Zener 形式。

负折射

由 \(k_y\) 守恒得到电子版 Snell 定律

\[k_n\sin\theta_n=-k_p\sin\theta_p.\]

负号来自空穴带中群速度与动量方向相反。两侧密度相等时折射率为 \(-1\)，理想情况下形成 Veselago 焦点。实验信号实际上是 \(T(\theta)\) 与注入器、收集器角接受度的积分，并不是单条“光线”的直接成像。

如何判别电子光学

聚焦峰应按回旋半径 \(r_c=\hbar k_F/(eB)\) 随磁场和载流子密度移动，在载流子极性翻转时反向，并随 \(L/\lambda_m\) 或 \(L/L_\phi\) 增大而消失。还需通过互易测量与静电模拟排除电流扩散、边缘导电或接触几何产生的类似非局域峰。

最小模型

\[T(θ)≈exp[-πk_F d sin²θ]\]

这个式子用于组织物理关系，而不是在任何条件下都成立的拟合公式。定量使用前必须检查其近似、维度和边界条件。

器件意义

石墨烯 p–n 结可以准直、折射和聚焦电子束，形成固态电子光学元件。

合理的分析顺序是

\[\text{材料与几何}\rightarrow\text{状态与势垒}\rightarrow\text{输运或翻转}\rightarrow\text{端口响应}.\]

限制与判据

无序、边缘粗糙、有限温度和谷间散射会削弱角度选择性。 当几何、温度、偏压流程、接触方式或器件间统计会影响结论时，可信实验必须完整报告这些条件。
