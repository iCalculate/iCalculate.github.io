Physical Picture

Separate 2D electrodes and barriers create spectrally and momentum-selective current, with gates tuning alignment rather than only barrier height. The useful state variables and conserved quantities must be identified before selecting a transport model. In a real device the intrinsic material response is coupled self-consistently to electrostatics, contacts, disorder and the external circuit.

Deep Derivation and Physical Interpretation

Resonant level and linewidth

For a discrete level \(E_0\) coupled to source and drain with broadenings \(\Gamma_S,\Gamma_D\), transmission is

\[T(E)=\frac{\Gamma_S\Gamma_D}{(E-E_0)^2+(\Gamma_S+\Gamma_D)^2/4}.\]

The peak height reflects coupling symmetry and linewidth reflects lifetime plus disorder. In 2D–2D tunnelling, momentum conservation adds a second resonance condition and can sharpen or suppress current.

NDR circuit stability

Inside the negative differential region, a device combined with load resistance \(R_L\) is stable only where the total load-line slope is positive. Parasitic capacitance and inductance can convert NDR into oscillation; electrostatic feedback can create bistability. The measured I–V is therefore a property of device plus external circuit.

Useful figures of merit

Report peak current density, peak-to-valley ratio, voltage span, linewidth, temperature and frequency response. Separate intrinsic resonance from series-resistance roll-off and self-heating with pulsed measurements and gate-tunable spectral alignment.

Minimal Model

\[Resonance occurs when occupied source and empty drain spectral functions overlap\]

This expression is a compact organizing relation, not a universal fitting law. Its assumptions, dimensionality and boundary conditions must be checked before quantitative use.

Device Consequences

Devices include NDR diodes, oscillators, spectrometers and transverse tunneling transistors.

The correct comparison therefore follows the causal chain

\[\text{material and geometry}\rightarrow\text{states and barriers}\rightarrow\text{transport or switching}\rightarrow\text{terminal response}.\]

Limits and Diagnostic Tests

Broadening, phonon assistance and electrostatic feedback can remove sharp resonance or produce bistability. A credible experiment should report geometry, temperature, bias protocol, contact configuration and device-to-device statistics whenever they can change the interpretation.

深入推导与物理解释

共振能级与线宽

离散能级 \(E_0\) 与源漏耦合展宽分别为 \(\Gamma_S,\Gamma_D\) 时，透射率为

\[T(E)=\frac{\Gamma_S\Gamma_D}{(E-E_0)^2+(\Gamma_S+\Gamma_D)^2/4}.\]

峰高反映耦合对称性，线宽反映寿命与无序。二维—二维隧穿还增加动量守恒条件，可进一步锐化或压制电流。

NDR 电路稳定性

在负微分区，器件与负载电阻 \(R_L\) 组合后只有总负载线斜率为正的工作点稳定。寄生电容、电感可把 NDR 转为振荡，静电反馈又可产生双稳态，因此实测 I–V 属于器件与外电路共同性质。

有用指标

应报告峰值电流密度、峰谷比、电压跨度、线宽、温度与频率响应。通过脉冲测量和栅控谱对准，把本征共振与串联电阻滚降、自热区分开。

最小模型

\[Resonance occurs when occupied source and empty drain spectral functions overlap\]

这个式子用于组织物理关系，而不是在任何条件下都成立的拟合公式。定量使用前必须检查其近似、维度和边界条件。

器件意义

器件包括 NDR 二极管、振荡器、能谱仪和横向隧穿晶体管。

合理的分析顺序是

\[\text{材料与几何}\rightarrow\text{状态与势垒}\rightarrow\text{输运或翻转}\rightarrow\text{端口响应}.\]

限制与判据

展宽、声子辅助及静电反馈可能抹去尖锐共振或产生双稳态。 当几何、温度、偏压流程、接触方式或器件间统计会影响结论时，可信实验必须完整报告这些条件。
