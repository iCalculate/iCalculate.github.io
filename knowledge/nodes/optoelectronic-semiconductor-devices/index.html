Optoelectronic Devices: LEDs, Photodiodes, and Solar Cells光电子器件：LED、光电二极管与太阳电池

A unified account of light emission, detection, and photovoltaics through absorption, generation–recombination, junction separation, and quasi-Fermi-level splitting.以光吸收、载流子产生复合、结区分离和准费米能级分裂统一理解发光、探测与光伏器件。

Core idea: Optoelectronic devices revolve around inverse processes: photon absorption generates electron–hole pairs, while electron–hole recombination emits photons. Junction fields separate carriers, and quasi-Fermi-level splitting measures the nonequilibrium drive.

Optical Absorption

For \(h\nu\ge E_g\), interband absorption can generate carriers. In a uniform absorber,

\[\Phi(x)=\Phi_0e^{-\alpha x},\qquad G(x)=\alpha\Phi(x).\]

Direct-gap materials allow radiative transitions without a phonon and are efficient emitters. Indirect-gap silicon requires phonon participation and is usually better for detection and photovoltaics than LEDs.

LEDs and Semiconductor Lasers

A forward-biased junction injects electrons and holes into an active region, with radiative rate \(R_{rad}=Bnp\). Internal efficiency is the radiative fraction of total recombination; extraction adds reflection, absorption, and electrode losses. A laser additionally requires stimulated-emission gain above cavity loss and population inversion. Quantum wells improve carrier and optical confinement.

Photodiodes

Reverse bias widens the depletion region and strengthens collection field. Carriers generated inside it are collected rapidly by drift; those generated in neutral material must diffuse to the junction. Ideal responsivity is

\[\mathcal R=\eta\frac{q}{h\nu}.\]

Figure 1 | Photocurrent shifts the diode I–V curve; its intersection with the load line sets the operating point. Д.Ильин, CC0; source.

An avalanche photodiode adds impact-ionization gain but also excess noise, high bias, and avalanche build-up time.

Solar Cells

At zero external reverse bias, the built-in field separates photocarriers. A simple illuminated-diode model is

\[J(V)=J_0(e^{qV/nkT}-1)-J_{ph}.\]

Short-circuit current follows absorption and collection; open-circuit voltage depends logarithmically on \(J_{ph}/J_0\). Band gap trades voltage against spectral absorption, while recombination and series/shunt resistance reduce fill factor.

Detection Limits

Shot, thermal, generation–recombination, and trap noise set NEP and detectivity. Response time is limited by the largest of transit, diffusion, and RC times. The measurement chain connects to photon-counting instrumentation.

核心结论：光电子器件围绕同一对逆过程展开：光子吸收产生电子–空穴对，电子–空穴复合发射光子。PN 结电场负责分离载流子，准费米能级分裂描述非平衡驱动力。

1. 光吸收

光子满足 \(h\nu\ge E_g\) 时可产生带间跃迁。光强在均匀材料中近似遵循 Beer–Lambert 定律

\[\Phi(x)=\Phi_0e^{-\alpha x},\qquad G(x)=\alpha\Phi(x).\]

直接带隙材料可在近乎相同晶格动量下完成跃迁，辐射复合效率高；Si 等间接带隙材料还需声子参与，因此更适合探测和光伏而非高效 LED。

2. LED与半导体激光器

正向偏置 PN 结向有源区注入电子和空穴，辐射复合率近似 \(R_{rad}=Bnp\)。内量子效率取决于辐射复合占总复合的比例；外量子效率还受全反射、吸收和电极遮挡限制。

激光器进一步要求受激辐射增益超过腔损耗，并在有源区建立粒子数反转。量子阱通过限制载流子和光场降低阈值；异质结同时提供载流子与光学限制。

3. 光电二极管

反偏扩大耗尽区并增强电场。耗尽区内产生的载流子主要靠漂移快速收集，准中性区内产生的载流子必须先扩散到结区，收集概率受扩散长度限制。理想响应度

\[\mathcal R=\eta\frac{q}{h\nu}\]

由量子效率 \(\eta\) 决定。

图 1｜光生电流使二极管 I–V 曲线向下平移；与负载线交点决定工作点。原图：Д.Ильин，CC0；文件页。

APD 在高反偏下用碰撞电离获得内部增益，但增益伴随额外噪声、较高偏压和雪崩建立时间。

4. 太阳电池

太阳电池在无外加反偏时利用内建场分离光生载流子。简化电流关系

\[J(V)=J_0\left(e^{qV/nkT}-1\right)-J_{ph}.\]

短路电流由吸收与收集决定；开路电压满足 \(J(V_{oc})=0\)，与 \(J_{ph}/J_0\) 的对数相关。输出功率还取决于填充因子、串并联电阻和复合。带隙存在双重权衡：较宽带隙提高电压但损失低能光子，较窄带隙吸收更多却增加热化损失与暗电流。

5. 探测极限

暗电流的散粒噪声、热噪声、产生复合噪声和低频陷阱噪声共同决定 NEP 与探测率。器件响应速度则由渡越时间、扩散时间和 RC 常数中的最大者限制。实际光子计数链还连接光子计数仪器与数据采集。
