Physical Picture

When the electronic density of states is small, adding charge must move the chemical potential; this finite electronic compressibility appears as a capacitance in series with the geometrical gate capacitance. The useful state variables and conserved quantities must be identified before selecting a transport model. In a real device the intrinsic material response is coupled self-consistently to electrostatics, contacts, disorder and the external circuit.

Deep Derivation and Physical Interpretation

Capacitance as thermodynamic compressibility

Adding sheet charge \(dQ=q,dn\) shifts both the electrostatic potential and chemical potential. The electronic contribution is

\[C_Q\equiv\frac{dQ}{d(\mu/q)}=q^2\frac{dn}{d\mu}.\]

For a disorder-free parabolic 2D band, \(D=g m^*/(2\pi\hbar^2)\) and \(C_Q=q^2D\) is constant once the band is occupied. For a Dirac band, \(C_Q\propto|E_F|\) and ideally vanishes at neutrality. Series voltage division gives

\[dV_G=\frac{dQ}{C_{\rm ox}}+\frac{dQ}{C_Q},\qquad C_G^{-1}=C_{\rm ox}^{-1}+C_Q^{-1}.\]

Small \(C_Q\) therefore reduces charge induced per volt, but it also makes capacitance a direct probe of density of states and interaction-driven compressibility.

Non-ideal equivalent circuit

Interface traps contribute a frequency-dependent \(C_{it}(\omega)\), access resistance creates a complex admittance, and geometrical fringing adds parallel capacitance. Negative measured compressibility can be physical when exchange lowers \(d\mu/dn\), but an apparent negative capacitance can also come from phase delay or incorrect de-embedding.

Extraction protocol

Measure amplitude and phase across frequency and temperature; independently estimate \(C_{\rm ox}\); de-embed pads and series resistance; integrate the corrected capacitance to recover \(n(V_G)\). A credible \(C_Q\) feature should track a band edge or Landau level in both capacitance and an independent spectroscopic/transport observable.

Minimal Model

\[C_Q=q²D(E_F), 1/C_G=1/C_ox+1/C_Q\]

This expression is a compact organizing relation, not a universal fitting law. Its assumptions, dimensionality and boundary conditions must be checked before quantitative use.

Device Consequences

Quantum capacitance affects gate efficiency, RF response, capacitance spectroscopy and cold-source electrostatics.

The correct comparison therefore follows the causal chain

\[\text{material and geometry}\rightarrow\text{states and barriers}\rightarrow\text{transport or switching}\rightarrow\text{terminal response}.\]

Limits and Diagnostic Tests

Extracted capacitance also contains traps, parasitics and access resistance; not every small measured capacitance is C_Q. A credible experiment should report geometry, temperature, bias protocol, contact configuration and device-to-device statistics whenever they can change the interpretation.

深入推导与物理解释

电容就是热力学可压缩性

加入面电荷 \(dQ=q,dn\) 时，静电势和化学势都会移动。电子系统本身的贡献定义为

\[C_Q\equiv\frac{dQ}{d(\mu/q)}=q^2\frac{dn}{d\mu}.\]

无无序抛物型二维能带具有 \(D=g m^*/(2\pi\hbar^2)\)，一旦能带被占据便有常数量子电容 \(C_Q=q^2D\)；Dirac 能带则满足 \(C_Q\propto|E_F|\)，理想情况下在电中性点消失。串联分压为

\[dV_G=\frac{dQ}{C_{\rm ox}}+\frac{dQ}{C_Q},\qquad C_G^{-1}=C_{\rm ox}^{-1}+C_Q^{-1}.\]

因此小 \(C_Q\) 会降低单位栅压诱导的电荷，却也使电容直接成为态密度和相互作用可压缩性的探针。

非理想等效电路

界面陷阱带来频率相关的 \(C_{it}(\omega)\)，接入电阻使导纳成为复数，边缘场又引入并联几何电容。交换作用降低 \(d\mu/dn\) 时，负可压缩性可以是真实多体效应；但相位延迟或错误去嵌也会制造表观负电容。

提取流程

应跨频率和温度同时测量幅值与相位，独立估计 \(C_{\rm ox}\)，去除焊盘与串联电阻，再积分校正电容恢复 \(n(V_G)\)。可信的 \(C_Q\) 特征应在电容谱中追踪到能带边或 Landau 能级，并与独立谱学或输运观测一致。

最小模型

\[C_Q=q²D(E_F), 1/C_G=1/C_ox+1/C_Q\]

这个式子用于组织物理关系，而不是在任何条件下都成立的拟合公式。定量使用前必须检查其近似、维度和边界条件。

器件意义

量子电容影响栅控效率、射频响应、电容谱和冷源器件静电学。

合理的分析顺序是

\[\text{材料与几何}\rightarrow\text{状态与势垒}\rightarrow\text{输运或翻转}\rightarrow\text{端口响应}.\]

限制与判据

测得电容还包含陷阱、寄生及接入电阻，不能把所有小电容都归因于量子电容。 当几何、温度、偏压流程、接触方式或器件间统计会影响结论时，可信实验必须完整报告这些条件。
