Physical Picture

Steep-slope concepts try to reduce switching voltage by changing injection statistics, adding internal voltage gain or reshaping the source density of states. The useful state variables and conserved quantities must be identified before selecting a transport model. In a real device the intrinsic material response is coupled self-consistently to electrostatics, contacts, disorder and the external circuit.

Deep Derivation and Physical Interpretation

Three distinct routes to a steep transfer curve

A transfer slope is the product of electrostatic gate leverage and the energy selectivity of injection. Writing current schematically as \(I=I(\psi_B)\),

\[\frac{d\log_{10}I}{dV_G}= \frac{d\log_{10}I}{d\psi_B}\frac{d\psi_B}{dV_G}.\]

Conventional MOSFETs are limited by a thermal injection factor and passive \(d\psi_B/dV_G\le1\). TFETs reshape the first factor through a tunnelling window; cold-source FETs suppress the high-energy spectral tail; NCFETs target internal voltage amplification in the second factor. Combining data from these mechanisms under the label “sub-60” hides their different speed, capacitance and hysteresis penalties.

System-level comparison

At fixed supply, intrinsic delay and switching energy scale as

\[\tau\sim\frac{C_{\rm load}V_{DD}}{I_{\rm on}},\qquad E_{\rm sw}\sim C_{\rm load}V_{DD}^2.\]

A steep slope helps only if it permits lower \(V_{DD}\) without losing \(I_{\rm on}\) or increasing capacitance. Benchmark the full current range, output conductance, parasitic capacitance, hysteresis, variability and temperature—not the best derivative from a smoothed curve.

Minimal Model

\[SS^{-1}=dlog10(I_D)/dV_G\]

This expression is a compact organizing relation, not a universal fitting law. Its assumptions, dimensionality and boundary conditions must be checked before quantitative use.

Device Consequences

This hub compares TFET, NCFET and cold-source routes using a common current–capacitance–delay framework.

The correct comparison therefore follows the causal chain

\[\text{material and geometry}\rightarrow\text{states and barriers}\rightarrow\text{transport or switching}\rightarrow\text{terminal response}.\]

Limits and Diagnostic Tests

Mechanisms should be identified from temperature, hysteresis, frequency and current scaling rather than SS alone. A credible experiment should report geometry, temperature, bias protocol, contact configuration and device-to-device statistics whenever they can change the interpretation.

深入推导与物理解释

实现陡峭转移曲线的三条不同路线

转移斜率是静电栅控效率与注入能量选择性的乘积。把电流写成 \(I=I(\psi_B)\)：

\[\frac{d\log_{10}I}{dV_G}= \frac{d\log_{10}I}{d\psi_B}\frac{d\psi_B}{dV_G}.\]

传统 MOSFET 同时受热注入因子与被动 \(d\psi_B/dV_G\le1\) 限制。TFET 通过隧穿窗口改变第一项，冷源 FET 通过源端谱截断高能尾，NCFET 则在第二项中寻求内部电压放大。若都简化成“亚 60”，就会掩盖它们在速度、电容和滞回上的不同代价。

系统级比较

固定电源下，本征延迟与开关能近似为

\[\tau\sim\frac{C_{\rm load}V_{DD}}{I_{\rm on}},\qquad E_{\rm sw}\sim C_{\rm load}V_{DD}^2.\]

只有在降低 \(V_{DD}\) 的同时不牺牲 \(I_{\rm on}\)、不增加电容时，陡峭斜率才有系统收益。应比较完整电流区间、输出电导、寄生电容、滞回、变异和温度，而不是从平滑曲线中挑选最小导数。

最小模型

\[SS^{-1}=dlog10(I_D)/dV_G\]

这个式子用于组织物理关系，而不是在任何条件下都成立的拟合公式。定量使用前必须检查其近似、维度和边界条件。

器件意义

本节点用统一的电流–电容–延迟框架比较 TFET、NCFET 和冷源路线。

合理的分析顺序是

\[\text{材料与几何}\rightarrow\text{状态与势垒}\rightarrow\text{输运或翻转}\rightarrow\text{端口响应}.\]

限制与判据

不能只凭 SS 判断机制，应同时分析温度、滞回、频率和电流标度。 当几何、温度、偏压流程、接触方式或器件间统计会影响结论时，可信实验必须完整报告这些条件。
