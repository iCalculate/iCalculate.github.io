Physical Picture

A cold-source FET suppresses the high-energy tail of injected carriers through a sharply decreasing source density of states or an energy-selective heterojunction. The useful state variables and conserved quantities must be identified before selecting a transport model. In a real device the intrinsic material response is coupled self-consistently to electrostatics, contacts, disorder and the external circuit.

Deep Derivation and Physical Interpretation

Spectral filtering of the injected distribution

The injected flux is proportional not only to the Fermi function but to source modes and transmission:

\[I\propto\int D_S(E)v_x(E)T(E,\psi_B)[f_S-f_D]dE.\]

In a thermionic MOSFET, modes above the barrier sample an approximately exponential Fermi tail. A cold source introduces a rapidly falling \(D_S(E)v_xT\) above a cutoff, so moving the barrier removes current faster than the Boltzmann factor alone. A Dirac point, narrow band or engineered heterojunction can supply the filter.

Effective temperature is energy dependent

Define a spectral slope

\[\frac{1}{k_BT_{\rm eff}(E)}=-\frac{d}{dE}\ln[D_S(E)v_x(E)f(E)].\]

\(T_{\rm eff}<T\) over the active window can yield subthermal swing, but only while the drain does not inject unfiltered modes and the gate maintains favorable quantum-capacitance division.

How filtering is destroyed

Electron–electron or phonon scattering can re-thermalize carriers and refill the nominally empty tail; disorder broadens sharp band edges; contact hybridization changes the source DOS. Evidence should correlate the steep-current window with an independently measured spectral cutoff, show predicted temperature evolution, and rule out contact-barrier modulation or transient trapping.

Minimal Model

\[I∝∫D_S(E)T(E)[f_S-f_D]dE\]

This expression is a compact organizing relation, not a universal fitting law. Its assumptions, dimensionality and boundary conditions must be checked before quantitative use.

Device Consequences

Dirac points, narrow bands and vdW band alignment are candidate filters that can produce subthermal injection without a ferroelectric gate.

The correct comparison therefore follows the causal chain

\[\text{material and geometry}\rightarrow\text{states and barriers}\rightarrow\text{transport or switching}\rightarrow\text{terminal response}.\]

Limits and Diagnostic Tests

Scattering and source thermalization refill the filtered tail; drain DOS and bias dependence must also be engineered. A credible experiment should report geometry, temperature, bias protocol, contact configuration and device-to-device statistics whenever they can change the interpretation.

深入推导与物理解释

注入分布的能谱过滤

注入通量不仅由费米函数决定，还包含源端模式与透射率：

\[I\propto\int D_S(E)v_x(E)T(E,\psi_B)[f_S-f_D]dE.\]

热发射 MOSFET 的势垒上方模式近似采样指数费米尾。冷源在截止能以上提供快速下降的 \(D_S(E)v_xT\)，因此移动势垒时，电流下降速度可快于单独 Boltzmann 因子。Dirac 点、窄带或人工异质结都可充当过滤器。

有效温度依赖能量

定义谱斜率

\[\frac{1}{k_BT_{\rm eff}(E)}=-\frac{d}{dE}\ln[D_S(E)v_x(E)f(E)].\]

若工作窗口内 \(T_{\rm eff}<T\)，便可能出现亚热摆幅；但前提是漏端不注入未过滤模式，同时栅极仍保持有利的量子电容分压。

过滤如何被破坏

电子—电子或声子散射会重新热化载流子并填充名义空尾；无序会展宽尖锐带边；接触杂化则改变源端 DOS。证据应把陡峭电流窗口与独立测得的谱截止对应起来，呈现预测的温度演化，并排除接触势垒调制和瞬态俘获。

最小模型

\[I∝∫D_S(E)T(E)[f_S-f_D]dE\]

这个式子用于组织物理关系，而不是在任何条件下都成立的拟合公式。定量使用前必须检查其近似、维度和边界条件。

器件意义

狄拉克点、窄能带及 vdW 能带对准可作为过滤器，在无需铁电栅的情况下产生亚热注入。

合理的分析顺序是

\[\text{材料与几何}\rightarrow\text{状态与势垒}\rightarrow\text{输运或翻转}\rightarrow\text{端口响应}.\]

限制与判据

散射和源区热化会重新填充被过滤的高能尾部，同时还需设计漏端态密度及偏压依赖。 当几何、温度、偏压流程、接触方式或器件间统计会影响结论时，可信实验必须完整报告这些条件。
