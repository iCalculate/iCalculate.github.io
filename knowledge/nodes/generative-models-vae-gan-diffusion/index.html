Generative Models: VAE, GAN and Diffusion生成模型：VAE、GAN 与扩散模型

Generative models learn a distribution or sampling transformation, with VAEs, GANs and diffusion models making different compromises among likelihood, latent structure and sample generation.生成模型学习概率分布或采样变换；VAE、GAN 与扩散模型在似然、潜在结构与样本生成之间做不同权衡。

Core idea: Generative models learn a distribution or sampling transformation, with VAEs, GANs and diffusion models making different compromises among likelihood, latent structure and sample generation.

Statistical and Computational Picture

VAEs optimize a variational likelihood bound, GANs train a generator against a discriminator, and diffusion reverses progressive noising through learned denoising scores.

A useful AI description separates the task distribution, data-generating process, model family, learning algorithm and deployed decision rule. Data, compute budget, feedback and tools are controls; parameters, representations, optimizer state, retrieved context and recurrent memory are internal state. A benchmark score is informative only when the route from sampling and training to inference and evaluation is explicit. This prevents leakage, prompt adaptation, retrieval quality or post-processing from being mislabelled as intrinsic model capability.

The relevant scales include sample size, parameter count, context length, effective rank, gradient noise, training FLOPs, inference tokens, memory traffic and latency. Their hierarchy determines whether approximation error, optimization error, estimation error or deployment shift dominates. The same architecture can therefore behave as an interpolator, calibrated predictor, memorizer or unstable agent under different data and inference protocols.

Deep Derivation and Algorithmic Interpretation

Governing relations

The first organizing objective or probabilistic relation is

\[\mathcal L_{VAE}=\mathbb E_q[\log p_\theta(x|z)]-D_{KL}(q_\phi(z|x)\|p(z))\]

A complementary generalization, scaling or inference relation is

\[\mathcal L_{diff}=\mathbb E_{t,x,\epsilon}\|\epsilon-\epsilon_\theta(x_t,t)\|^2\]

The VAE bound trades reconstruction against latent regularity. Denoising score matching learns how probability mass changes across noise levels, enabling iterative reverse sampling.

The variables in these equations must be tied to a concrete sampling protocol and unit of analysis. Training loss, held-out loss, task utility and human preference are not interchangeable. Report optimizer, data mixture, random seeds, decoding policy, context construction, tool permissions and compute accounting whenever they can change the measured result.

Causal and systems view

The auditable chain is

\[\text{data and objective}\rightarrow\text{learned representation}\rightarrow\text{inference or action}\rightarrow\text{evaluation and feedback}.\]

Following this chain separates an intrinsic mechanism or learned capability from losses introduced by transport, sampling, interfaces, readout or orchestration. It also enables consistency tests: parameters inferred at one stage should predict independent observations downstream without being silently refitted.

Evaluation, Diagnostics and Falsification

Evaluate sample fidelity, diversity, coverage, memorization, conditional control and domain-specific validity rather than one perceptual metric.

A convincing evaluation uses contamination-resistant splits, task-relevant baselines, uncertainty intervals and ablations that isolate data, model, retrieval, tools and inference budget. Robustness should be tested across seeds, paraphrases, subgroups and distribution shifts. For agents, the evaluated object is the complete stateful system, including environment, permissions, stopping rule and recovery behaviour.

System and Research Consequences

Generative models create media, molecular candidates, inverse designs, simulations and synthetic training data.

Deployment quality couples accuracy with calibration, latency, throughput, energy, privacy, maintainability and human oversight. A larger benchmark number is not automatically a better system. Scientific use further requires provenance, unit consistency, conservation constraints and a route for domain experts to inspect or reject generated hypotheses.

Limits, Failure Modes and Open Questions

Likelihood and visual quality can disagree, sampling is expensive, and plausible outputs can violate physics, identity or provenance.

Common failure modes include shortcut learning, dataset shift, feedback loops, hallucinated evidence, reward hacking, brittle tool use and hidden correlations between benchmark and training data. Frontier claims must distinguish demonstrated performance on a bounded protocol from general reasoning, autonomous reliability or social acceptability.

统计与计算图像

VAE 优化变分似然下界，GAN 让生成器与判别器对抗训练，扩散模型则学习逆转逐步加噪的去噪过程。

可靠的 AI 描述必须区分任务分布、数据生成过程、模型族、学习算法和部署决策规则。数据、算力预算、反馈与工具是控制量；参数、表征、优化器状态、检索上下文和循环记忆是内部状态。只有明确采样与训练如何通向推理和评估，基准分数才有意义，也才能避免把数据泄漏、提示适配、检索质量或后处理误称为模型本征能力。

相关尺度包括样本量、参数量、上下文长度、有效秩、梯度噪声、训练 FLOPs、推理 token、存储带宽和延迟。它们的层级决定逼近误差、优化误差、估计误差还是部署漂移占主导。同一架构在不同数据与推理协议下可能表现为插值器、校准预测器、记忆器或不稳定智能体。

深入推导与算法解释

控制方程

第一条组织目标或概率关系为

\[\mathcal L_{VAE}=\mathbb E_q[\log p_\theta(x|z)]-D_{KL}(q_\phi(z|x)\|p(z))\]

与之互补的泛化、缩放或推理关系为

\[\mathcal L_{diff}=\mathbb E_{t,x,\epsilon}\|\epsilon-\epsilon_\theta(x_t,t)\|^2\]

VAE 下界在重建与潜空间正则间权衡；去噪分数匹配学习概率质量随噪声层级的变化，从而支持迭代逆向采样。

这些方程中的变量必须对应明确的采样协议和分析单位。训练损失、留出损失、任务效用与人类偏好不可互换；凡优化器、数据混合、随机种子、解码策略、上下文构造、工具权限和算力核算能够改变结果，都应被报告。

因果链与系统视角

可审计的因果链为

\[\text{数据与目标}\rightarrow\text{学习到的表征}\rightarrow\text{推理或行动}\rightarrow\text{评估与反馈}.\]

沿这条链可以把本征机理或学习能力与传质、采样、界面、读出或编排引入的损失区分开，也能建立一致性检验：在一个环节推断出的参数，应在不暗中重新拟合的前提下预测下游独立观测。

评估、诊断与证伪

应评估样本保真度、多样性、覆盖、记忆、条件控制及领域有效性，而非单一感知指标。

有说服力的评估需要抗污染划分、任务相关基线、不确定区间，以及分别隔离数据、模型、检索、工具和推理预算的消融。稳健性还应跨随机种子、改写、子群体和分布漂移检验。对智能体而言，被评估对象是包含环境、权限、停止规则和恢复行为的完整有状态系统。

系统与科研意义

生成模型可创建媒体、分子候选、逆向设计、模拟与合成训练数据。

部署质量把准确率与校准、延迟、吞吐、能耗、隐私、可维护性和人工监督耦合在一起，更高的基准数字并不自动意味着更好的系统。科研使用还要求来源可追溯、单位一致、守恒约束明确，并允许领域专家检查或否决生成假设。

限制、失效模式与开放问题

似然与视觉质量可能不一致，采样成本高，可信外观的输出也可能违反物理、身份或来源约束。

常见失效模式包括捷径学习、数据集漂移、反馈回路、虚构证据、奖励投机、脆弱工具调用，以及基准与训练数据之间的隐藏相关。前沿结论必须区分受限协议中的性能演示、通用推理能力、自主可靠性与社会可接受性。
