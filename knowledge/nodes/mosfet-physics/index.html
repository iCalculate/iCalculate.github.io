The MOSFET: Gate-Controlled Charge and Channel TransportMOSFET：栅控电荷与沟道输运

Combining vertical MOS electrostatics with lateral source–drain transport to derive long-channel I–V, transconductance, saturation, and subthreshold behavior.将MOS电容的垂直静电与源漏方向的横向输运结合，推导长沟道I-V、跨导、饱和和亚阈值行为。

Core idea: A MOSFET separates into two coupled problems: MOS electrostatics sets the local inversion charge, and the source–drain field transports that charge laterally. The square law is only the result of several long-channel approximations.

Figure 1 | Gate, source, drain, oxide, and substrate of a planar MOSFET. Brews ohare / BentSm, CC BY-SA 3.0; source.

From MOS Capacitor to Channel

For an ideal long-channel nMOS in strong inversion,

\[Q_i(y)=-C_{ox}[V_{GS}-V_T-V(y)].\]

The gradual-channel approximation treats each lateral position as a local MOS capacitor. With

\[I_D=-W\mu_nQ_i(y)\frac{dV}{dy},\]

integration gives the linear-region current

\[I_D=\mu_nC_{ox}\frac WL\left[(V_{GS}-V_T)V_{DS}-\frac{V_{DS}^2}{2}\right].\]

Saturation Does Not Stop Carrier Motion

At \(V_{DS}=V_{GS}-V_T\equiv V_{OV}\), the ideal drain-end inversion charge approaches zero and the channel pinches off:

\[I_{D,sat}=\frac12\mu_nC_{ox}\frac WL(V_{GS}-V_T)^2.\]

Carriers still traverse the high-field pinch-off region. “Saturation” means reduced first-order sensitivity to \(V_{DS}\). Channel-length modulation gives

\[I_D\approx I_{D,sat}(1+\lambda V_{DS}).\]

Small-Signal Parameters

In square-law saturation,

\[g_m=\mu_nC_{ox}\frac WL V_{OV}=\frac{2I_D}{V_{OV}},\qquad r_o\approx\frac1{\lambda I_D}.\]

Intrinsic voltage gain is approximately \(g_mr_o\), so increasing current does not automatically increase voltage gain.

Subthreshold Is Diffusion-Controlled

In weak inversion,

\[I_D\propto\exp\left(\frac{V_{GS}-V_T}{nV_T}\right),\]

and

\[S=(\ln10)\frac{kT}{q}\left(1+\frac{C_d+C_{it}}{C_{ox}}\right).\]

The room-temperature classical limit near \(60\ \mathrm{mV/dec}\) comes from Boltzmann statistics and gate coupling, not from silicon alone.

Why Modern Devices Depart from the Square Law

Vertical-field mobility reduction, velocity saturation, DIBL, series/contact resistance, quantum confinement, quantum capacitance, ballistic injection, gate leakage, junction leakage, and band-to-band tunneling all change the ideal result. See Breakdown and Scaling, MOSFET Quantum Transport Simulation, and From Capacitors to FETs.

Charge-sheet current and its breakdown

Under gradual-channel and strong-inversion approximations,

\[Q_i(x)\approx-C_{ox}[V_{GS}-V_T-V(x)],\]

and integrating \(I=-W\mu Q_i,dV/dx\) gives

\[I_D=\mu C_{ox}\frac WL[(V_{GS}-V_T)V_{DS}-V_{DS}^2/2].\]

Pinch-off at \(V_{DS}=V_{GS}-V_T\) yields the square-law saturation expression, but modern short channels saturate mainly through velocity saturation and source injection. In weak inversion, current is diffusion controlled and exponential in surface potential. A compact device view must therefore connect subthreshold electrostatics, charge control, quasi-ballistic injection and parasitic resistance rather than extrapolate one square law across all regimes. Delay depends on total gate charge and available drive current, not mobility alone.

核心结论：MOSFET 可拆成两个耦合问题：栅压通过 MOS 静电决定每一点的沟道反型电荷，源漏电场驱动这些电荷沿沟道输运。长沟道平方律只是这一物理链在若干强近似下的结果。

1. 从 MOS 电容到沟道

对理想长沟道 nMOS，强反型下局部沟道片电荷近似

\[Q_i(y)=-C_{ox}[V_{GS}-V_T-V(y)].\]

缓变沟道近似要求横向场变化尺度远大于垂直耗尽尺度，使每个沟道位置都可视为局域 MOS 电容。漂移电流

\[I_D=-W\mu_n Q_i(y)\frac{dV}{dy}\]

沿沟道积分得到线性区

\[I_D=\mu_nC_{ox}\frac WL \left[(V_{GS}-V_T)V_{DS}-\frac{V_{DS}^2}{2}\right].\]

2. 饱和不是载流子停止运动

当 \(V_{DS}=V_{GS}-V_T\equiv V_{OV}\)，漏端反型电荷在理想模型中趋零并出现夹断：

\[I_{D,sat}=\frac12\mu_nC_{ox}\frac WL(V_{GS}-V_T)^2.\]

夹断后电子仍被高场扫入漏极；“饱和”指理想电流对 \(V_{DS}\) 的一阶敏感性变弱。实际输出电流仍因沟道长度调制而上升：

\[I_D\approx I_{D,sat}(1+\lambda V_{DS}).\]

3. 小信号参数

平方律饱和区

\[g_m=\frac{\partial I_D}{\partial V_{GS}} =\mu_nC_{ox}\frac WL V_{OV}=\frac{2I_D}{V_{OV}},\]

输出电阻 \(r_o\approx1/(\lambda I_D)\)。本征增益约为 \(g_mr_o\)，因此提高电流并不必然提高电压增益。

4. 亚阈值是扩散控制

当表面尚未强反型时，漏电流主要由弱反型载流子扩散产生：

\[I_D\propto\exp\left(\frac{V_{GS}-V_T}{nV_T}\right),\]

亚阈值摆幅

\[S=\frac{dV_G}{d\log_{10}I_D} =(\ln10)\frac{kT}{q}\left(1+\frac{C_d+C_{it}}{C_{ox}}\right).\]

室温经典极限约 \(60\ \mathrm{mV/dec}\)，来自玻尔兹曼统计与栅控效率，而不是某个硅材料常数。

5. 现代器件为何偏离平方律

  • 垂直场降低有效迁移率；
  • 横向高场导致速度饱和，\(I_{sat}\) 更接近与 \(V_{OV}\) 线性；
  • 漏致势垒降低（DIBL）使 \(V_T\) 随漏压下降；
  • 源漏串联电阻和接触势垒限制跨导；
  • 量子限域、量子电容和弹道注入改变电荷与电流关系；
  • 栅漏、结漏和带间隧穿形成关态电流。

详见击穿、缩放与器件非理想效应、MOSFET Quantum Transport Simulation以及From Capacitors to FETs。

电荷片电流及其失效

在渐变沟道和强反型近似下

\[Q_i(x)\approx-C_{ox}[V_{GS}-V_T-V(x)],\]

积分 \(I=-W\mu Q_i,dV/dx\) 得

\[I_D=\mu C_{ox}\frac WL[(V_{GS}-V_T)V_{DS}-V_{DS}^2/2].\]

\(V_{DS}=V_{GS}-V_T\) 时夹断并得到平方律饱和，但现代短沟道更多由速度饱和和源端注入限制。弱反型区电流由扩散控制并对表面势呈指数关系。因此完整器件图像必须连接亚阈值静电、电荷控制、准弹道注入与寄生电阻，而不能把一条平方律外推到所有区间。延迟取决于总栅电荷和可用驱动电流，并非只由迁移率决定。
