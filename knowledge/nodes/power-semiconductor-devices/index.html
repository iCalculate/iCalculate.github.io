Power Semiconductor Devices功率半导体器件

Power diodes, MOSFETs, IGBTs, and thyristors through the trade-offs among blocking voltage, conduction loss, switching loss, and heat removal.用耐压、导通损耗、开关损耗和热管理的权衡统一理解功率二极管、MOSFET、IGBT与晶闸管。

Power DevicesJunction DevicesDevice Physics
Read in阅读语言

Core idea: A power device needs a thick, lightly doped drift region to block high voltage, yet would prefer a thin, heavily doped region for low on-resistance. Unipolar devices mitigate this conflict through material critical field; bipolar devices use conductivity modulation but pay in stored charge and switching loss.

Drift Region: Blocking Versus Resistance

For a unipolar vertical device,

\[R_{on,sp}\sim\frac{W_D}{q\mu N_D},\]

and the ideal material trade-off scales roughly as

\[R_{on,sp}\propto\frac{V_B^2}{\varepsilon\mu E_c^3}.\]

The large critical fields of SiC and GaN therefore reduce drift resistance dramatically at a given blocking voltage.

Power MOSFET

Figure 1 | Many parallel vertical cells increase effective channel width. Cyril BUTTAY, CC BY-SA; source.

At low voltage, channel, accumulation, contact, and spreading resistances matter; at high voltage the drift region dominates. The body/drift PN junction forms an intrinsic body diode whose recovery can influence switching.

IGBT

Figure 2 | A p-type injector adds bipolar conductivity modulation beneath a MOS gate. Cyril BUTTAY; source and license.

Hole injection raises both carrier densities in the n\(^-\) drift region and lowers its resistance. Those carriers must recombine or be extracted at turn-off, producing tail current. IGBTs therefore suit higher voltage and moderate frequency; MOSFETs suit higher frequency and lower-to-medium voltage.

Power Diodes and Thyristors

A PIN diode uses a lightly doped layer for blocking and conductivity modulation for low forward drop, at the cost of reverse recovery. A pnpn thyristor contains regenerative feedback: once the equivalent transistor gains sustain one another, it latches on and is difficult to turn off from the gate.

Beyond the Die

Gate charge, Miller capacitance, diode recovery, stray inductance, and thermal impedance determine dynamic loss. Safe operating area is bounded by breakdown, local hot spots, secondary breakdown, and package cooling—not merely nameplate voltage and static \(R_{DS(on)}\).

核心结论：功率器件的根本矛盾是“关断时要用轻掺杂厚漂移区承受高压，导通时又希望它低电阻”。单极器件靠材料临界场缓解矛盾，双极器件靠少子注入调制漂移区电导，但会付出存储电荷和开关损耗。

1. 漂移区决定耐压和电阻

一维近似下，单极垂直器件的击穿电压随临界场与漂移区厚度增加；而导通比电阻

\[R_{on,sp}\sim\frac{W_D}{q\mu N_D}\]

随更厚、更轻掺杂的漂移区急剧上升。对理想同质结单极器件可得到近似材料极限

\[R_{on,sp}\propto\frac{V_B^2}{\varepsilon\mu E_c^3}.\]

这解释了宽禁带 SiC、GaN 的价值：高临界击穿场 \(E_c\) 可显著降低给定耐压下的漂移区电阻。

2. 功率MOSFET

图 1｜垂直功率 MOSFET 通过大量并联单元增加有效沟道宽度，电流垂直穿过漂移区。原图：Cyril BUTTAY，CC BY-SA；文件页。

低压时沟道、接触和积累区电阻重要；高压时漂移区主导。体区与漂移区形成寄生体二极管，适合某些换流场景，但其反向恢复与 SiC MOSFET 界面迁移率仍是设计重点。

3. IGBT

图 2｜IGBT 在 MOS 栅结构下加入 p 型注入层，使漂移区发生双极电导调制。原图：Cyril BUTTAY；文件页与许可。

IGBT 导通时空穴注入 n\(^-\) 漂移区，提高电子与空穴浓度并降低电阻；关断时这些存储载流子必须复合或被抽取，产生尾电流。它因此适合较高电压和中等开关频率，而功率 MOSFET 更适合高频与较低/中等电压。

4. 功率二极管与晶闸管

PIN 二极管以本征/轻掺杂层承受反压，正向时通过电导调制降低压降，但存在反向恢复。晶闸管的 pnpn 结构包含正反馈：两个等效晶体管的总电流增益达到自持条件后锁定导通，适合极高功率但关断控制困难。

5. 器件之外仍是器件物理

动态性能由栅电荷、Miller 电容、反向恢复、寄生电感和热阻共同决定。安全工作区同时受击穿、局部热点、二次击穿与封装散热限制；只比较额定电压和静态 \(R_{DS(on)}\) 会遗漏真实系统损耗。
