Physical Picture

Atomically sharp stacks of 2D magnets, semiconductors and insulators create spin-dependent tunneling or spin-filter barriers controlled by magnetic configuration and gates. The useful state variables and conserved quantities must be identified before selecting a transport model. In a real device the intrinsic material response is coupled self-consistently to electrostatics, contacts, disorder and the external circuit.

Deep Derivation and Physical Interpretation

Spin- and momentum-selective tunnelling

Conductance sums spin-resolved spectral overlap

\[G\propto\sum_{\sigma,\mathbf k}|t_{\sigma\mathbf k}|^2 D_{1\sigma}(\mathbf k,E_F)D_{2\sigma}(\mathbf k,E_F).\]

Parallel and antiparallel magnetic configurations change both spin availability and, in crystalline 2D stacks, momentum/valley matching. A magnetic semiconductor barrier can itself act as a spin filter through exchange-split barrier heights.

Gate control

Electrostatic gating moves band edges and Fermi surfaces, so TMR can change sign when different spin/valley bands enter the tunnelling window. This is richer than a fixed Jullière polarization but also makes contact doping and layer potential essential.

Proof and limitations

Correlate magnetoconductance steps with independent layer magnetization; map bias and gate dependence; distinguish spin filtering from ordinary barrier-height magnetoresistance. Low ordering temperatures, field-required switching, air degradation and twist variability must be included when comparing with conventional MTJs.

Minimal Model

\[G_P and G_AP depend on spin-resolved DOS and momentum matching\]

This expression is a compact organizing relation, not a universal fitting law. Its assumptions, dimensionality and boundary conditions must be checked before quantitative use.

Device Consequences

The devices connect vdW spintronics to MRAM, spin sensing and multifunctional p–n junctions.

The correct comparison therefore follows the causal chain

\[\text{material and geometry}\rightarrow\text{states and barriers}\rightarrow\text{transport or switching}\rightarrow\text{terminal response}.\]

Limits and Diagnostic Tests

Low ordering temperature, air sensitivity, magnetic-field dependence and reproducible interfaces remain obstacles. A credible experiment should report geometry, temperature, bias protocol, contact configuration and device-to-device statistics whenever they can change the interpretation.

深入推导与物理解释

自旋与动量选择隧穿

电导累加自旋分辨谱重叠：

\[G\propto\sum_{\sigma,\mathbf k}|t_{\sigma\mathbf k}|^2 D_{1\sigma}(\mathbf k,E_F)D_{2\sigma}(\mathbf k,E_F).\]

平行与反平行磁构型同时改变自旋可用态；在晶态二维堆叠中还改变动量/谷匹配。磁性半导体势垒本身也可通过交换劈裂势垒高度充当自旋过滤器。

栅控

静电栅移动带边与费米面，不同自旋/谷能带进入隧穿窗口时 TMR 甚至可变号。这比固定 Jullière 极化更丰富，也使接触掺杂和分层电势不可忽略。

证据与局限

应把磁电导台阶与独立层磁化对应，绘制偏压—栅压图，并区分自旋过滤与普通势垒高度磁阻。与传统 MTJ 比较时还必须计入低有序温度、外场翻转需求、空气退化和扭角变异。

最小模型

\[G_P and G_AP depend on spin-resolved DOS and momentum matching\]

这个式子用于组织物理关系，而不是在任何条件下都成立的拟合公式。定量使用前必须检查其近似、维度和边界条件。

器件意义

这些器件把 vdW 自旋电子学连接到 MRAM、自旋传感和多功能 p–n 结。

合理的分析顺序是

\[\text{材料与几何}\rightarrow\text{状态与势垒}\rightarrow\text{输运或翻转}\rightarrow\text{端口响应}.\]

限制与判据

低有序温度、空气敏感、磁场依赖及界面重复性仍是障碍。 当几何、温度、偏压流程、接触方式或器件间统计会影响结论时，可信实验必须完整报告这些条件。
