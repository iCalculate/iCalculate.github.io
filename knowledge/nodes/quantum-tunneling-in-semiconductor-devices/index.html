Physical Picture

A carrier can cross a classically forbidden barrier when its wavefunction has finite amplitude on the far side; barrier shape, effective mass and conserved momentum set the transmission. The useful state variables and conserved quantities must be identified before selecting a transport model. In a real device the intrinsic material response is coupled self-consistently to electrostatics, contacts, disorder and the external circuit.

Deep Derivation and Physical Interpretation

Derivation from evanescent waves

In a one-dimensional forbidden region, the stationary Schrödinger equation gives an imaginary wave vector

\[\kappa(x,E)=\frac{\sqrt{2m^*(x)[U(x)-E]}}{\hbar}.\]

Writing \(\psi=A(x)e^{-\int^x\kappa dx'}\) and retaining the leading WKB term yields

\[T(E)\approx \exp\!\left[-2\int_{x_1}^{x_2}\kappa(x,E)\,dx\right].\]

For a rectangular barrier, \(\ln T\propto-t\sqrt{m^*\Phi}\); for a triangular barrier \(U(x)=\Phi-qFx\), integration gives the Fowler–Nordheim exponent

\[T_{\rm FN}\sim \exp\!\left[-\frac{4\sqrt{2m^*}\Phi^{3/2}}{3q\hbar F}\right].\]

This is why thickness variation enters exponentially, whereas area usually enters only as a prefactor.

From transmission to measured current

Transmission is not current. Reservoir occupation and available transverse modes must be included:

\[I=\frac{gq}{h}\int M(E)T(E)[f_S(E)-f_D(E)]\,dE.\]

Direct tunnelling preserves the band identity across a thin barrier; band-to-band tunnelling connects valence and conduction bands; trap-assisted tunnelling inserts one or more real intermediate states. Their temperature, thickness and field dependences are therefore different.

What falsifies a tunnelling interpretation

A straight Fowler–Nordheim plot is not sufficient. One should test area scaling, polarity, temperature dependence, barrier-thickness dependence and whether extracted barrier heights agree with independent band alignment. Strong temperature activation, random-telegraph noise or weak exponential thickness dependence can indicate trap-assisted transport. WKB is unreliable at atomically abrupt interfaces, near resonances, and when in-plane momentum conservation or many-body spectral functions control the process.

Minimal Model

\[T(E)≈exp[-2∫κ(x)dx], κ=sqrt(2m*(U-E))/ħ\]

This expression is a compact organizing relation, not a universal fitting law. Its assumptions, dimensionality and boundary conditions must be checked before quantitative use.

Device Consequences

Direct, Fowler–Nordheim, band-to-band and trap-assisted tunneling form a common language for TFETs, flash, tunnel junctions and leakage.

The correct comparison therefore follows the causal chain

\[\text{material and geometry}\rightarrow\text{states and barriers}\rightarrow\text{transport or switching}\rightarrow\text{terminal response}.\]

Limits and Diagnostic Tests

WKB fails near abrupt turning points, resonances and atomically thin barriers; coherent NEGF or transfer-matrix treatments may be required. A credible experiment should report geometry, temperature, bias protocol, contact configuration and device-to-device statistics whenever they can change the interpretation.

深入推导与物理解释

从倏逝波推导隧穿率

在一维经典禁阻区，定态薛定谔方程给出虚波矢

\[\kappa(x,E)=\frac{\sqrt{2m^*(x)[U(x)-E]}}{\hbar}.\]

令 \(\psi=A(x)e^{-\int^x\kappa dx'}\)，保留 WKB 的最低阶项可得

\[T(E)\approx \exp\!\left[-2\int_{x_1}^{x_2}\kappa(x,E)\,dx\right].\]

矩形势垒满足 \(\ln T\propto-t\sqrt{m^*\Phi}\)；三角势垒 \(U(x)=\Phi-qFx\) 的积分给出 Fowler–Nordheim 指数

\[T_{\rm FN}\sim \exp\!\left[-\frac{4\sqrt{2m^*}\Phi^{3/2}}{3q\hbar F}\right].\]

因此厚度涨落以指数方式进入隧穿率，而面积通常只出现在前因子中。

从透射率到端口电流

透射率本身不是电流，还必须乘上电极库占据差和横向模式数：

\[I=\frac{gq}{h}\int M(E)T(E)[f_S(E)-f_D(E)]\,dE.\]

直接隧穿跨越薄势垒但保持能带身份；带间隧穿连接价带与导带；陷阱辅助隧穿则引入一个或多个真实中间态。因此三者的温度、厚度和电场标度不同。

如何证伪“隧穿”解释

Fowler–Nordheim 作图近似线性并不是充分证据。还应检验面积、极性、温度、势垒厚度标度，并把拟合势垒高度与独立能带对准比较。强热激活、随机电报噪声或过弱的厚度指数关系常提示陷阱辅助过程。对于原子级突变界面、共振附近，以及由面内动量守恒或多体谱函数控制的过程，WKB 通常失效。

最小模型

\[T(E)≈exp[-2∫κ(x)dx], κ=sqrt(2m*(U-E))/ħ\]

这个式子用于组织物理关系，而不是在任何条件下都成立的拟合公式。定量使用前必须检查其近似、维度和边界条件。

器件意义

直接、Fowler–Nordheim、带间及陷阱辅助隧穿统一描述 TFET、Flash、隧穿结和栅泄漏。

合理的分析顺序是

\[\text{材料与几何}\rightarrow\text{状态与势垒}\rightarrow\text{输运或翻转}\rightarrow\text{端口响应}.\]

限制与判据

WKB 在突变转折点、共振及原子级薄势垒中会失效，可能需要相干 NEGF 或传输矩阵。 当几何、温度、偏压流程、接触方式或器件间统计会影响结论时，可信实验必须完整报告这些条件。
