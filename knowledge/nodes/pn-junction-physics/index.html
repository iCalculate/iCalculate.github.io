The PN Junction: Space Charge, Band Bending, and RectificationPN结：空间电荷、能带与整流

From diffusion-built space charge and built-in potential to the diode equation, junction capacitance, transients, and breakdown.从扩散建立空间电荷和内建电势，再由少数载流子边界条件推导二极管方程、结电容与击穿。

Junction DevicesDevice PhysicsTransport Physics
Read in阅读语言

Core idea: A PN-junction barrier is not inserted by hand. Initial diffusion leaves immobile ionized dopants behind; their space charge creates an opposing field until drift and diffusion balance dynamically.

Figure 1 | Formation of the depletion region and opposing drift currents. Inductiveload, public domain; source.

Equilibrium and Built-In Potential

For an abrupt, nondegenerate homojunction with fully ionized dopants,

\[V_{bi}=\frac{kT}{q}\ln\frac{N_AN_D}{n_i^2}.\]

The depletion approximation gives \(N_Ax_p=N_Dx_n\) and

\[W=x_p+x_n=\sqrt{\frac{2\varepsilon_s}{q} \left(\frac{1}{N_A}+\frac{1}{N_D}\right)(V_{bi}-V_A)}.\]

Forward bias lowers the barrier and narrows the depletion region; reverse bias does the opposite.

Figure 2 | A flat Fermi level at equilibrium and band bending across the built-in potential. Inductiveload, public domain; source.

Why Forward Current Is Exponential

Figure 3 | Forward bias lowers the majority-carrier barrier. Inductiveload, public domain; source.

An ideal diode assumes low injection, negligible depletion-region recombination, negligible field in the neutral regions, and sufficiently distant contacts. Minority-carrier densities at the depletion edges become

\[p_n(0)=p_{n0}e^{qV_A/kT},\qquad n_p(0)=n_{p0}e^{qV_A/kT}.\]

Solving the minority-carrier diffusion equations yields

\[J=J_S(e^{qV_A/kT}-1),\]
\[J_S=q\left(\frac{D_pp_{n0}}{L_p}+\frac{D_nn_{p0}}{L_n}\right).\]

The exponential is a Boltzmann response of the junction-edge concentration to barrier lowering, not an empirical fit.

Capacitance, Transients, and Breakdown

Reverse bias stores depletion charge, producing

\[C_j'=\frac{\varepsilon_s}{W}.\]

Forward bias stores excess minority carriers in the neutral regions, producing diffusion capacitance and reverse-recovery delay. Strong reverse fields lead either to band-to-band tunneling in narrow, heavily doped junctions or to impact-ionization avalanche in wider, more lightly doped junctions.

From One Junction to Many Devices

  • Two coupled junctions form the BJT.
  • Junction fields separate photocarriers in photodiodes and solar cells.
  • A lightly doped drift region supports voltage in power devices at the cost of resistance.
  • MOSFET source/body and drain/body junctions create the body diode and junction capacitances.

Common Pitfalls

  • A depletion region is not charge-free: it lacks mobile carriers but contains ionized dopant charge.
  • Reverse saturation current is supplied mainly by thermally generated minority carriers.
  • “0.7 V turn-on” is not a law; voltage depends on current density, temperature, material, area, and resistance.

From equilibrium field to diode current

Equating Fermi levels across uniformly doped material gives

\[V_{bi}=\frac{k_BT}{q}\ln\frac{N_AN_D}{n_i^2}.\]

In the depletion approximation, charge neutrality \(N_Ax_p=N_Dx_n\) and Poisson integration give

\[W=\sqrt{\frac{2\epsilon_s}{q}\left(\frac1{N_A}+\frac1{N_D}\right)(V_{bi}-V)}.\]

Forward bias injects minority carriers whose diffusion–recombination solution yields \(I=I_S(e^{qV/k_BT}-1)\). The ideality factor changes when depletion-region recombination, high injection or series resistance dominates. Junction capacitance \(C_j=\epsilon A/W\) probes doping, while diffusion capacitance measures stored minority charge. Reverse breakdown must distinguish tunnelling from impact ionization rather than treating breakdown voltage as a single empirical limit.

核心结论：PN 结的势垒不是预先放进去的。电子和空穴最初因浓度梯度扩散，留下不可移动的离化杂质；这些空间电荷产生反向电场，直到漂移与扩散达到动态平衡。

1. 热平衡与内建电势

对突变同质结、完全电离、非简并近似，远离结区有 \(n_n\approx N_D\)、\(p_p\approx N_A\)。平衡费米能级对齐给出

\[V_{bi}=\frac{kT}{q}\ln\frac{N_AN_D}{n_i^2}.\]

耗尽近似下电荷密度分段为 \(-qN_A\) 与 \(+qN_D\)，电中性要求

\[N_Ax_p=N_Dx_n.\]

总耗尽宽度

\[W=x_p+x_n= \sqrt{\frac{2\varepsilon_s}{q} \left(\frac{1}{N_A}+\frac{1}{N_D}\right)(V_{bi}-V_A)}.\]

正偏 \(V_A>0\) 降低势垒并缩窄耗尽区；反偏增大势垒与耗尽区。

图 2｜平衡时费米能级水平，能带弯曲对应内建电势。原图：Inductiveload，公有领域；文件页。

2. 正向偏置为何产生指数电流

图 3｜正向偏置降低多数载流子跨结势垒。原图：Inductiveload，公有领域；文件页。

理想二极管采用四个关键假设：耗尽区内无净产生复合、低注入、准中性区电场可忽略、接触足够远。结边少子由玻尔兹曼因子提升：

\[p_n(0)=p_{n0}e^{qV_A/kT},\qquad n_p(0)=n_{p0}e^{qV_A/kT}.\]

解准中性区少子扩散方程得到

\[J=J_S\left(e^{qV_A/kT}-1\right),\]
\[J_S=q\left(\frac{D_pp_{n0}}{L_p}+\frac{D_nn_{p0}}{L_n}\right).\]

指数项来自“偏压改变势垒后，结边热平衡载流子按玻尔兹曼因子改变”，而不是来自经验拟合。

3. 电容、瞬态与击穿

反偏下耗尽层存储空间电荷，单位面积结电容

\[C_j'=\frac{\varepsilon_s}{W}.\]

正偏下准中性区存储过剩少子，形成扩散电容；它常主导二极管反向恢复时间。反向高场下有两类极限：重掺杂、窄耗尽区的带间隧穿（Zener）和较轻掺杂、宽耗尽区的碰撞电离（雪崩）。详见击穿、缩放与器件非理想效应。

4. 从一个结到其他器件

  • 两个耦合 PN 结构成双极晶体管：少子注入与电流放大；
  • 光生电子空穴对被结区电场分离，形成光电二极管与太阳电池；
  • 功率器件通过轻掺杂漂移区承担高压，但付出导通电阻代价；
  • MOSFET 的源/体和漏/体同样是 PN 结，决定体二极管与结电容。

5. 常见误区

  • 耗尽区不是完全“没有电荷”，而是缺少自由载流子、保留离化杂质空间电荷。
  • 反向饱和电流不来自多数载流子越过更高势垒，而主要由热生少子供应。
  • “0.7 V 导通”不是基本定律；电压取决于电流密度、温度、材料、面积和串联电阻。

从平衡内建场到二极管电流

均匀掺杂材料接触后费米能级对齐，得到

\[V_{bi}=\frac{k_BT}{q}\ln\frac{N_AN_D}{n_i^2}.\]

耗尽近似下由 \(N_Ax_p=N_Dx_n\) 与泊松积分得到

\[W=\sqrt{\frac{2\epsilon_s}{q}\left(\frac1{N_A}+\frac1{N_D}\right)(V_{bi}-V)}.\]

正向偏压注入少数载流子，扩散—复合方程给出 \(I=I_S(e^{qV/k_BT}-1)\)。耗尽区复合、高注入或串联电阻占主导时理想因子会改变。结电容 \(C_j=\epsilon A/W\) 探测掺杂，扩散电容则测量少子存储。反向击穿必须区分隧穿与碰撞电离，不能把击穿电压视为一个纯经验极限。
