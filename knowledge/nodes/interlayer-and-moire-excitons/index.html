Physical Picture

In a type-II vdW heterostructure the electron and hole can occupy different layers; the resulting dipole lengthens lifetime and makes the exciton sensitive to gate field, twist and moiré confinement. The useful state variables and conserved quantities must be identified before selecting a transport model. In a real device the intrinsic material response is coupled self-consistently to electrostatics, contacts, disorder and the external circuit.

Deep Derivation and Physical Interpretation

Dipolar interlayer excitons

In a type-II heterobilayer, the electron and hole occupy different layers separated by \(d\). Their permanent out-of-plane dipole \(p=ed\) produces a gate-tunable Stark shift

\[\Delta E_X=-pF_z-\frac12\alpha F_z^2.\]

Reduced wavefunction overlap lengthens radiative lifetime but also weakens oscillator strength. The dipole–dipole interaction is repulsive at long range, approximately \(U(r)\propto p^2/(4\pi\epsilon r^3)\).

Moiré confinement

Twist or lattice mismatch creates a slowly varying registry and exciton potential

\[V_M(\mathbf r)=\sum_j V_j\cos(\mathbf G_j\cdot\mathbf r+\phi_j).\]

When the moiré potential exceeds exciton kinetic bandwidth, centre-of-mass motion localizes into an array of quantum emitters; at weaker modulation it forms minibands. Optical selection rules become site and valley dependent because local stacking changes hybridization.

What must be separated experimentally

Multiple narrow peaks can originate from moiré trapping, strain or defects. A moiré assignment should track twist-controlled periodicity, show systematic gate/Stark response and ideally correlate with structural imaging. Long lifetime does not by itself imply long diffusion: localized moiré excitons can live long while moving very little.

Minimal Model

\[ΔE_Stark≈-p_z E_z\]

This expression is a compact organizing relation, not a universal fitting law. Its assumptions, dimensionality and boundary conditions must be checked before quantitative use.

Device Consequences

Interlayer excitons enable electrically tunable emitters, modulators, excitonic transport and arrays of localized quantum emitters.

The correct comparison therefore follows the causal chain

\[\text{material and geometry}\rightarrow\text{states and barriers}\rightarrow\text{transport or switching}\rightarrow\text{terminal response}.\]

Limits and Diagnostic Tests

Long lifetime can coexist with weak oscillator strength; disorder and reconstruction complicate assignment of moiré exciton lines. A credible experiment should report geometry, temperature, bias protocol, contact configuration and device-to-device statistics whenever they can change the interpretation.

深入推导与物理解释

偶极型层间激子

在 II 型异质双层中，电子和空穴分处间距为 \(d\) 的不同层。永久面外偶极 \(p=ed\) 产生可栅控 Stark 位移

\[\Delta E_X=-pF_z-\frac12\alpha F_z^2.\]

波函数重叠减小会延长辐射寿命，同时也削弱振子强度。远距离偶极—偶极作用为排斥，近似满足 \(U(r)\propto p^2/(4\pi\epsilon r^3)\)。

莫尔限域

扭角或晶格失配产生缓慢变化的局域堆垛与激子势

\[V_M(\mathbf r)=\sum_j V_j\cos(\mathbf G_j\cdot\mathbf r+\phi_j).\]

当莫尔势超过激子动能带宽，质心运动被局域成量子发射体阵列；调制较弱时则形成迷你能带。局域堆垛改变杂化，使光学选择定则同时依赖位置和谷。

实验上必须区分什么

多条窄峰也可能来自应变或缺陷。莫尔归因应随扭角控制的周期系统变化，表现可预测的栅压/Stark 响应，并最好与结构成像相关。长寿命也不等于长扩散：局域莫尔激子可以寿命很长却几乎不移动。

最小模型

\[ΔE_Stark≈-p_z E_z\]

这个式子用于组织物理关系，而不是在任何条件下都成立的拟合公式。定量使用前必须检查其近似、维度和边界条件。

器件意义

层间激子支持电调发光器、调制器、激子输运及局域量子发射体阵列。

合理的分析顺序是

\[\text{材料与几何}\rightarrow\text{状态与势垒}\rightarrow\text{输运或翻转}\rightarrow\text{端口响应}.\]

限制与判据

长寿命可能伴随较弱振子强度，无序和结构重构也会使莫尔激子谱线归属复杂化。 当几何、温度、偏压流程、接触方式或器件间统计会影响结论时，可信实验必须完整报告这些条件。
