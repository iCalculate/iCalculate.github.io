Physical Picture

Switchable polarization can survive in atomically thin crystals; in sliding ferroelectrics it arises from stacking-dependent interlayer charge transfer and reverses when layers translate. The useful state variables and conserved quantities must be identified before selecting a transport model. In a real device the intrinsic material response is coupled self-consistently to electrostatics, contacts, disorder and the external circuit.

Deep Derivation and Physical Interpretation

Order parameter and electrostatic boundary condition

Ferroelectricity requires a switchable polarization \(P\), not merely a polar crystal structure. A minimal Landau free energy is

\[F(P)=\frac{\alpha}{2}P^2+\frac{\beta}{4}P^4+\frac{\gamma}{6}P^6-EP.\]

For \(\alpha<0\) and stabilizing higher terms, two minima appear. In an ultrathin film, incomplete screening creates depolarization field \(E_d\approx-P/(\epsilon_0\epsilon_f)\), modified by electrode screening and dielectric dead layers. The observable state is therefore a property of the entire electrostatic stack.

Sliding ferroelectricity

Some van der Waals bilayers acquire polarization from registry-dependent interlayer charge transfer. Switching moves one layer between stacking minima rather than displacing ions inside a conventional unit cell. A periodic coordinate \(u\) replaces a simple scalar \(P\) and domain walls can involve partial dislocations.

Proof of switchability

Hysteretic transport or piezoresponse alone is ambiguous because traps and ionic motion also remember bias. Strong evidence combines opposite stable polarization states, structural or electrostatic contrast, switching-current integration, thickness/temperature trends and pulse protocols that separate switching from leakage.

Minimal Model

\[P=-∂F/∂E; F(P)=αP²/2+βP⁴/4+...\]

This expression is a compact organizing relation, not a universal fitting law. Its assumptions, dimensionality and boundary conditions must be checked before quantitative use.

Device Consequences

The physics supports ultrathin FeFETs, multistate memories, tunnel junctions and synaptic transistors.

The correct comparison therefore follows the causal chain

\[\text{material and geometry}\rightarrow\text{states and barriers}\rightarrow\text{transport or switching}\rightarrow\text{terminal response}.\]

Limits and Diagnostic Tests

Charge trapping can imitate hysteresis; polarization, retention, fatigue and structural switching must be distinguished experimentally. A credible experiment should report geometry, temperature, bias protocol, contact configuration and device-to-device statistics whenever they can change the interpretation.

深入推导与物理解释

序参量与静电边界条件

铁电性要求可翻转极化 \(P\)，而不只是具有极性晶体结构。最小 Landau 自由能为

\[F(P)=\frac{\alpha}{2}P^2+\frac{\beta}{4}P^4+\frac{\gamma}{6}P^6-EP.\]

当 \(\alpha<0\) 且高阶项稳定时出现两个极小值。超薄膜中的不完全屏蔽产生退极化场 \(E_d\approx-P/(\epsilon_0\epsilon_f)\)，并受到电极屏蔽和介电死层修正。因此可观测铁电态属于整个静电堆叠，而非孤立材料常数。

滑移铁电

某些范德华双层的极化来自堆垛依赖的层间电荷转移。翻转过程是在不同堆垛极小值之间滑动整层，而不是传统晶胞内离子位移。周期滑移坐标 \(u\) 取代简单标量 \(P\)，畴壁还可能包含部分位错。

如何证明可翻转

滞回输运或压电响应并不唯一，因为陷阱和离子运动也会记忆偏压。强证据应结合两种相反稳定极化态、结构或静电对比、开关电流积分、厚度/温度趋势，以及能把真实翻转与漏电分开的脉冲协议。

最小模型

\[P=-∂F/∂E; F(P)=αP²/2+βP⁴/4+...\]

这个式子用于组织物理关系，而不是在任何条件下都成立的拟合公式。定量使用前必须检查其近似、维度和边界条件。

器件意义

该物理支持超薄 FeFET、多状态存储、铁电隧穿结及突触晶体管。

合理的分析顺序是

\[\text{材料与几何}\rightarrow\text{状态与势垒}\rightarrow\text{输运或翻转}\rightarrow\text{端口响应}.\]

限制与判据

电荷俘获也会产生类似滞回，实验必须区分真实极化、保持、疲劳及结构翻转。 当几何、温度、偏压流程、接触方式或器件间统计会影响结论时，可信实验必须完整报告这些条件。
