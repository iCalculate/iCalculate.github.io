Physical Picture

A conventional MOSFET changes current by electrostatically moving a barrier through a thermally broadened Fermi distribution, producing the familiar room-temperature 60 mV/dec lower bound under ideal quasistatic conditions. The useful state variables and conserved quantities must be identified before selecting a transport model. In a real device the intrinsic material response is coupled self-consistently to electrostatics, contacts, disorder and the external circuit.

Deep Derivation and Physical Interpretation

Where 60 mV per decade comes from

For a long-channel MOSFET in weak inversion, the source injects over a barrier whose height is moved by the surface potential \(\psi_s\). The mobile charge and diffusion current scale as

\[I_D\propto n_s\propto \exp\!\left(\frac{q\psi_s}{k_BT}\right).\]

The gate voltage is divided between oxide and semiconductor. Small-signal charge balance gives

\[\frac{dV_G}{d\psi_s}=m=1+\frac{C_{\rm dep}+C_{\rm it}}{C_{\rm ox}},\]

and therefore

\[SS\equiv\frac{dV_G}{d\log_{10}I_D} =(\ln10)\frac{k_BT}{q}m.\]

At 300 K, \((\ln10)k_BT/q=59.6\) mV/dec. The limit contains two assumptions: the injected distribution has a thermal Boltzmann tail and the internal barrier cannot move faster than the applied gate (\(m\ge1\) for passive positive capacitances).

How steep-slope concepts change the premises

TFETs replace thermionic injection with a gate-controlled tunnelling window; cold-source FETs truncate the source spectral tail; NCFETs seek \(d\psi_s/dV_G>1\) through a stabilized ferroelectric response. These are distinct mechanisms and should not be compared by the minimum local slope alone.

Useful device-level integral

For a required on/off ratio \(R\), an average swing \(SS_{\rm avg}\) sets the gate-voltage budget

\[\Delta V_G=SS_{\rm avg}\log_{10}R.\]

A 20 mV/dec minimum over half a decade is irrelevant if six decades require 400 mV. Report the current interval, temperature scaling, forward/reverse sweeps, capacitance, on-current at fixed \(V_{DD}\), delay \(CV/I\), and device statistics.

Minimal Model

\[SS=(ln10)kT/q · (1+C_dep/C_ox)\]

This expression is a compact organizing relation, not a universal fitting law. Its assumptions, dimensionality and boundary conditions must be checked before quantitative use.

Device Consequences

TFETs change injection, NCFETs amplify surface potential and cold-source FETs filter the injected energy distribution.

The correct comparison therefore follows the causal chain

\[\text{material and geometry}\rightarrow\text{states and barriers}\rightarrow\text{transport or switching}\rightarrow\text{terminal response}.\]

Limits and Diagnostic Tests

Sub-60 mV/dec over a narrow current interval does not alone establish useful low-voltage logic; hysteresis, current, capacitance and delay must be reported. A credible experiment should report geometry, temperature, bias protocol, contact configuration and device-to-device statistics whenever they can change the interpretation.

深入推导与物理解释

60 mV/dec 从哪里来

长沟道 MOSFET 在弱反型区由源端载流子越过势垒注入，势垒高度由表面势 \(\psi_s\) 调节。面载流子密度与扩散电流满足

\[I_D\propto n_s\propto \exp\!\left(\frac{q\psi_s}{k_BT}\right).\]

栅压分配在氧化层和半导体上。小信号电荷平衡给出

\[\frac{dV_G}{d\psi_s}=m=1+\frac{C_{\rm dep}+C_{\rm it}}{C_{\rm ox}},\]

于是

\[SS\equiv\frac{dV_G}{d\log_{10}I_D} =(\ln10)\frac{k_BT}{q}m.\]

300 K 时 \((\ln10)k_BT/q=59.6\) mV/dec。这个极限包含两项前提：注入分布具有热 Boltzmann 高能尾，同时由正电容组成的被动栅堆叠不能使内部势垒移动得比外加栅压更快，即 \(m\ge1\)。

陡峭亚阈值器件改变了哪条前提

TFET 用栅控隧穿窗口替代热发射；冷源 FET 截断源端能谱高能尾；NCFET 则试图用稳定化铁电响应实现 \(d\psi_s/dV_G>1\)。三者机制不同，不能只比较一个最小局域斜率。

从局域斜率回到器件指标

若要求开关比 \(R\)，平均摆幅决定所需栅压范围：

\[\Delta V_G=SS_{\rm avg}\log_{10}R.\]

若六个数量级仍需 400 mV，那么只在半个数量级内出现 20 mV/dec 没有系统意义。应同时报告电流区间、温度标度、正反扫描、栅电容、固定 \(V_{DD}\) 下的开态电流、\(CV/I\) 延迟及器件统计。

最小模型

\[SS=(ln10)kT/q · (1+C_dep/C_ox)\]

这个式子用于组织物理关系，而不是在任何条件下都成立的拟合公式。定量使用前必须检查其近似、维度和边界条件。

器件意义

TFET 改变注入机制，NCFET 放大表面势，冷源 FET 则过滤注入能量分布。

合理的分析顺序是

\[\text{材料与几何}\rightarrow\text{状态与势垒}\rightarrow\text{输运或翻转}\rightarrow\text{端口响应}.\]

限制与判据

窄电流区间出现亚 60 mV/dec 并不等于具备实用低压逻辑性能，还必须报告滞回、电流、电容和延迟。 当几何、温度、偏压流程、接触方式或器件间统计会影响结论时，可信实验必须完整报告这些条件。
