Physical Picture

A FeFET stores remanent polarization in its gate stack; the polarization charge shifts channel threshold and can be read nondestructively as conductance. The useful state variables and conserved quantities must be identified before selecting a transport model. In a real device the intrinsic material response is coupled self-consistently to electrostatics, contacts, disorder and the external circuit.

Deep Derivation and Physical Interpretation

Polarization-to-threshold transduction

Remanent polarization changes channel electrostatics. In an ideal stack the two states create a memory-window scale

\[\Delta V_T\sim\frac{2P_r}{C_{stack}},\]

but depolarization, interface charge and semiconductor screening reduce it. The ferroelectric and interfacial dielectric fields must satisfy displacement continuity \(D=\epsilon E+P\).

Switching distribution

Real films switch through nucleation and domain-wall growth rather than coherent rotation. A distribution of activation fields gives gradual pulse-number-dependent polarization, useful for analogue weights but harmful to binary uniformity. Merz-type kinetics often obey \(t_{sw}\sim t_0\exp(E_a/E)\) over a limited regime.

Retention, endurance and read disturb

Depolarization favours back-switching; charge trapping can temporarily compensate polarization but later drift; cycling can create wake-up and fatigue. Separate polarization switching from trapping using pulse schemes, switching-current integration and retention after removing read bias. A threshold loop alone cannot identify which state variable is stored.

Minimal Model

\[ΔV_T≈2P_r/C_stack, modified by depolarization and traps\]

This expression is a compact organizing relation, not a universal fitting law. Its assumptions, dimensionality and boundary conditions must be checked before quantitative use.

Device Consequences

HfO2-based and 2D FeFETs offer compact nonvolatile memory and analogue synaptic weights.

The correct comparison therefore follows the causal chain

\[\text{material and geometry}\rightarrow\text{states and barriers}\rightarrow\text{transport or switching}\rightarrow\text{terminal response}.\]

Limits and Diagnostic Tests

Wake-up, fatigue, imprint, depolarization, interfacial trapping and device-to-device variability must be separated. A credible experiment should report geometry, temperature, bias protocol, contact configuration and device-to-device statistics whenever they can change the interpretation.

深入推导与物理解释

从极化到阈值位移

剩余极化改变沟道静电。理想堆叠的两种状态产生近似存储窗口

\[\Delta V_T\sim\frac{2P_r}{C_{stack}},\]

但退极化、界面电荷与半导体屏蔽会减小它。铁电层和界面介质必须满足位移场连续 \(D=\epsilon E+P\)。

翻转分布

真实薄膜通过成核和畴壁生长而非一致转动翻转。激活场分布使极化随脉冲数逐步变化，这对模拟权重有利，却损害二值均匀性。有限区间内常见 Merz 动力学 \(t_{sw}\sim t_0\exp(E_a/E)\)。

保持、耐久与读扰动

退极化促进回翻；电荷俘获可暂时补偿极化却随后漂移；循环还会产生唤醒与疲劳。应通过脉冲协议、开关电流积分和去除读偏压后的保持测试区分极化翻转与俘获。只有阈值回线不能确定真正存储的状态变量。

最小模型

\[ΔV_T≈2P_r/C_stack, modified by depolarization and traps\]

这个式子用于组织物理关系，而不是在任何条件下都成立的拟合公式。定量使用前必须检查其近似、维度和边界条件。

器件意义

HfO₂ 基与二维 FeFET 可实现紧凑非易失存储及模拟突触权重。

合理的分析顺序是

\[\text{材料与几何}\rightarrow\text{状态与势垒}\rightarrow\text{输运或翻转}\rightarrow\text{端口响应}.\]

限制与判据

必须区分唤醒、疲劳、印记、退极化、界面俘获及器件间变异。 当几何、温度、偏压流程、接触方式或器件间统计会影响结论时，可信实验必须完整报告这些条件。
