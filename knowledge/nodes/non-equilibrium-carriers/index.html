Nonequilibrium Carriers: Generation, Recombination, and Quasi-Fermi Levels非平衡载流子：产生、复合与准费米能级

Carrier lifetime, diffusion length, generation–recombination mechanisms, and electron–hole quasi-Fermi-level splitting under optical or electrical injection.描述光生或电注入载流子的寿命、扩散长度、产生复合机制及电子空穴准费米能级分裂。

Transport PhysicsOptical PropertiesDevice Physics
Read in阅读语言

Core idea: Optical excitation, electrical injection, or impact ionization drives \(np\) away from its equilibrium value \(n_i^2\). Recombination restores equilibrium; lifetime and diffusion length then control diode injection, photodetector collection, and transistor gain.

Excess Carriers and Low Injection

Optical generation normally creates \(\Delta n=\Delta p\). In p-type material, \(\Delta n\ll p_0\approx N_A\) defines low injection. The majority-carrier density then changes little and minority-carrier dynamics can be linearized. Under high injection, conductivity, electric field, recombination, and neutrality must all be solved self-consistently.

Quasi-Fermi Levels

If intraband thermalization is much faster than interband generation and recombination, each carrier population remains approximately thermal:

\[n=N_Ce^{-(E_C-E_{Fn})/kT},\qquad p=N_Ve^{-(E_{Fp}-E_V)/kT}.\]

Therefore

\[np=n_i^2\exp\left(\frac{E_{Fn}-E_{Fp}}{kT}\right).\]

The splitting \(E_{Fn}-E_{Fp}\) is the nonequilibrium chemical-potential difference. In an open-circuit solar cell it is closely related to the maximum obtainable terminal voltage.

Generation and Recombination Mechanisms

Mechanism Representative rate Typical regime
Radiative \(R_{rad}=B(np-n_i^2)\) direct-gap emitters
Shockley–Read–Hall \(U_{SRH}=\dfrac{np-n_i^2}{\tau_p(n+n_1)+\tau_n(p+p_1)}\) deep defects and traps
Auger \(R_A=(C_nn+C_pp)(np-n_i^2)\) high injection or heavy doping
Optical generation \(G(x)=\alpha\Phi_0e^{-\alpha x}\) absorption coefficient \(\alpha\)

Midgap traps are especially efficient SRH centers because they can successively capture both carrier types.

Lifetime and Diffusion Length

Under uniform low injection after generation is removed,

\[\Delta n(t)=\Delta n(0)e^{-t/\tau_n}.\]

The characteristic travel distances before recombination are

\[L_n=\sqrt{D_n\tau_n},\qquad L_p=\sqrt{D_p\tau_p}.\]

They enter PN-diode saturation current, BJT base transport, and photocarrier collection.

Common Pitfalls

  • Lifetime is not an immutable property of an individual electron; it depends on injection, defects, temperature, and competing channels.
  • A quasi-Fermi level is not an additional band. It parameterizes a nonequilibrium occupation.
  • Strong light emission requires radiative recombination to dominate nonradiative loss, not merely a large total recombination rate.

Quasi-Fermi levels and recombination balance

When scattering thermalizes each carrier population faster than generation/recombination, electrons and holes have separate quasi-Fermi levels:

\[n=N_Ce^{-(E_C-E_{Fn})/k_BT},\qquad p=N_Ve^{-(E_{Fp}-E_V)/k_BT}.\]

Their separation measures departure from equilibrium and sets the maximum electrochemical energy of a photovoltaic or LED. Shockley–Read–Hall recombination through a trap is

\[R_{SRH}=\frac{np-n_i^2}{\tau_p(n+n_1)+\tau_n(p+p_1)}.\]

Low-level lifetime is not transferable to high injection, where radiative \(Bnp\) and Auger \(C_nn^2p+C_pnp^2\) terms grow differently. Transient photoconductance, time-resolved luminescence and frequency response probe different weighted combinations of lifetime, diffusion and surface recombination.

1. 过剩载流子与小注入

光生一对电子和空穴时通常有 \(\Delta n=\Delta p\)。在 p 型材料中若

\[\Delta n\ll p_0\approx N_A,\]

称为小注入。此时多数载流子浓度几乎不变，少数载流子的复合动力学可线性化；大注入时电场、电导率、复合率和电中性条件都必须自洽求解。

2. 准费米能级

若带内热化远快于带间产生复合，电子和空穴各自仍近似服从费米分布：

\[n=N_Ce^{-(E_C-E_{Fn})/kT},\qquad p=N_Ve^{-(E_{Fp}-E_V)/kT}.\]

由此

\[np=n_i^2\exp\left(\frac{E_{Fn}-E_{Fp}}{kT}\right).\]

准费米能级分裂 \(E_{Fn}-E_{Fp}\) 是非平衡“化学势差”；在太阳电池开路状态下，它给出可实现端电压的热力学上限之一。

3. 产生与复合机制

机制 典型表达 主导情形
辐射复合 \(R_{rad}=B(np-n_i^2)\) 直接带隙材料、LED/激光器
Shockley–Read–Hall \(U_{SRH}=\dfrac{np-n_i^2}{\tau_p(n+n_1)+\tau_n(p+p_1)}\) 缺陷与深能级主导
Auger 复合 \(R_A=(C_nn+C_pp)(np-n_i^2)\) 高注入、高掺杂
光生 \(G(x)=\alpha\Phi_0e^{-\alpha x}\) 吸收系数为 \(\alpha\) 的均匀材料

SRH 公式说明：位于禁带中部附近的陷阱通常最有效，因为它能依次俘获电子和空穴。

4. 寿命与扩散长度

均匀小注入、关断外部产生后，过剩少子近似满足

\[\Delta n(t)=\Delta n(0)e^{-t/\tau_n}.\]

在空间输运问题中，扩散长度

\[L_n=\sqrt{D_n\tau_n},\qquad L_p=\sqrt{D_p\tau_p}\]

给出少子在复合前扩散的典型距离。PN 二极管饱和电流、BJT 基区输运因子、光电探测器收集效率都依赖它。

5. 常见误区

  • “寿命”不是单个电子固有且固定的寿命；它取决于注入水平、陷阱、温度和复合通道。
  • 准费米能级不是新增能带，而是描述非平衡占据的热力学参数。
  • 发光强不等于总复合少；LED 需要让辐射复合相对非辐射复合占优。

准费米能级与复合平衡

当载流子内部热化快于产生/复合时，电子和空穴分别具有准费米能级：

\[n=N_Ce^{-(E_C-E_{Fn})/k_BT},\qquad p=N_Ve^{-(E_{Fp}-E_V)/k_BT}.\]

两者分裂衡量非平衡程度，也决定光伏或 LED 可输出的最大电化学能。陷阱辅助 Shockley–Read–Hall 复合为

\[R_{SRH}=\frac{np-n_i^2}{\tau_p(n+n_1)+\tau_n(p+p_1)}.\]

低注入寿命不能直接迁移到高注入，因为辐射 \(Bnp\) 与 Auger \(C_nn^2p+C_pnp^2\) 项的增长规律不同。瞬态光电导、时间分辨发光与频率响应分别测到寿命、扩散和表面复合的不同加权组合。
