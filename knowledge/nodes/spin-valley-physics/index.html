Physical Picture

Broken inversion symmetry and strong spin–orbit coupling in monolayer TMDs tie spin, valley and optical helicity to the K and K′ band extrema. The useful state variables and conserved quantities must be identified before selecting a transport model. In a real device the intrinsic material response is coupled self-consistently to electrostatics, contacts, disorder and the external circuit.

Device Consequences

Optical selection, valley Hall transport and spin–valley locking provide extra state variables for sensing and information processing.

Limits and Diagnostic Tests

Fast intervalley scattering, contacts and room-temperature depolarization limit device retention and readout. A credible experiment should report geometry, temperature, bias protocol, contact configuration and device-to-device statistics whenever they can change the interpretation.
