Core idea: MRAM stores the relative magnetization of magnetic layers and reads it through tunneling magnetoresistance.

Physical Picture

MRAM stores the relative magnetization of magnetic layers and reads it through tunneling magnetoresistance. The useful state variables and conserved quantities must be identified before selecting a transport model. In a real device the intrinsic material response is coupled self-consistently to electrostatics, contacts, disorder and the external circuit.

Deep Derivation and Physical Interpretation

Tunnel magnetoresistance readout

An MTJ contains two ferromagnets separated by a thin tunnel barrier. Spin-resolved densities of states give, in the Jullière picture,

\[TMR=\frac{R_{AP}-R_P}{R_P}\approx\frac{2P_1P_2}{1-P_1P_2}.\]

Crystalline MgO junctions additionally use symmetry filtering, so interface disorder strongly affects TMR.

Magnetization dynamics and writing

The free-layer unit vector follows Landau–Lifshitz–Gilbert dynamics with spin torque

\[\dot{\mathbf m}=-\gamma\mathbf m\times\mathbf H_{eff} +\alpha\mathbf m\times\dot{\mathbf m}+\boldsymbol\tau_{STT/SOT}.\]

STT passes write current through the barrier; SOT uses an adjacent spin source. Lowering anisotropy reduces write current but also reduces retention.

Thermal stability

The stability factor \(\Delta=E_b/k_BT\approx K_{eff}V/k_BT\) controls spontaneous error rate. Switching is stochastic near threshold, so write-error rate must be measured versus pulse width and current. Endurance claims should include barrier breakdown, read disturb and distribution of \(\Delta\), not only median TMR.

Minimal Model

\[TMR=(R_AP-R_P)/R_P; Δ=E_barrier/kT controls retention\]

This expression is a compact organizing relation, not a universal fitting law. Its assumptions, dimensionality and boundary conditions must be checked before quantitative use.

Device Consequences

Spin-transfer and spin–orbit torque write schemes trade current, speed, selector area and endurance.

The correct comparison therefore follows the causal chain

\[\text{material and geometry}\rightarrow\text{states and barriers}\rightarrow\text{transport or switching}\rightarrow\text{terminal response}.\]

Limits and Diagnostic Tests

Thermal stability conflicts with low switching energy; variation in anisotropy and critical current affects arrays. A credible experiment should report geometry, temperature, bias protocol, contact configuration and device-to-device statistics whenever they can change the interpretation.

深入推导与物理解释

隧道磁阻读出

MTJ 由两个铁磁层和薄隧穿势垒组成。在 Jullière 图像中，自旋分辨态密度给出

\[TMR=\frac{R_{AP}-R_P}{R_P}\approx\frac{2P_1P_2}{1-P_1P_2}.\]

晶态 MgO 结还依赖对称性过滤，因此界面无序会强烈影响 TMR。

磁化动力学与写入

自由层单位磁化矢量服从带自旋力矩的 Landau–Lifshitz–Gilbert 方程

\[\dot{\mathbf m}=-\gamma\mathbf m\times\mathbf H_{eff} +\alpha\mathbf m\times\dot{\mathbf m}+\boldsymbol\tau_{STT/SOT}.\]

STT 让写电流穿过势垒，SOT 则使用邻近自旋源。降低各向异性可减小写电流，却会同时牺牲保持。

热稳定性

稳定因子 \(\Delta=E_b/k_BT\approx K_{eff}V/k_BT\) 控制自发错误率。阈值附近的翻转具有随机性，因此必须测量写错误率随脉宽和电流的变化。耐久结论还应包含势垒击穿、读扰动及 \(\Delta\) 分布，而不只是中位 TMR。

最小模型

\[TMR=(R_AP-R_P)/R_P; Δ=E_barrier/kT controls retention\]

这个式子用于组织物理关系，而不是在任何条件下都成立的拟合公式。定量使用前必须检查其近似、维度和边界条件。

器件意义

自旋转移矩和自旋轨道矩写入在电流、速度、选通面积与耐久之间权衡。

合理的分析顺序是

\[\text{材料与几何}\rightarrow\text{状态与势垒}\rightarrow\text{输运或翻转}\rightarrow\text{端口响应}.\]

限制与判据

热稳定性与低翻转能量相冲突，各向异性和临界电流变异影响阵列。 当几何、温度、偏压流程、接触方式或器件间统计会影响结论时，可信实验必须完整报告这些条件。
