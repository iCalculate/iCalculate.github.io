Core idea: Transport becomes ballistic when device length is shorter than the relevant momentum-relaxation mean free path; quasi-ballistic devices retain only part of the injected flux.

Physical Picture

Transport becomes ballistic when device length is shorter than the relevant momentum-relaxation mean free path; quasi-ballistic devices retain only part of the injected flux. The useful state variables and conserved quantities must be identified before selecting a transport model. In a real device the intrinsic material response is coupled self-consistently to electrostatics, contacts, disorder and the external circuit.

Deep Derivation and Physical Interpretation

Length scales and the crossover

Transport is ballistic only relative to a specified scattering process. Define momentum, energy and phase-relaxation lengths \(\lambda_m=v\tau_m\), \(\lambda_E=v\tau_E\) and \(L_\phi\). A short channel can satisfy \(L<\lambda_m\) while still losing phase through electron–electron collisions, or remain phase coherent while optical phonons remove energy at high bias.

For a mode with a spatially uniform backscattering mean free path \(\lambda\), adding channel resistance to the quantum contact resistance gives

\[T\simeq\frac{\lambda}{L+\lambda},\qquad G=G_0M\frac{\lambda}{L+\lambda}.\]

The diffusive limit \(L\gg\lambda\) recovers \(G\propto1/L\); the ballistic limit \(L\ll\lambda\) saturates at \(G_0M\). This interpolation is useful but does not describe energy-dependent or strongly inelastic scattering.

Injection-limited transistor current

At the top of a FET barrier, positive-velocity states are filled by the source and negative-velocity states partly by the drain. The ballistic current is set by charge times injection velocity rather than by a local mobility:

\[I_{\rm bal}\approx WQ_{\rm top}v_{\rm inj}.\]

Quasi-ballistic backscattering reduces the forward flux. Length scaling across otherwise identical devices is therefore more informative than fitting a single transfer curve. Contact resistance, access regions and self-heating must be removed before attributing current saturation to a channel ballistic limit.

Minimal Model

\[T≈λ/(λ+L)\]

This expression is a compact organizing relation, not a universal fitting law. Its assumptions, dimensionality and boundary conditions must be checked before quantitative use.

Device Consequences

This regime links Landauer injection to nanoscale MOSFET and 2D-FET current limits.

The correct comparison therefore follows the causal chain

\[\text{material and geometry}\rightarrow\text{states and barriers}\rightarrow\text{transport or switching}\rightarrow\text{terminal response}.\]

Limits and Diagnostic Tests

Ballistic does not mean scattering-free everywhere: contacts and reservoirs remain dissipative, and optical phonons can dominate at high field. A credible experiment should report geometry, temperature, bias protocol, contact configuration and device-to-device statistics whenever they can change the interpretation.

深入推导与物理解释

长度尺度与跨越区

“弹道”必须相对于具体散射过程定义。动量、能量和相位弛豫长度分别为 \(\lambda_m=v\tau_m\)、\(\lambda_E=v\tau_E\) 与 \(L_\phi\)。短沟道可能满足 \(L<\lambda_m\)，却通过电子—电子碰撞失去相位；也可能仍保持相干，但在高偏压下由光学声子迅速耗散能量。

若均匀背散射平均自由程为 \(\lambda\)，把沟道电阻与量子接触电阻串联可得

\[T\simeq\frac{\lambda}{L+\lambda},\qquad G=G_0M\frac{\lambda}{L+\lambda}.\]

\(L\gg\lambda\) 时恢复 \(G\propto1/L\) 的扩散极限，\(L\ll\lambda\) 时饱和到 \(G_0M\)。该插值便于建立直觉，但不能描述强能量依赖或强非弹性散射。

注入受限的晶体管电流

在 FET 势垒顶，正速度态由源极填充，负速度态部分由漏极填充。弹道电流由电荷与注入速度决定，而非局域迁移率：

\[I_{\rm bal}\approx WQ_{\rm top}v_{\rm inj}.\]

准弹道背散射会减少正向通量。因此，对工艺相同器件做沟道长度标度比拟合单条转移曲线更有信息。只有先去除接触电阻、接入区和自热，才能把电流饱和归因于沟道弹道极限。

最小模型

\[T≈λ/(λ+L)\]

这个式子用于组织物理关系，而不是在任何条件下都成立的拟合公式。定量使用前必须检查其近似、维度和边界条件。

器件意义

该区间把 Landauer 注入与纳米 MOSFET、二维 FET 的电流极限联系起来。

合理的分析顺序是

\[\text{材料与几何}\rightarrow\text{状态与势垒}\rightarrow\text{输运或翻转}\rightarrow\text{端口响应}.\]

限制与判据

弹道不等于处处无散射：耗散仍发生在接触和电极库，高场下光学声子也可能占主导。 当几何、温度、偏压流程、接触方式或器件间统计会影响结论时，可信实验必须完整报告这些条件。
