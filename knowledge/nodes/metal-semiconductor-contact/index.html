Metal–Semiconductor Contacts and Schottky Barriers金属-半导体接触与肖特基势垒

Schottky and ohmic contacts from work-function alignment, electron affinity, thermionic emission, tunneling, and Fermi-level pinning.从功函数、电子亲和能和费米能级对齐理解肖特基与欧姆接触，并比较热发射、隧穿和界面钉扎。

Core idea: A metal–semiconductor contact is governed by majority carriers crossing or tunneling through an interface barrier. The ideal work-function model is a starting point; real barriers also depend on interface states, metal-induced gap states, chemical reactions, and Fermi-level pinning.

Ideal Barrier

With metal work function \(\Phi_M=E_0-E_{FM}\) and semiconductor electron affinity \(\chi=E_0-E_C\), the ideal n-type electron barrier is

\[q\Phi_{Bn}=\Phi_M-\chi.\]

The semiconductor-side built-in voltage is approximately

\[V_{bi}=\Phi_{Bn}-\frac{E_C-E_F}{q}.\]

Barrier height controls thermionic emission exponentially; depletion width controls capacitance and tunneling. They are distinct quantities.

Current Transport

When thermionic emission dominates,

\[J=A^{**}T^2e^{-q\Phi_B/kT}\left(e^{qV/nkT}-1\right).\]

Higher doping narrows the barrier, shifting transport from thermionic emission to thermionic-field emission and finally field emission.

Ohmic Contacts Still Have Interface Physics

An ohmic contact is approximately linear and sufficiently low-resistance over the operating range. It may require favorable work functions, a low-barrier interfacial phase, heavy contact doping for tunneling, and controlled annealing. As intrinsic channel resistance shrinks, contact resistance often becomes the dominant bottleneck.

Nonideal Interfaces

Interface dipoles, defects, and metal-induced gap states weaken the ideal dependence of \(\Phi_B\) on metal work function. This Fermi-level pinning is why vacuum-level alignment alone rarely predicts a processed contact quantitatively.

Device Connections

Schottky diodes are majority-carrier devices and usually avoid PN-diode minority storage. MESFETs use a Schottky gate; MOSFET and two-dimensional FET contacts may be injection-limited; metal–semiconductor–metal photodetectors use back-to-back barriers.

Injection regimes and contact resistivity

For an ideal n-type Schottky contact, thermionic current is

\[J=A^{**}T^2e^{-q\Phi_{Bn}/k_BT}(e^{qV/nk_BT}-1).\]

Heavy doping narrows the depletion barrier and moves transport through thermionic-field emission toward field emission. The controlling ratio compares tunnelling energy \(E_{00}\) with \(k_BT\); contact resistance can fall even if barrier height is unchanged.

Measured two-terminal resistance obeys \(R=2R_c+R_{sh}L/W\). Transfer-length analysis gives \(L_T=\sqrt{\rho_c/R_{sh}}\) and separates sheet from contact resistance only if current spreading and geometry assumptions hold. Barrier extraction from Arrhenius plots is unreliable when tunnelling, inhomogeneous patches or gate-dependent area dominate.

1. 理想势垒

金属功函数 \(\Phi_M=E_0-E_{FM}\)，半导体电子亲和能 \(\chi=E_0-E_C\)。理想 n 型接触的电子势垒为

\[q\Phi_{Bn}=\Phi_M-\chi,\]

而半导体侧内建电势约为

\[V_{bi}=\Phi_{Bn}-\frac{E_C-E_F}{q}.\]

接触后费米能级必须对齐，半导体能带因空间电荷而弯曲。势垒高度和耗尽宽度是两个不同量：前者主要控制热发射指数，后者决定隧穿概率和电容。

2. 电流输运

热发射主导、串联电阻可忽略时，Schottky 二极管近似满足

\[J=A^{**}T^2e^{-q\Phi_B/kT} \left(e^{qV/nkT}-1\right),\]

其中 \(A^{**}\) 为有效 Richardson 常数，\(n\) 为理想因子。掺杂升高使势垒变窄，输运从热发射转向热场发射乃至直接场发射。

3. 欧姆接触不是“没有接触物理”

器件希望接触的 I–V 在工作范围近似线性且接触电阻低。常见策略是：

  • 选择有利功函数或形成低势垒相；
  • 接触区重掺杂，把耗尽层压窄以增强隧穿；
  • 退火形成硅化物或低电阻界面，同时控制扩散与缺陷。

接触电阻可通过传输线法等测量；总器件电阻下降后，接触往往从“边缘问题”变成主导瓶颈。

4. 非理想界面

真实界面常有界面偶极、缺陷和金属诱导禁带态，使 \(\Phi_B\) 对金属功函数的依赖弱于理想模型，即费米能级钉扎。图中的真空能级对齐只能作为概念起点，不能直接预测真实工艺的势垒。

5. 与其他器件的关系

  • Schottky 二极管是多数载流子器件，通常没有 PN 二极管那样的少子存储，因而开关快；
  • MESFET 用 Schottky 栅控制耗尽层；
  • MOSFET 源漏接触与二维材料 FET 的接触势垒会限制注入；
  • 光电探测中，金属–半导体–金属结构利用背靠背势垒收集光生载流子。

注入区间与接触比电阻

理想 n 型 Schottky 接触的热发射电流为

\[J=A^{**}T^2e^{-q\Phi_{Bn}/k_BT}(e^{qV/nk_BT}-1).\]

重掺杂会缩窄耗尽势垒，使输运从热场发射转向场发射；关键是隧穿能 \(E_{00}\) 与 \(k_BT\) 的比值。即使势垒高度不变，接触电阻也可因势垒变薄而下降。

二端电阻满足 \(R=2R_c+R_{sh}L/W\)。传输长度法用 \(L_T=\sqrt{\rho_c/R_{sh}}\) 分离片电阻与接触电阻，但前提是电流扩展和几何假设成立。若隧穿、不均匀低势垒斑块或栅压依赖有效面积占主导，用 Arrhenius 图提取势垒会失真。
