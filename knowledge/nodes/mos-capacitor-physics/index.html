The MOS Capacitor: Accumulation, Depletion, and InversionMOS电容：积累、耗尽与反型

Accumulation, depletion, inversion, threshold, and C–V response from gate-voltage partitioning, surface potential, and semiconductor space charge.用栅压分配、表面势和半导体空间电荷解释MOS结构的积累、耗尽、反型、阈值与C-V响应。

Core idea: The MOS capacitor is the electrostatic core of the field-effect transistor. Gate voltage divides self-consistently among oxide drop, semiconductor surface potential, work-function offset, and fixed/interface charge.

Figure 1 | Positive gate bias first depletes holes and then forms an electron inversion layer. Brews ohare / Fred the Oyster, CC BY-SA 3.0; source.

Voltage Partition

For a p-type substrate,

\[V_G=V_{FB}+\psi_s-\frac{Q_s}{C_{ox}},\qquad C_{ox}=\frac{\varepsilon_{ox}}{t_{ox}}.\]

\(V_{FB}\) contains work-function difference and fixed/interface charge, \(\psi_s\) is surface potential, and \(Q_s\) is semiconductor sheet charge. Signs must remain consistent with the chosen potential convention.

Three Surface Regimes

  • Accumulation: negative gate bias attracts majority holes.
  • Depletion: modest positive bias repels holes and exposes ionized acceptors.
  • Inversion: stronger positive bias raises the electron concentration above the hole concentration at the surface.

Under depletion approximation,

\[|Q_B|=\sqrt{2q\varepsilon_sN_A\psi_s},\qquad W_d=\sqrt{\frac{2\varepsilon_s\psi_s}{qN_A}}.\]

Strong inversion is conventionally defined by \(\psi_s=2\phi_F\), giving

\[V_T=V_{FB}+2\phi_F+ \frac{\sqrt{4q\varepsilon_sN_A\phi_F}}{C_{ox}}.\]

Why C–V Depends on Frequency

Accumulation capacitance approaches \(C_{ox}\). In depletion, oxide and depletion capacitances are in series:

\[\frac1C=\frac1{C_{ox}}+\frac1{C_d}.\]

At low frequency, minority carriers follow the signal and inversion capacitance rises. At high frequency they cannot respond fast enough, so capacitance remains near its minimum. Deep depletion occurs when the voltage sweep outruns minority-carrier generation.

Nonideality as a Measurement Tool

Flat-band shifts reveal fixed charge; C–V stretch-out and frequency dispersion reveal interface states; hysteresis may indicate mobile ions, trapping, or ferroelectric polarization; gate current diagnoses direct, Fowler–Nordheim, or defect-assisted tunneling. Effects harmful to logic can become the state variable of nonvolatile memory.

From Capacitor to Transistor

The MOS capacitor solves the vertical map gate voltage → surface potential → inversion charge. The MOSFET adds a lateral source–drain potential that transports this charge. See also From Capacitors to FETs.

Surface-potential closure

The gate equation is

\[V_G=V_{FB}+\psi_s-\frac{Q_s(\psi_s)}{C_{ox}},\]

so MOS electrostatics is solved by finding \(\psi_s\) consistent with semiconductor charge. In depletion \(|Q_d|\approx\sqrt{2q\epsilon_sN_A\psi_s}\); strong inversion begins near \(\psi_s=2\phi_F\), giving

\[V_T=V_{FB}+2\phi_F+\frac{\sqrt{4q\epsilon_sN_A\phi_F}}{C_{ox}}.\]

Low-frequency C–V lets inversion charge follow the signal, while high-frequency C–V leaves minority carriers unable to respond. Interface traps stretch the curve and add frequency-dependent capacitance; oxide charge shifts it. Extracting doping or trap density therefore requires known area, series resistance, frequency and sweep history.

核心结论：MOS 电容是场效应晶体管的静电核心。栅压不全部落在氧化层，也不全部落在半导体；它在氧化层电压降、表面势和功函数/固定电荷偏移之间自洽分配。

1. 电压分配

对 p 型衬底，通用栅压关系写成

\[V_G=V_{FB}+\psi_s-\frac{Q_s}{C_{ox}}, \qquad C_{ox}=\frac{\varepsilon_{ox}}{t_{ox}}.\]

\(V_{FB}\) 汇总金属–半导体功函数差、氧化层固定电荷和界面电荷；\(\psi_s\) 是半导体表面相对体内的势；\(Q_s\) 是单位面积半导体电荷。符号必须与电势和电荷约定配套，不能脱离定义记公式。

2. 三种表面状态

以 p 型衬底为例：

  • 积累：负栅压吸引空穴，表面多数载流子增多；
  • 耗尽：小正栅压排斥空穴，留下离化受主；
  • 反型：更大正栅压使电子浓度超过空穴，表面形成 n 型导电层。

耗尽近似下

\[|Q_B|=\sqrt{2q\varepsilon_sN_A\psi_s},\qquad W_d=\sqrt{\frac{2\varepsilon_s\psi_s}{qN_A}}.\]

强反型常以 \(\psi_s=2\phi_F\) 定义，其中 \(\phi_F=(kT/q)\ln(N_A/n_i)\)。理想长沟道 nMOS 的阈值电压近似

\[V_T=V_{FB}+2\phi_F+ \frac{\sqrt{4q\varepsilon_sN_A\phi_F}}{C_{ox}}.\]

3. C–V 为何依赖频率

积累时小信号电容接近 \(C_{ox}\)；耗尽时氧化层电容与耗尽层电容串联：

\[\frac{1}{C}=\frac{1}{C_{ox}}+\frac{1}{C_d}.\]

低频下少数载流子来得及产生并跟随交流信号，强反型电容回升；高频下反型层小信号电荷不能及时响应，电容保持在较低值。深耗尽说明扫压快于少子产生，而不是新的平衡态。

4. 非理想性是一种测量资源

  • 平带电压平移可估计固定氧化层电荷；
  • C–V 拉伸和频率色散反映界面态；
  • 滞回可能来自可移动离子、陷阱充放电或铁电极化；
  • 栅漏电提示直接隧穿、Fowler–Nordheim 隧穿或缺陷辅助导电。

这些效应在逻辑 MOSFET 中往往有害，却是非易失存储器的器件物理用来保存信息的基础。

5. 从电容到晶体管

MOS 电容只解决垂直方向的“栅压 → 表面势 → 反型电荷”。MOSFET：栅控电荷与沟道输运再加入横向源漏电势，使反型电荷沿沟道运动。更完整推导见From Capacitors to FETs。

表面势闭合关系

栅方程为

\[V_G=V_{FB}+\psi_s-\frac{Q_s(\psi_s)}{C_{ox}},\]

因此 MOS 静电问题就是寻找与半导体电荷自洽的 \(\psi_s\)。耗尽区 \(|Q_d|\approx\sqrt{2q\epsilon_sN_A\psi_s}\)，强反型约从 \(\psi_s=2\phi_F\) 开始，对应

\[V_T=V_{FB}+2\phi_F+\frac{\sqrt{4q\epsilon_sN_A\phi_F}}{C_{ox}}.\]

低频 C–V 中反型电荷可跟随信号，高频 C–V 中少数载流子来不及响应。界面陷阱会拉伸曲线并引入频率相关电容，氧化层电荷则整体平移曲线。因此提取掺杂或陷阱密度必须明确面积、串联电阻、频率与扫描历史。
