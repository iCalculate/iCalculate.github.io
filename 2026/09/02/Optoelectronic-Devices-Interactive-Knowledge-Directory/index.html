Optoelectronic Devices: An Interactive Knowledge Directory

Optoelectronics is best understood as a conversion network: electronic states and photons exchange energy, optical structures control modes, and contacts expose those microscopic processes as measurable signals. The directory keeps traditional devices and current quantum frontiers on the same causal map.
