Energy and Chemistry: An Interactive Knowledge Directory

Energy chemistry is a hierarchy of conserved flows rather than a list of materials. Thermodynamics defines attainable conversion, kinetics selects pathways, transport supplies reactants, interfaces couple phases, and engineering decides whether laboratory selectivity survives scale-up.
