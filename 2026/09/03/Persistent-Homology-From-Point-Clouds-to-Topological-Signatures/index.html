Persistent homology: watching shape emerge from a point cloud

Data usually reaches us as coordinates, not as a clean geometric object. A scanner returns points on a surface; a simulation returns states in a high-dimensional space; an experiment returns samples whose shape is hidden by noise. Persistent homology asks which topological features survive when we change the scale at which the points are connected.

The key move is simple: place a ball of radius r around every point and let all balls grow together. What matters is not one arbitrarily chosen radius, but the entire movie.

1. A shape is not attached to a single scale

At very small $r$, every sample is isolated, so $\beta_0$ — the number of connected components — is close to the number of points. As the balls touch, components merge. At an intermediate radius the points around the ring form one component while leaving a central hole, so $\beta_0=1$ and $\beta_1=1$. At a large radius the hole is covered and $\beta_1$ returns to zero.

Birth

A feature first appears at a critical radius. Components exist at the beginning; loops are born when a cycle closes.

Persistence

A feature that survives a wide interval of radii is less likely to be a small geometric accident.

Death

A component dies when it merges into an older one; a one-dimensional loop dies when triangles fill it.

2. From balls to a computable simplicial complex

The union of balls is intuitive but awkward to store. We replace it with a simplicial complex. In the Vietoris–Rips construction, two vertices receive an edge when their distance is at most $2r$; three mutually connected vertices form a filled triangle, and the rule continues in higher dimensions:

$$ \mathrm{VR}(X,r)=\left\{\sigma\subseteq X:\ d(x_i,x_j)\le 2r\ \text{for every}\ x_i,x_j\in\sigma\right\}. $$

Increasing $r$ can only add simplices, so the complexes are nested:

$$
r_1\le r_2 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\mathrm{VR}(X,r_1)\subseteq\mathrm{VR}(X,r_2).
$$

This nested sequence is a filtration. Homology turns every frame into algebra, while persistence tracks how homology classes map from one frame to the next.

3. Reading the barcode and the curves

Each horizontal barcode segment is a lifetime $[r_{\text{birth}},r_{\text{death}})$. The many short cyan $H_0$ bars show components quickly merging. One cyan bar remains forever because the final object is still one connected component. The coral $H_1$ bar records the ring-shaped hole.

The Betti curves are a different summary of the same information:

$$
\beta_k(r)=\operatorname{count}{\text{bars in }H_k\text{ crossing }r}.
$$

Barcodes retain each lifetime; Betti curves retain only the number alive at each radius. A persistence diagram gives the same lifetimes as points $(r_{\text{birth}},r_{\text{death}})$: distance from the diagonal becomes persistence.

Topological signal is not simply “the longest bar.” Sampling density, metric, coefficient field, filtration and domain knowledge determine what counts as meaningful. Persistence provides a stable multiscale descriptor, not an automatic interpretation.

4. What is actually computed?

For a simplicial complex with boundary maps $\partial_k:C_k\to C_{k-1}$, the $k$-th homology group is

$$
H_k=\ker \partial_k/\operatorname{im}\partial_{k+1},
\qquad
\beta_k=\dim H_k.
$$

This page constructs edges and triangles at the selected radius and performs Gaussian elimination over $\mathbb F_2$. Consequently,

$$
\beta_0=n-\operatorname{rank}\partial_1,
\qquad
\beta_1=m-\operatorname{rank}\partial_1-\operatorname{rank}\partial_2,
$$

where $n$ and $m$ are the numbers of vertices and edges. Working over $\mathbb F_2$ makes orientation signs disappear; for applications sensitive to torsion, the coefficient choice matters.

5. A practical workflow

  1. Choose a meaningful representation and metric. Topology is only as relevant as the distances supplied to it.
  2. Select a filtration: Vietoris–Rips for general point clouds, Čech for an exact nerve of balls, alpha complexes for efficient Euclidean geometry, or sublevel filtrations for scalar fields and images.
  3. Compute persistence in the dimensions justified by the problem.
  4. Compare diagrams with bottleneck or Wasserstein distance, vectorize them for statistics, or test stability against resampling.
  5. Return to the domain: a long $H_1$ bar may represent a physical cycle, a periodic coordinate, a sampling boundary, or a preprocessing artefact.

Closing perspective

Clustering asks which points belong together at a chosen resolution. Persistent homology asks a broader question: which connected components, loops and voids remain coherent across resolutions? Its power comes from refusing to choose $r$ too early. The answer is not a single shape, but a history of shapes.

持久同调：看形状如何从点云中浮现

数据到达我们手里时，通常只是坐标，而不是一个干净的几何对象：扫描仪给出表面上的点，模拟给出高维状态空间中的采样，实验给出被噪声遮住形状的观测。持久同调关心的是：当我们改变连接数据点的尺度时，哪些拓扑特征仍然存在？

核心动作很简单：以每个数据点为圆心，画一个半径为 r 的圆盘，再让所有圆盘同时长大。真正重要的不是任意挑中的某一个半径，而是整段“生长电影”。

1. 形状并不依附于唯一尺度

当 $r$ 很小时，每个样本都彼此孤立，所以表示连通分量数量的 $\beta_0$ 接近数据点总数。圆盘逐渐相碰后，分量开始合并。在中等半径下，环形排列的点已经连成一个整体，但中央仍留下孔洞，此时 $\beta_0=1,\ \beta_1=1$。当半径足够大，中央被覆盖，$\beta_1$ 又回到零。

出生 Birth

特征在某个临界半径第一次出现。连通分量从起点就存在；边首尾闭合时，一维环出生。

持续 Persistence

如果一个结构能跨越很宽的半径区间，它通常不太可能只是局部几何抖动造成的偶然现象。

死亡 Death

一个分量并入更早出生的分量时死亡；组成孔洞的环被三角形填满时，一维同调类死亡。

2. 从圆盘并集到可计算的单纯复形

圆盘并集很直观，却不适合直接存储。我们用单纯复形把它离散化。在 Vietoris–Rips 构造中，两个顶点距离不超过 $2r$ 时连边；三个顶点两两相连时填入三角形；更高维依此类推：

$$ \mathrm{VR}(X,r)=\left\{\sigma\subseteq X:\ d(x_i,x_j)\le 2r\ \text{对任意}\ x_i,x_j\in\sigma\right\}. $$

增大 $r$ 只会增加单形，不会删除已有单形，因此得到一串彼此嵌套的复形：

$$
r_1\le r_2 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\mathrm{VR}(X,r_1)\subseteq\mathrm{VR}(X,r_2).
$$

这串嵌套结构叫作过滤（filtration）。同调把每一帧转化为代数对象，持久性则追踪同调类如何从一帧映射到下一帧。

3. 如何读条形码和数据曲线？

条形码中的每条横线都是一个寿命区间 $[r_{\text{birth}},r_{\text{death}})$。许多短青色 $H_0$ 条带表示连通分量很快互相合并；最终保留一条，是因为整体始终还有一个连通分量。珊瑚色 $H_1$ 长条则记录环形孔洞。

Betti 曲线是对同一份信息的另一种压缩：

$$
\beta_k(r)=\operatorname{count}{\text{在半径 }r\text{ 处仍存活的 }H_k\text{ 条带}}.
$$

条形码保留每个特征的寿命；Betti 曲线只保留每个半径处还活着多少个特征。持久图则把同样的寿命画成点 $(r_{\text{birth}},r_{\text{death}})$，点到对角线的距离就是持久性。

“拓扑信号”并不简单等于“最长的条”。采样密度、距离度量、系数域、过滤方式和领域知识都会改变解释。持久性给出稳定的多尺度描述符，却不会自动替我们解释数据。
4. 页面实际计算了什么？

对于边界映射为 $\partial_k:C_k\to C_{k-1}$ 的单纯复形，第 $k$ 阶同调群为

$$
H_k=\ker \partial_k/\operatorname{im}\partial_{k+1},
\qquad
\beta_k=\dim H_k.
$$

上面的交互图会在当前半径下真实构造边和三角形，并在 $\mathbb F_2$ 上对边界矩阵做高斯消元。因此

$$
\beta_0=n-\operatorname{rank}\partial_1,
\qquad
\beta_1=m-\operatorname{rank}\partial_1-\operatorname{rank}\partial_2,
$$

其中 $n,m$ 分别是顶点数与边数。在 $\mathbb F_2$ 上计算可省略定向的正负号；若问题涉及扭结（torsion），系数选择就会很重要。

5. 一个可靠的分析流程
  1. 选择有意义的表征和度量。 输入距离没有科学意义，得到的拓扑也不会自动获得意义。
  2. 选择过滤。 一般点云常用 Vietoris–Rips；Čech 复形严格对应圆盘并集的神经；欧氏空间可用更高效的 alpha 复形；图像和标量场常用下水平集过滤。
  3. 计算问题真正需要的维度。 $H_0$ 看簇，$H_1$ 看环，$H_2$ 看空腔；维度越高，代价增长越快。
  4. 检验稳定性。 用 bottleneck 或 Wasserstein 距离比较持久图，向量化后做统计，或通过重采样检查条带是否稳定。
  5. 回到领域解释。 一条很长的 $H_1$ 可能是物理周期、周期坐标、采样边界，也可能只是预处理伪影。
结语：不要过早选定 $r$

聚类往往在一个选定分辨率下追问“哪些点属于同一组”；持久同调问得更宽：哪些连通分量、环和空腔能跨越多个分辨率保持一致？ 它最有力量的地方，是拒绝过早选定某个 $r$。答案不是一个形状，而是一段形状的历史。
